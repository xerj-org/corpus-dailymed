DailyMed 71f56227-d87a-4bc0-a879-c1993c6ccba6 | Dexmethylphenidate HydrochlorideExtended-Release | 2.3 Administration Instructions

Dexmethylphenidate hydrochloride extended-release capsules is administered orally and may be taken whole or the capsule may be opened and the entire contents sprinkled onto applesauce. If the patient is using the sprinkled administration method, the sprinkled applesauce should be consumed immediately; it should not be stored. Patients should take the applesauce with sprinkled beads in its entirety without chewing. The dose of a single capsule should not be divided. The contents of the entire capsule should be taken, and patients should not take anything less than one capsule per day.
