DailyMed 717eb542-3a05-485e-be76-3930efb40d33 | Levocetirizine Dihydrochloride | 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

In vitrodata indicate that levocetirizine is unlikely to produce pharmacokinetic interactions through inhibition or induction of liver drug-metabolizing enzymes. No

  in vivodrug-drug interaction studies have been performed with levocetirizine. Drug interaction studies have been performed with racemic cetirizine.
