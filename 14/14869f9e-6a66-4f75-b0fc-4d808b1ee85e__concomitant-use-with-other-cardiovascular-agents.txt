DailyMed 14869f9e-6a66-4f75-b0fc-4d808b1ee85e | Diltiazem Hydrochloride | Concomitant Use with Other Cardiovascular Agents

Sublingual NTG may be taken as required to abort acute anginal attacks during diltiazem hydrochloride tablet therapy.

                              Prophylactic Nitrate Therapy. Diltiazem hydrochloride tablets may be safely coadministered with short- and long-acting nitrates, but there have been no controlled studies to evaluate the antianginal effectiveness of this combination.
