DailyMed 149d20de-f262-4567-aa97-d4d28a1653cb | topiramate | 5.13 Anaphylaxis and Angioedema

Hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis and angioedema, have occurred in patients treated with topiramate in the postmarketing setting. If a hypersensitivity reaction occurs, discontinuetopiramate and initiate appropriate therapy

   [see Contraindications (

    4)].
