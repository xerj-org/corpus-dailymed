DailyMed 149d20de-f262-4567-aa97-d4d28a1653cb | topiramate | 5.8 Withdrawal of Antiepileptic Drugs

In patients with or without a history of seizures or epilepsy, antiepileptic drugs, including topiramate, should be gradually withdrawn to minimize the potential for seizures or increased seizure frequency

  [see Clinical Studies (

   14)]

  . In situations where rapid withdrawal of topiramate is medically required, appropriate monitoring is recommended.
