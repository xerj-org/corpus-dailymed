DailyMed 56f27540-9b2a-4266-9e15-a5b2d738c7a1 | AIPATCH NUMBING PAIN RELIEF | Warnings

for external use only

                           When using this product

  • use only as directed. Read and follow all directions and warnings on this carton

  • do not bandage tightly

  • avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes

  • do not use at the same time as other topical analgesics

  • do not remove when wet or directly after shower, allow to dry and then slowly remove

                           Stop use and ask a doctor if

  • condition worsens

  • symptoms persist for more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days

  • you experience signs of skin injury, such as pain, swelling or blistering where the patch was applied

                        • If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health professional before use
