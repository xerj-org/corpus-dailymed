DailyMed dea035a6-dcf1-4b7d-8adc-a2f678b71fd9 | Celecoxib | 14.5 Analgesia, including Primary Dysmenorrhea

In acute analgesic models of post-oral surgery pain, post-orthopedic surgical pain, and primary dysmenorrhea, celecoxib relieved pain that was rated by patients as moderate to severe. Single doses [see Dosage and Administration (2.6)
                           ] of celecoxib provided pain relief within 60 minutes.
