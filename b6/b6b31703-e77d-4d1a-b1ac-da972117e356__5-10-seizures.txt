Mirtazapine tablets has not been systematically evaluated in patients with seizure disorders. In premarketing clinical trials, 1 seizure was reported among the 2796 U.S. and non-U.S. patients treated with mirtazapine tablets. Mirtazapine tablets should be prescribed with caution in patients with a seizure disorder.
