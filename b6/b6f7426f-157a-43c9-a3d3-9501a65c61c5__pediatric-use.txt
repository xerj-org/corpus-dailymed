DailyMed b6f7426f-157a-43c9-a3d3-9501a65c61c5 | DIPHENHYDRAMINE | Pediatric Use

Diphenhydramine hydrochloride should not be used in neonates and premature infants (see

                                 CONTRAINDICATIONS

                           ).

  Diphenhydramine hydrochloride injection may diminish mental alertness, or, in the young pediatric patient, cause excitation. Overdosage may cause hallucinations, convulsions, or death (see

                                 WARNINGS

                           and

                                 OVERDOSAGE

                           ).

  See also

                                 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

                           section.
