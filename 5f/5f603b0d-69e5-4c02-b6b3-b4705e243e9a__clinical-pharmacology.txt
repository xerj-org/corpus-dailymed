DailyMed 5f603b0d-69e5-4c02-b6b3-b4705e243e9a | Butalbital, Acetaminophen and Caffeine | CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY

This combination drug product is intended as a treatment for tension headache. It consists of a fixed combination of butalbital, acetaminophen and caffeine. The role each component plays in the relief of the complex of symptoms known as tension headache is incompletely understood.
