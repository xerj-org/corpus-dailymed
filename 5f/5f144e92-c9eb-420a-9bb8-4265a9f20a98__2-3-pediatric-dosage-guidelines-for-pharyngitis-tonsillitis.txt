DailyMed 5f144e92-c9eb-420a-9bb8-4265a9f20a98 | Azithromycin | 2.3 Pediatric Dosage Guidelines for Pharyngitis/Tonsillitis

The recommended dose of azithromycin for pediatric patients 2 years of age and older with pharyngitis/tonsillitis is 12 mg/kg once daily for 5 days
