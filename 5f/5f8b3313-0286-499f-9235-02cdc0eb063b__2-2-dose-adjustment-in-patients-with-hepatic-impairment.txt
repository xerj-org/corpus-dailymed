DailyMed 5f8b3313-0286-499f-9235-02cdc0eb063b | Bupropion Hydrochloride SR | 2.2 Dose Adjustment in Patients with Hepatic Impairment

In patients with moderate to severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh score: 7 to 15), the maximum dose of bupropion hydrochloride extended-release tablets (SR) is 100 mg/day or 150 mg every other day. In patients with mild hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh score: 5 to 6), consider reducing the dose and/or frequency of dosing
  [see Use in Specific Populations (
   8.7), Clinical Pharmacology (
   12.3)].
