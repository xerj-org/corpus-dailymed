DailyMed 5f603b0d-69e5-4c02-b6b3-b4705e243e9a | Butalbital, Acetaminophen and Caffeine | General

Butalbital, acetaminophen and caffeine oral solution should be prescribed with caution in certain special-risk patients, such as the elderly or debilitated, and those with severe impairment of renal or hepatic function, or acute abdominal conditions.
