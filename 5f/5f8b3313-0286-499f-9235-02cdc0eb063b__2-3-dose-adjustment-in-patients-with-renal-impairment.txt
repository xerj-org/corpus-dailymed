DailyMed 5f8b3313-0286-499f-9235-02cdc0eb063b | Bupropion Hydrochloride SR | 2.3 Dose Adjustment in Patients with Renal Impairment

Consider reducing the dose and/or frequency of bupropion hydrochloride extended-release tablets (SR) in patients with renal impairment (Glomerular Filtration Rate less than 90 mL/min)
