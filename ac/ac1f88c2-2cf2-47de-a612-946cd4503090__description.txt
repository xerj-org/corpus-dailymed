Lorazepam, USP, an antianxiety agent, has the chemical formula, 7-chloro-5-(

  o-chlorophenyl)-1,3-dihydro-3-hydroxy-2

  H-1,4-benzodiazepin-2-one:

                  It is a nearly white powder almost insoluble in water. Each lorazepam tablet, to be taken orally, contains 0.5 mg, 1 mg, or 2 mg of lorazepam, USP. The inactive ingredients present are lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, and polacrilin potassium.
