How Supplied

                  Dextroamphetamine saccharate, amphetamine aspartate monohydrate, dextroamphetamine sulfate, amphetamine sulfate extended-release capsules are available as:

                     12.5 mg: green cap and green body imprinted with “950” on cap and “12.5 mg” on body in black ink containing light orange to orange colored pellets, bottles of 100 CRC, NDC 57664-950-88

                     25 mg: green opaque cap and ivory body imprinted with “951” on cap and “25 mg” on body in black ink containing light orange to orange colored pellets, bottles of 100 CRC, NDC 57664-951-88

                     37.5 mg: light caramel cap and ivory body imprinted with “952” on cap and “37.5 mg” on body in black ink containing light orange to orange colored pellets, bottles of 100 CRC, NDC 57664-952-88

                     50 mg: purple cap and ivory body imprinted with “953” on cap and “50 mg” on body in black ink containing light orange to orange colored pellets, bottles of 100 CRC, NDC 57664-953-88

                     Storage and Handling

                  Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.

                  Store at room temperature, 20ºC to 25ºC (68ºF to 77ºF). Excursions permitted between 15ºC to 30ºC (59ºF to 86ºF)

  [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
