DailyMed 30e515c3-a18f-499a-87c4-be8d64cfdbc6 | Dextroamphetamine saccharate, amphetamine aspartate monohydrate, dextroamphetamine sulfate, amphetamine sulfate | 13.2 Animal Toxicology and/or Pharmacology

Acute administration of high doses of amphetamine (
  d- or
  d, l-) has been shown to produce long-lasting neurotoxic effects, including irreversible nerve fiber damage in rodents. The significance of these findings to humans is unknown.
