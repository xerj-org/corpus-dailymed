DailyMed 30e515c3-a18f-499a-87c4-be8d64cfdbc6 | Dextroamphetamine saccharate, amphetamine aspartate monohydrate, dextroamphetamine sulfate, amphetamine sulfate | 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Dextroamphetamine saccharate, amphetamine aspartate monohydrate, dextroamphetamine sulfate, amphetamine sulfate extended-release capsules are indicated for the treatment of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) in patients 13 years and older

                        [see Clinical Studies (14)].

                     Limitations of Use:

                  Pediatric patients 12 years and younger experienced higher plasma exposure than patients 13 years and older at the same dose, and experienced higher rates of adverse reactions, mainly insomnia and decreased appetite

                        [see Use in Specific Populations (8.4)].
