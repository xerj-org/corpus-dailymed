For patients switching from another medication or any other amphetamine products, discontinue that treatment, and titrate with dextroamphetamine saccharate, amphetamine aspartate monohydrate, dextroamphetamine sulfate, amphetamine sulfate extended-release capsules using the titration schedule
  [
   see Dosage and Administration (2.4)]
  .

                        Do not substitute for other amphetamine products on a milligram-per-milligram basis because of different amphetamine base compositions and differing pharmacokinetic profiles
  [
   see Warnings and Precautions (5.9),
   Description (11),

                              Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)

                           ].
