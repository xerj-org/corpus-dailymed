DailyMed 30e515c3-a18f-499a-87c4-be8d64cfdbc6 | Dextroamphetamine saccharate, amphetamine aspartate monohydrate, dextroamphetamine sulfate, amphetamine sulfate | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Dextroamphetamine saccharate, amphetamine aspartate monohydrate, dextroamphetamine sulfate, amphetamine sulfate extended-release capsules are contraindicated in patients with:

                     Known hypersensitivity to amphetamine, or other components of dextroamphetamine saccharate, amphetamine aspartate monohydrate, dextroamphetamine sulfate, amphetamine sulfate extended-release capsules. Hypersensitivity reactions such as angioedema and anaphylactic reactions have been reported in patients treated with other amphetamine products
   [

                           see Adverse Reactions (6.2)

                        ].

                     Concomitant treatment with monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs), and also within 14 days of stopping MAOIs

	(including MAOIs such as linezolid or intravenous methylene blue), because of an increased risk of hypertensive crisis
   [

                           see Drug Interactions (7.1)

                        ].
