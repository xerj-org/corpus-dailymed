DailyMed 30e515c3-a18f-499a-87c4-be8d64cfdbc6 | Dextroamphetamine saccharate, amphetamine aspartate monohydrate, dextroamphetamine sulfate, amphetamine sulfate | 8.6 Renal Impairment

Due to reduced clearance of amphetamine in patients with severe renal insufficiency (GFR 15 to < 30 mL/min/1.73 m
  2), the maximum dose in adults should be reduced. Pediatric patients ages 13 to 17 years with severe renal insufficiency can be given the recommended starting dose if tolerated, but the dose should not be escalated. Dextroamphetamine saccharate, amphetamine aspartate monohydrate, dextroamphetamine sulfate, amphetamine sulfate extended-release capsules are not recommended in patients with ESRD (GFR < 15 mL/min/1.73 m
  2)
