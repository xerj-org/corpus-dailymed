DailyMed 886aa8d8-b293-258f-b16d-be40418787a8 | Icosapent Ethyl | 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Icosapent ethyl capsules are indicated:

                     as an adjunct to diet to reduce triglyceride (TG) levels in adult patients with severe (≥500 mg/dL) hypertriglyceridemia.

                     Limitations of Use:

                  The effect of icosapent ethyl capsules on the risk for pancreatitis in patients with severe hypertriglyceridemia has not been determined.
