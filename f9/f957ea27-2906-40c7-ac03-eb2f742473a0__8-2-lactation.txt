DailyMed f957ea27-2906-40c7-ac03-eb2f742473a0 | Cephalexin | 8.2 Lactation

Risk Summary

                        Data from a published clinical lactation study reports that cephalexin is present in human milk. The relative infant dose (RID) is considered to be <1% of the maternal weight adjusted dose. There are limited data on the effects of cephalexin on the breastfed child. There are no data on the effects of cephalexin on milk production.

                        The developmental health benefits of breastfeeding should be considered along with the mother’s clinical need for cephalexin and any potential adverse effects on the breastfed child from cephalexin or from the underlying maternal condition.
