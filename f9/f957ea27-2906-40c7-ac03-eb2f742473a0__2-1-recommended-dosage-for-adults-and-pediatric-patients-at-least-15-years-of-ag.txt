DailyMed f957ea27-2906-40c7-ac03-eb2f742473a0 | Cephalexin | 2.1 Recommended Dosage for Adults and Pediatric Patients at Least 15 Years of Age

The recommended dosage of oral cephalexin capsules is 250 mg every 6 hours, but a dose of 500 mg every 12 hours may be administered. Treatment is administered for 7 to 14 days.

                           For more severe infections larger doses of oral cephalexin capsules may be needed, up to 4 grams daily in two to four equally divided doses.
