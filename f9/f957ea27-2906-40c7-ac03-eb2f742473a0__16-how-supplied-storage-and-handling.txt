Cephalexin capsules, USP are available in:

                        750 mg Capsule

 Dark green opaque/dark green opaque size ‘00EL’ hard gelatin capsule filled with off white granular powder and imprinted with ‘CE’ on dark green opaque cap and ‘750’ on dark green opaque body with black ink.

 Bottles of 20                                                               NDC 59651-728-20

 Bottles of 50                                                               NDC 59651-728-50

 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature]. 

                  Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container.
