                     Dapagliflozin and Metformin HCl Extended-Release Tablets

                  Dapagliflozin and metformin HCl extended-release tablets are available for oral administration as tablets containing 5 mg dapagliflozin and 500 mg metformin HCl which is equivalent to 389.9 mg metformin base (dapagliflozin and metformin HCl extended-release tablets 5 mg/500 mg), 5 mg dapagliflozin and 1,000 mg metformin HCl which is equivalent to 779.86 mg metformin base (dapagliflozin and metformin HCl extended-release tablets 5 mg/1,000 mg), 10 mg dapagliflozin and 500 mg metformin HCl which is equivalent to 389.9 mg metformin base (dapagliflozin and metformin HCl extended-release tablets 10 mg/500 mg), 10 mg dapagliflozin and 1,000 mg metformin HCl which is equivalent to 779.86 mg metformin base (dapagliflozin and metformin HCl extended-release tablets 10 mg/1,000 mg).

                  Each film-coated tablet of dapagliflozin and metformin HCl extended-release tablets contains the following inactive ingredients: colloidal silicon dioxide, crospovidone, ferric oxide yellow, glyceryl dibehenate, hypromellose, lactose anhydrous, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, polyethylene glycol, polysorbate 80, povidone k30, talc.

                  The film coatings contain the following inactive ingredients: polyvinyl alcohol, titanium dioxide, polyethylene glycol, and talc. Additionally, the film coating for the dapagliflozin and metformin HCl extended-release tablets 5 mg/500 mg tablets contains FD&C Yellow No. 6; the 5 mg/1000 mg and 10 mg/500 mg tablets contains iron oxide red and ferrosoferric oxide; and the 10 mg/1000 mg tablets contains iron oxide yellow and ferrosoferric oxide.
