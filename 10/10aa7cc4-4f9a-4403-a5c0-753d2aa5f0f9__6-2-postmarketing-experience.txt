DailyMed 10aa7cc4-4f9a-4403-a5c0-753d2aa5f0f9 | Dapagliflozin and Metformin Hydrochloride | 6.2 Postmarketing Experience

Additional adverse reactions have been identified during post-approval use of dapagliflozin or metformin HCl. Because these reactions are reported voluntarily from a population of uncertain size, it is generally not possible to reliably estimate their frequency or establish a causal relationship to drug exposure.

                           Dapagliflozin

                            

                           Infections: Necrotizing fasciitis of the perineum (Fournier’s Gangrene), urosepsis and pyelonephritis

                           Metabolism and Nutrition Disorders: Ketoacidosis

                           Renal and Urinary Disorders: Acute kidney injury

                           Skin and Subcutaneous Tissue Disorders: Rash

                           Metformin HCl

                            

                           Hepatobiliary Disorders:Cholestatic, hepatocellular, and mixed hepatocellular liver injury
