DailyMed 10aa7cc4-4f9a-4403-a5c0-753d2aa5f0f9 | Dapagliflozin and Metformin Hydrochloride | 5.5 Hypoglycemia with Concomitant Use with Insulin or Insulin Secretagogues

Insulin and insulin secretagogues (e.g., sulfonylureas) are known to cause hypoglycemia. Dapagliflozin and metformin HCl may increase the risk of hypoglycemia when combined with insulin and/or an insulin secretagogue

  [see

   Adverse Reactions (6.1)]

  . The risk of hypoglycemia may be lowered by a reduction in the dose of sulfonylurea (or other concomitantly administered insulin secretagogues) or insulin. Inform patients using these concomitant medications of the risk of hypoglycemia and educate them on the signs and symptoms of hypoglycemia.
