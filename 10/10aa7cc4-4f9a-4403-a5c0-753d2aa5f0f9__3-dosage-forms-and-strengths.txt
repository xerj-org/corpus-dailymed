Dapagliflozin and metformin HCl extended-release tablets are available as follows:

                     Dapagliflozin and metformin HCl extended-release tablets
                     5/1000 mg:Pink to dark pink colored oval shaped, beveled edge, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “DM3” on one face and plain on other face.

                      

                     Dapagliflozin and metformin HCl extended-release tablets
                     10/500 mg:Pink colored oval shaped, beveled edge, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “DM2” on one face and plain on other face.

                      

                     Dapagliflozin and metformin HCl extended-release tablets
                     10/1000 mg:Yellow to dark yellow colored oval shaped, beveled edge, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “DM4” on one face and plain on other face.
