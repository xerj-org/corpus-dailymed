DailyMed 10aa7cc4-4f9a-4403-a5c0-753d2aa5f0f9 | Dapagliflozin and Metformin Hydrochloride | 8.7 Hepatic Impairment

Use of metformin in patients with hepatic impairment has been associated with some cases of lactic acidosis. Dapagliflozin and metformin HCl is not recommended in patients with hepatic impairment
