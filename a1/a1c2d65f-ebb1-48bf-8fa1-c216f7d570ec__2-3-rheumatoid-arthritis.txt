DailyMed a1c2d65f-ebb1-48bf-8fa1-c216f7d570ec | Zybic | 2.3 Rheumatoid Arthritis

For the relief of the signs and symptoms of rheumatoid arthritis, the recommended starting and maintenance oral dose of Zybic™ is 7.5 mg once daily. Some patients may receive additional benefit by increasing the dose to 15 mg once daily.
