DailyMed a1c2d65f-ebb1-48bf-8fa1-c216f7d570ec | Zybic | 5.9 Serious Skin Reactions

NSAIDs, including meloxicam, can cause serious skin adverse reactions such as exfoliative dermatitis, Stevens-Johnson Syndrome (SJS), and toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), which can be fatal. NSAIDs can also cause fixed drug eruption (FDE). FDE may present as a more severe variant known as generalized bullous fixed drug eruption (GBFDE), which can be life-threatening. These serious events may occur without warning. Inform patients about the signs and symptoms of serious skin reactions, and to discontinue the use of Zybic™ at the first appearance of skin rash or any other sign of hypersensitivity. Zybic™ is contraindicated in patients with previous serious skin reactions to NSAIDs [see Contraindications (4)].
