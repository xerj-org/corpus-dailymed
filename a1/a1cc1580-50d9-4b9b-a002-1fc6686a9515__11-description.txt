  D-arabinofuranosylguanine (ara-G).

                  Nelarabine is slightly soluble to soluble in water and melts with decomposition between 209°C and 217°C.

                  Nelarabine injection is supplied as a clear, colorless, sterile solution in glass single-dose vials. Each vial contains 250 mg of nelarabine (5 mg nelarabine per mL) and the inactive ingredient sodium chloride (4.5 mg per mL) in Water for Injection, USP. Nelarabine injection is intended for intravenous infusion.

                  Hydrochloric acid and sodium hydroxide may have been used to adjust the pH. The solution pH ranges from 5.0 to 7.0.
