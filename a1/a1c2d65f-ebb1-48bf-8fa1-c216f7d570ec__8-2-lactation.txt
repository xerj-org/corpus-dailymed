Risk Summary

                        There are no human data available on whether meloxicam is present in human milk, or on the effects on breastfed infants, or on milk production. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be considered along with the mother's clinical need for Zybic™ and any potential adverse effects on the breastfed infant from the Zybic™ or from the underlying maternal condition.

                           Data

                           Animal Data

                        Meloxicam was present in the milk of lactating rats at concentrations higher than those in plasma.
