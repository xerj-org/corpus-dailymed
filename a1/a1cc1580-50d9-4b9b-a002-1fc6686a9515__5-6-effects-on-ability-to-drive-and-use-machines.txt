DailyMed a1cc1580-50d9-4b9b-a002-1fc6686a9515 | Nelarabine | 5.6 Effects on Ability to Drive and Use Machines

Patients treated with nelarabine injection may experience somnolence during and for several days after treatment
  [see

                              Adverse Reactions (6.1)

                           ]. Advise patients to refrain from driving or engaging in hazardous occupations or activities until somnolence has resolved.
