DailyMed a1cc1580-50d9-4b9b-a002-1fc6686a9515 | Nelarabine | 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Nelarabine injection is supplied as a clear, colorless, sterile solution in Type I, clear glass single-dose vials with a gray bromobutyl rubber stopper (not made with natural rubber latex) and an aluminum seal with a white snap-off cap.

                  Single-dose Vials are available in 250 mg/50mL (5 mg/mL) strength and the following carton sizes:

NDC 39822-0650-1 (package of 1).

NDC 39822-0650-6 (package of 6).

                  Discard unused portion.

                  Store nelarabine injection between 20°C and 25°C (68°F and 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F). See USP Controlled Room Temperature.
