Consider the possibility of multiple drug ingestion. The pharmacokinetic profile of Dextroamphetamine sulfate should be considered when treating patients with overdose. D-amphetamine is not dialyzable. Consider contacting the Poison Help line (1-800-222-1222) or a medical toxicologist for additional overdose management recommendations.
