DailyMed 9e375c07-70db-458c-9336-22a7037b0a5f | montelukast sodium | 2.1 Asthma

For asthma, administer montelukast sodium orally once daily in the evening, with or without food. There have been no clinical trials in patients with asthma to evaluate the relative efficacy of morning versus evening dosing.
