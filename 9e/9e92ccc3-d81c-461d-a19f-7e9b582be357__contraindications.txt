DailyMed 9e92ccc3-d81c-461d-a19f-7e9b582be357 | dextroamphetamine sulfate | CONTRAINDICATIONS

In patients known to be hypersensitive to amphetamine, or other components of dextroamphetamine sulfate extended-release. Hypersensitivity reactions such as angioedema and anaphylactic reactions have been reported in patients treated with other amphetamine products [see Adverse Reactions].

                  Patients taking monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs), or within 14 days of stopping MAOIs (including MAOIs such as linezolid or intravenous methylene blue), because of an increased risk of hypertensive crisis [see Warnings 
                     and Drug Interactions].
