DailyMed 9e375c07-70db-458c-9336-22a7037b0a5f | montelukast sodium | 2.2 Exercise-Induced Bronchoconstriction (EIB)

For  prevention of EIB, administer a single dose of montelukast sodium orally at least 2  hours, before exercise.

                        An additional dose of montelukast sodium should not be taken within 24 hours of a previous dose. Patients already taking montelukast sodium daily for another indication (including chronic asthma) should not take an additional dose to prevent EIB. All patients should have available for rescue a short-acting β-agonist.

                        Daily administration of montelukast sodium for the chronic treatment of asthma has not been established to prevent acute episodes of EIB.
