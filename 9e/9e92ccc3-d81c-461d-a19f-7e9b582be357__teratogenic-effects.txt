DailyMed 9e92ccc3-d81c-461d-a19f-7e9b582be357 | dextroamphetamine sulfate | Teratogenic Effects

Dextroamphetamine sulfate extended-release has been shown to have embryotoxic and teratogenic effects when administered to A/Jax mice and C57BL mice in doses approximately 41 times the maximum human dose. Embryotoxic effects were not seen in New Zealand white rabbits given the drug in doses 7 times the human dose nor in rats given 12.5 times the maximum human dose. While there are no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women, there has been 1 report of severe congenital bony deformity, tracheoesophageal fistula, and anal atresia (VATER association) in a baby born to a woman who took dextroamphetamine sulfate with lovastatin during the first trimester of pregnancy. Dextroamphetamine sulfate extended-release should be used during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus.
