DailyMed 52cf3ba9-0a7c-4809-830e-af93d1532726 | CARBAMAZEPINE | Aplastic Anemia and Agranulocytosis

Aplastic anemia and agranulocytosis have been reported in association with the use of carbamazepine (see

  BOXED WARNING). Patients with a history of adverse hematologic reaction to any drug may be particularly at risk of bone marrow depression.
