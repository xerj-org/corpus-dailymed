DailyMed 434fb630-c8d6-4583-b3ee-8e4ba6552c3d | CLAFORAN | HOW SUPPLIED

Sterile CLAFORAN (cefotaxime for injection) is a dry off-white to pale yellow crystalline powder supplied in single-dose vials containing cefotaxime sodium as follows:

                  1 g cefotaxime (equivalent to 1.048 g of cefotaxime sodium) in single-dose vials in packages of 10 (NDC 21586-031-02).

                  Store CLAFORAN dry powder at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77ºF); excursions permitted to 15ºC to 30ºC (59ºF to 86ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] and should be protected from excessive light.

                  The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
