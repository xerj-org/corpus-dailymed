DailyMed 07a701e2-39b7-4bb1-a7c7-d9baed4ab18a | Guanfacine Extended-Release | 2.7 Dosage Adjustment with Concomitant Use of Strong and Moderate CYP3A4 Inhibitors or Inducers

Dosage adjustments for guanfacine extended-release tablets are recommended with concomitant use of strong and moderate CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole), or CYP3A4 inducers (e.g., carbamazepine) ( Table 2 ) [see
