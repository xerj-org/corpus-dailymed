DailyMed 07a701e2-39b7-4bb1-a7c7-d9baed4ab18a | Guanfacine Extended-Release | 2.5 Discontinuation of Treatment

Following discontinuation of guanfacine extended-release tablets, patients may experience increases in blood pressure and heart rate [see
                            Warnings and Precautions (

                              5.4

                           )
                            and
                            Adverse Reactions (

                              6

                           )
                           ]. Patients/caregivers should be instructed not to discontinue guanfacine extended-release tablets without consulting their health care provider. Monitor blood pressure and pulse when reducing the dose or discontinuing the drug. Taper the daily dose in decrements of no more than 1 mg every 3 to 7 days to minimize the risk of rebound hypertension.
