DailyMed 07a701e2-39b7-4bb1-a7c7-d9baed4ab18a | Guanfacine Extended-Release | 5.2 Sedation and Somnolence

Somnolence and sedation were commonly reported adverse reactions in clinical studies [see
                           Adverse Reactions (

                              6.1

                           )
                           ]. Before using guanfacine extended-release tablets with other centrally active depressants, consider the potential for additive sedative effects. Caution patients against operating heavy equipment or driving until they know how they respond to treatment with guanfacine extended-release tablets .  Advise patients to avoid use with alcohol.
