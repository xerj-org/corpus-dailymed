DailyMed 2f73a4db-2c7f-4e42-a914-87aedc2780fb | Ticagrelor | 6.2 Postmarketing Experience

The following adverse reactions have been identified during post-approval use of ticagrelor tablets. Because these reactions are reported voluntarily from a population of an unknown size, it is not always possible to reliably estimate their frequency or establish a causal relationship to drug exposure.

                            

                           Blood and lymphatic system disorders: Thrombotic Thrombocytopenic Purpura (TTP) has been rarely reported with the use of ticagrelor tablets. TTP is a serious condition which can occur after a brief exposure (less than 2 weeks) and requires prompt treatment.  

                           Immune system disorders: Hypersensitivity reactions including angioedema [see Contraindications (4.3)].

                           Respiratory Disorders: Central sleep apnea, Cheyne-Stokes respiration
