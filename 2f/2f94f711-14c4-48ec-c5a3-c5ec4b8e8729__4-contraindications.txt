DailyMed 2f94f711-14c4-48ec-c5a3-c5ec4b8e8729 | Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine Injections are contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to lidocaine or to any local anesthetics of the amide-type or to other components of Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine Injections.
