DailyMed 2f94f711-14c4-48ec-c5a3-c5ec4b8e8729 | Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine | 5.15 Risk of Inadvertent Trauma to Tongue, Lips, and Buccal Mucosa in Dental Applications

Because of the long duration of anesthesia, when Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine Injection [0.5% (5 mg/mL) of lidocaine] is used for dental injections, warn patients about the possibility of inadvertent trauma to tongue, lips, and buccal mucosa and advise them not to chew solid foods until sensation returns [see Patient Counseling Information (17)].
