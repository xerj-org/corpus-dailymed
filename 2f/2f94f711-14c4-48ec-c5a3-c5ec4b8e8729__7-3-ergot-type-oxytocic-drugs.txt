DailyMed 2f94f711-14c4-48ec-c5a3-c5ec4b8e8729 | Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine | 7.3 Ergot-Type Oxytocic Drugs

Concurrent administration of vasopressor drugs (for the treatment of hypotension related to obstetric blocks) and ergot-type oxytocic drugs may cause severe, persistent hypertension or cerebrovascular accidents. Avoid use of Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine Injection concomitantly with ergot-type oxytocic drugs [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
