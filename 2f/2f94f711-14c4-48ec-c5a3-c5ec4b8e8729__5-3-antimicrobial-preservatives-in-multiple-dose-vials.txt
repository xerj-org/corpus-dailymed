DailyMed 2f94f711-14c4-48ec-c5a3-c5ec4b8e8729 | Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine | 5.3 Antimicrobial Preservatives in Multiple-Dose Vials

Avoid use of Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine Injection solutions containing antimicrobial preservatives (i.e., those supplied in multiple-dose vials) for epidural or caudal anesthesia because safety has not been established with such use.
