DailyMed 2f73a4db-2c7f-4e42-a914-87aedc2780fb | Ticagrelor | 5.5 Severe Hepatic Impairment

Avoid use of ticagrelor tablets in patients with severe hepatic impairment. Severe hepatic impairment is likely to increase serum concentration of ticagrelor. There are no studies of ticagrelor tablets patients with severe hepatic impairment [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
