DailyMed 2f94f711-14c4-48ec-c5a3-c5ec4b8e8729 | Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine | 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine Injection is indicated in adult and pediatric patients for the production of local or regional anesthesia or analgesia for surgery, dental, and oral surgery procedures, diagnostic and therapeutic procedures, and for obstetrical procedures. Specific concentrations and presentations of Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine Injection are recommended for each type of block indicated to produce local or regional anesthesia or analgesia [see Dosage and Administration (2.2)].
