DailyMed 2f73a4db-2c7f-4e42-a914-87aedc2780fb | Ticagrelor | 2.3 Coronary Artery Disease but No Prior Stroke or Myocardial Infarction

Administer 60 mg of ticagrelor tablets twice daily. Generally, use ticagrelor tablets with a daily maintenance dose of aspirin of 75 mg to 100 mg [see Clinical Studies (14)].
