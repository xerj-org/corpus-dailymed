DailyMed 2f94f711-14c4-48ec-c5a3-c5ec4b8e8729 | Lidocaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine | 7.7 Phenothiazines and Butyrophenones

Phenothiazines and butyrophenones may reduce or reverse the pressor effect of epinephrine. Concurrent use of Lidocaine Hydrochloride with Epinephrine and these agents should generally be avoided. In situations when concurrent therapy is necessary, careful patient monitoring is essential.
