DailyMed 2f73a4db-2c7f-4e42-a914-87aedc2780fb | Ticagrelor | 5.2 Discontinuation of Ticagrelor Tablets in Patients Treated for Coronary Artery Disease

Discontinuation of ticagrelor tablets will increase the risk of myocardial infarction, stroke, and death in patients being treated for coronary artery disease. If ticagrelor tablets must be temporarily discontinued (e.g., to treat bleeding or for significant surgery), restart it as soon as possible. When possible, interrupt therapy with ticagrelor tablets for five days prior to surgery that has a major risk of bleeding. Resume ticagrelor tablets as soon as hemostasis is achieved.
