DailyMed da78d290-c71f-44bc-8db4-58fe4195e2f8 | Allopurinol | 8.6 Renal Impairment

Allopurinol and its primary active metabolite, oxipurinol, are eliminated by the kidneys; therefore, changes in renal function have a profound effect on exposure. In patients with decreased renal function or who have concurrent illnesses which can affect renal function, perform periodic laboratory parameters of renal function and reassess the patient's dosage of allopurinol

  [see

   Dosage and Administration (2.6),

   Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
