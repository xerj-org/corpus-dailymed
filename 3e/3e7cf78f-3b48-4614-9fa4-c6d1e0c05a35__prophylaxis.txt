Doxycycline is indicated for the prophylaxis of malaria due to

  Plasmodium falciparumin short-term travelers (<4 months) to areas with chloroquine and/or pyrimethamine-sulfadoxine resistant strains. (See

  DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION section and

  Information for Patients subsection of the

  PRECAUTIONS section.)
