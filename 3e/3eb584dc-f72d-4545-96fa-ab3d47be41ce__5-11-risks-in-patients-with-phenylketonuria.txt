DailyMed 3eb584dc-f72d-4545-96fa-ab3d47be41ce | Mycophenolate mofetil | 5.11 Risks in Patients with Phenylketonuria

Phenylalanine can be harmful to patients with phenylketonuria (PKU). CELLCEPT® Oral Suspension (mycophenolate mofetil) for oral suspension contains aspartame, a source of phenylalanine (0.56 mg phenylalanine/mL suspension). Before prescribing CELLCEPT® Oral Suspension (mycophenolate mofetil) for oral suspension to a patient with PKU, consider the combined daily amount of phenylalanine from all sources, including mycophenolate mofetil.
