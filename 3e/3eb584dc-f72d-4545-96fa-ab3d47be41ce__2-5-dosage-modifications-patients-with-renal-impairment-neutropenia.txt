Renal Impairment

                        No dosage modifications are needed in kidney transplant patients with delayed graft function postoperatively [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]. In kidney transplant patients with severe chronic impairment of the graft (GFR <25 mL/min/1.73 m2), do not administer doses of mycophenolate mofetil capsules greater than 1 g twice a day. These patients should be carefully monitored [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

                           Neutropenia

                        If neutropenia develops (ANC <1.3 x 103/µL), dosing with mycophenolate mofetil capsules should be interrupted or reduced, appropriate diagnostic tests performed, and the patient managed appropriately [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4) and Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
