DailyMed d68b79ab-3104-410e-a63f-9919a23625af | Ticagrelor | 2.4 Acute Ischemic Stroke or Transient Ischemic Attack (TIA)

Initiate treatment with a 180 mg loading dose of ticagrelor tablet and then continue with 90 mg twice daily for up to 30 days. Administer the first maintenance dose 6 to 12 hours after the loading dose. 

                        Use ticagrelor tablet with a loading dose of aspirin (300 mg to 325 mg) and a daily maintenance dose of aspirin of 75 mg to 100 mg [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2) and Clinical Studies (14)].
