Exposure-Response Relationships

                        The exposure-response relationship and time course of pharmacodynamic response for the effectiveness of capivasertib have not been fully characterized. An exposure-response relationship was observed where increased capivasertib exposure was associated with increased incidence of diarrhea (CTCAE Grade 2 to 4), rash (CTCAE Grade 2 to 4) and hyperglycemia (CTCAE Grades 3 or 4) at doses of 80 to 800 mg (0.2 to 2 times the approved recommended dosage).

                           Cardiac Electrophysiology

                        At the recommended TRUQAP dose, a mean increase in the QTc interval > 20 ms was not observed.
