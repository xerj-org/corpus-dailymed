Rivaroxaban Tablets, USP are available in the strength and packages listed below:

                     2.5 mg tablets are round, light yellow to yellow biconvex, film coated tablet debossed with 'LS6' on one side and plain on other side. The tablets are supplied in the packages listed:

                  Bottle of 60 tablets with child-resistant closure                                     NDC 70756-006-60

                  Bottle of 180 tablets with child-resistant closure                                   NDC 70756-006-81

                  Bottle of 1000 tablets with child-resistant closure                                 NDC 70756-006-12

                  Blister package containing 100 tablets                                                  NDC 70756-006-99

                  (10 blister cards containing 10 tablets each)

                  Store at room temperature between 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

                  Keep out of the reach of children.
