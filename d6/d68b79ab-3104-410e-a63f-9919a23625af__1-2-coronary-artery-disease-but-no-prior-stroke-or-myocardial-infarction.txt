DailyMed d68b79ab-3104-410e-a63f-9919a23625af | Ticagrelor | 1.2 Coronary Artery Disease but No Prior Stroke or Myocardial Infarction

Ticagrelor tablet is indicated to reduce the risk of a first MI or stroke in patients with coronary artery disease (CAD) at high risk for such events [see Clinical Studies (14.2)]. While use is not limited to this setting, the efficacy of ticagrelor tablet was established in a population with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM).
