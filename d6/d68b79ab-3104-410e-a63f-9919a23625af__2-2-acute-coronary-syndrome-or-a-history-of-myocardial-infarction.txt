DailyMed d68b79ab-3104-410e-a63f-9919a23625af | Ticagrelor | 2.2 Acute Coronary Syndrome or a History of Myocardial Infarction

Initiate treatment with a 180 mg loading dose of ticagrelor tablet. Administer the first 90 mg maintenance dose of ticagrelor tablet, 6 to 12 hours after the loading dose. Administer 90 mg of ticagrelor tablet twice daily during the first year after an ACS event. After one year, administer 60 mg of ticagrelor tablet twice daily. 

 Initiate ticagrelor tablet with a daily maintenance dose of aspirin of 75 mg to 100 mg. However, in patients who have undergone percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI), consider single antiplatelet therapy with ticagrelor tablet based on the evolving risk for thrombotic versus bleeding events [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1) and Clinical Studies (14)].
