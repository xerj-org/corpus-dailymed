DailyMed d68b79ab-3104-410e-a63f-9919a23625af | Ticagrelor | 2.3 Coronary Artery Disease but No Prior Stroke or Myocardial Infarction

Administer 60 mg of ticagrelor tablet twice daily.  

                        Generally, use ticagrelor tablet with a daily maintenance dose of aspirin of 75 mg to 100 mg [see Clinical Studies (14)].
