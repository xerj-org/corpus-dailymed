DailyMed d698c106-2322-401e-b738-cbd83c843ecf | TRUQAP | 13.2 Animal Toxicology and/or Pharmacology

Oral administration of capivasertib for up to 2 years resulted in islet of Langerhans hypertrophy/ hyperplasia in the pancreas and lens degeneration in rats at doses ≥ 15 mg/kg/day (≥ 0.1 times the human exposure at the recommended dose based on AUC).
