DailyMed 80bef3fd-4f6f-4190-8865-9ac46c8947a3 | Amoxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium | 1.8 Limitations of Use

Therapy may be initiated before bacteriological results are available when beta-lactamase-producing pathogens are suspected. When results show susceptibility to amoxicillin alone, amoxicillin and clavulanate potassium tablets should not be used.
