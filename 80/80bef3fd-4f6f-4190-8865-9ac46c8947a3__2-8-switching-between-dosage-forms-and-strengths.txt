DailyMed 80bef3fd-4f6f-4190-8865-9ac46c8947a3 | Amoxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium | 2.8 Switching Between Dosage Forms and Strengths

A
                           moxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium
                            
                           Tablets

                           Substitution among amoxicillin and clavulanate potassium formulations should only occur when the amoxicillin-to-­clavulanic acid ratio is matched; AUGMENTIN Chewable Tablets and AUGMENTIN for Oral Suspension are substitutable on a mg-per-mg basis, and certain Amoxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium Tablet strengths are substitutable with specific AUGMENTIN for Oral Suspension or AUGMENTIN Chewable Tablet strengths.

                           Amoxicillin and clavulanate potassium tablets should not be used in pediatric patients weighing less than 40 kg

   [see

    Indications and Usage (1),

    Dosage and Administration (2.4),

    Use in Specific Populations (8.4)]

   .
