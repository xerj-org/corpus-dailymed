DailyMed 80bef3fd-4f6f-4190-8865-9ac46c8947a3 | Amoxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium | 4.2 Cholestatic Jaundice/Hepatic Dysfunction

Amoxicillin and clavulanate potassium tablets are contraindicated in patients with a previous history of cholestatic jaundice/hepatic dysfunction associated with treatment with amoxicillin/clavulanate potassium.
