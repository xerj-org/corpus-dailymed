DailyMed 6e02229e-262c-4e25-9240-9e9de4eb7c1d | ONSWIK KwikPen | 8.4 Pediatric Use

Safety and effectiveness of ONSWIK have not been established in pediatric patients.

                        ONSWIK is not recommended for use in pediatric patients with type 1 diabetes mellitus due to increased risk of severe hypoglycemia [see Indications and Usage (1) and Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
