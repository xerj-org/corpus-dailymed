DailyMed 6e02229e-262c-4e25-9240-9e9de4eb7c1d | ONSWIK KwikPen | 14.1 Overview of Clinical Studies

The effectiveness of ONSWIK administered once weekly to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus and used in combination with common noninsulin antidiabetic medications with or without mealtime insulin was evaluated in four randomized, open-label, treat-to-target, active-controlled trials. ONSWIK was studied in insulin-naïve adults using fixed-dosage titration (Trial 1) and in insulin-naïve adults using flexible-dosage titration (Trial 2), in adults previously treated with once or twice daily basal insulin (Trial 3), and in adults previously treated with daily basal and mealtime insulin (Trial 4).
