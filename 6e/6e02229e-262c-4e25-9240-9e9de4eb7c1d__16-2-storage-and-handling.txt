DailyMed 6e02229e-262c-4e25-9240-9e9de4eb7c1d | ONSWIK KwikPen | 16.2 Storage and Handling

Dispense in the original sealed carton with the enclosed Instructions for Use.

                           Store ONSWIK refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) at all times.

                           Do not store your pen with the needle attached.

                           If refrigeration is unavailable, the pen can be stored at room temperature up to 30°C (86°F) for up to a total of 14 days. Dispose of ONSWIK that has been left at room temperature for longer than 14 days.

                           Do not freeze ONSWIK. Do not use ONSWIK if frozen. Keep away from heat and light.

                           Use within 112 days (16 weeks) after first use. Discard after 112 days (16 weeks) even if the pen still contains ONSWIK.
