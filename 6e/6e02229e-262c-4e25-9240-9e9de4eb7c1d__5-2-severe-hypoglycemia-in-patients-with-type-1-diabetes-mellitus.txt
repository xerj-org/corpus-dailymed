DailyMed 6e02229e-262c-4e25-9240-9e9de4eb7c1d | ONSWIK KwikPen | 5.2 Severe Hypoglycemia in Patients with Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus

ONSWIK is not recommended for use in patients with type 1 diabetes mellitus due to increased risk of severe hypoglycemia. In a 52-week clinical trial of adults with type 1 diabetes mellitus, the incidence of Level 3 (severe) hypoglycemia was higher for patients treated with ONSWIK compared with insulin degludec (10% versus 3%).
