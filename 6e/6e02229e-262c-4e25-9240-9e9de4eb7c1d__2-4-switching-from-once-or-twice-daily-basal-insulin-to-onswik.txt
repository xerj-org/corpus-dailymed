DailyMed 6e02229e-262c-4e25-9240-9e9de4eb7c1d | ONSWIK KwikPen | 2.4 Switching from Once- or Twice-Daily Basal Insulin to ONSWIK

Administer the first dose of ONSWIK on the day after the last dose of once- or twice-daily basal insulin.

                           Close glucose monitoring is recommended during the switch and until the dosage and glycemic control are stable.

                           Doses and timing of concurrent mealtime insulin products or other concomitant antidiabetic medications may require adjustments.

                           See Table 3 for dosage calculations and important considerations when switching patients from once- or twice-daily basal insulin to ONSWIK.
