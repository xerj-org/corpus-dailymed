DailyMed 6e02229e-262c-4e25-9240-9e9de4eb7c1d | ONSWIK KwikPen | 2.2 Important Administration Instructions

Always check insulin labels before administration [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

                           Inspect ONSWIK visually before use. Only use ONSWIK if the solution is clear and colorless to yellow with no visible particles.

                           Prior to initiation, train patients and their caregiver(s) on how to select their weekly ONSWIK dosage.

                           Instruct patients to visually verify the dialed units on the dose window of the ONSWIK KwikPen before each injection to avoid dosing errors, including underdosage and overdosage, that may lead to hyperglycemia or hypoglycemia, respectively.

                           Inject ONSWIK subcutaneously into the abdomen, upper arm, thigh, or buttocks.

                           Rotate injection sites within the same region from one injection to the next to reduce the risk of lipodystrophy and localized cutaneous amyloidosis. Do not inject into areas of lipodystrophy or localized cutaneous amyloidosis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4), Adverse Reactions (6.1)].

                           During changes to a patient's insulin regimen, increase the frequency of glucose monitoring [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].

                           Use ONSWIK prefilled pen products with caution in patients with visual impairment who may rely on audible clicks to dial their dose.

                           ONSWIK may be administered alone or in any combination with oral and injectable antidiabetic medicines and rapid-acting or short-acting insulin products.

                           Do not administer ONSWIK intramuscularly, intravenously or in an insulin pump.

                            Do not dilute or mix ONSWIK with any insulin product or other solutions.

                           Do not transfer ONSWIK from the prefilled pen to a syringe for administration [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
