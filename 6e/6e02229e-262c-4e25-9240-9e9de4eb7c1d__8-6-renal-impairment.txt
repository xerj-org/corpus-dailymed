DailyMed 6e02229e-262c-4e25-9240-9e9de4eb7c1d | ONSWIK KwikPen | 8.6 Renal Impairment

No clinically relevant differences in the pharmacokinetics of insulin efsitora alfa-gobe were observed across the different renal impairment subgroups [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]. Additional dose adjustment should not be necessary for patients with renal impairment. However, as with all insulin products, glucose monitoring should be intensified and the ONSWIK dosage adjusted on an individual basis in patients with renal impairment.
