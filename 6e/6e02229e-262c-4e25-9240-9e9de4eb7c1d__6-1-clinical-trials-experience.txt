Because clinical trials are conducted under widely varying conditions, adverse reaction rates observed in the clinical trials of a drug cannot be directly compared to rates in the clinical trials of another drug and may not reflect the rates observed in clinical practice.

                        The safety of ONSWIK was evaluated in four clinical trials involving 1,883 adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus exposed to ONSWIK, with a mean exposure duration of 372 days [see Clinical Studies (14)]. The mean age was 59 years, the mean duration of diabetes was 13 years, 55% were male, 63% were White, 21% were Asian, 10% were American Indian or Alaska Native, 5% were Black or African American, and 45% were Hispanic. The mean BMI was 31 kg/m2 and the mean HbA1c at baseline was 8.1%.
