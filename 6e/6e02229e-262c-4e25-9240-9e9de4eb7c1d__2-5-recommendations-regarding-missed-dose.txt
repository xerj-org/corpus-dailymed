DailyMed 6e02229e-262c-4e25-9240-9e9de4eb7c1d | ONSWIK KwikPen | 2.5 Recommendations Regarding Missed Dose

If a dose is missed:
                                 Administer the missed dose as soon as possible within 4 days of the missed dose then maintain the regular weekly dosing schedule.

                           If more than 4 days have passed there are two options. Patients can skip the missed dose and administer the usual dose on the regularly scheduled day. Alternatively, patients can administer the missed dose then resume the once-weekly dosing schedule, one week from the day the missed dose was administered.

                           Increase blood glucose monitoring with missed dosage.
