DailyMed 6e02229e-262c-4e25-9240-9e9de4eb7c1d | ONSWIK KwikPen | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

ONSWIK is contraindicated:

                     during episodes of hypoglycemia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].

                     in patients with hypersensitivity to insulin efsitora alfa-gobe or any of the excipients in ONSWIK. Serious hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis, have occurred with insulin and insulin analogs [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
