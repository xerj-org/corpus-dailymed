DailyMed 6e02229e-262c-4e25-9240-9e9de4eb7c1d | ONSWIK KwikPen | 2.6 Management of Accidental Extra Dose(s)

If the one-time starting dose is accidentally administered more than once (for example, if the one-time starting dose is administered for 2 consecutive weeks), skip the next week's dose.

                           If the weekly dosage is administered more than once in the same week, skip the next week's dose.

                           Monitor glucose levels more frequently and assess for repeated or severe hypoglycemia.
