Insulin efsitora alfa-gobe is a long-acting insulin analog that is divalent (dimer), consisting of a single-chain insulin (comprised of insulin B-chain analog, a short peptide linker, and an insulin A-chain analog) fused to a second peptide linker that is fused to a human IgG2 Fc region. Each monomer is comprised of 299 amino acid residues. Insulin efsitora alfa-gobe is produced using Chinese Hamster Ovary cells by recombinant DNA technology. The molecule contains 24-Cys residues that are involved in 2 interchain and 10 intrachain disulfide bonds. The predominant post-translational modification includes N-linked glycosylation of the heavy chain (Asn150). The molecular weight of insulin efsitora alfa-gobe is approximately 64 kDa.

                  ONSWIK (insulin efsitora alfa-gobe) injection is a clear and colorless to yellow solution for subcutaneous use.

                  ONSWIK injection is a sterile, preserved formulation supplied in two concentrations (500 units/mL or 1,000 units/mL) manufactured for use in a prefilled pen.

                  Each mL of ONSWIK U-500 contains 500 units of insulin efsitora alfa-gobe and the inactive ingredients benzyl alcohol (9 mg), dibasic sodium phosphate (0.084 mg), glycerin (17 mg), monobasic sodium phosphate (0.53 mg), phenol (1.8 mg), poloxamer 188 (0.4 mg), and Water for Injection. The 500 units/mL cartridge delivers 5 units of ONSWIK per 0.01 mL (14.3 mg/mL).

                  Each mL of ONSWIK U-1,000 contains 1,000 units of insulin efsitora alfa-gobe and the inactive ingredients benzyl alcohol (9 mg), dibasic sodium phosphate (0.168 mg), glycerin (17 mg), monobasic sodium phosphate (1.06 mg), phenol (1.8 mg), poloxamer 188 (0.4 mg), and Water for Injection. The 1,000 units/mL cartridge delivers 10 units of ONSWIK per 0.01 mL (28.6 mg/mL).

                  Hydrochloric acid, glacial acetic acid and/or sodium hydroxide may be added to adjust the pH. ONSWIK has a pH of approximately 6.5.
