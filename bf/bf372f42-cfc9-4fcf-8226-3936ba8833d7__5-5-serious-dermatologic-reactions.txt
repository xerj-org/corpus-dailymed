DailyMed bf372f42-cfc9-4fcf-8226-3936ba8833d7 | BRIVARACETAM | 5.5 Serious Dermatologic Reactions

Serious dermatologic reactions, including Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS) and toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), have been reported in patients treated with brivaracetam tablets. Time to onset of the serious dermatologic reaction ranged from 3 to 45 days after brivaracetam tablets initiation in reported cases. Brivaracetam tablets should be discontinued at the first sign of a rash, unless the rash is clearly not drug-related. If signs or symptoms suggest a serious dermatologic reaction, use of brivaracetam tablets should not be resumed and alternative therapy should be considered.
