DailyMed bf372f42-cfc9-4fcf-8226-3936ba8833d7 | BRIVARACETAM | 9.2 Abuse

In a human abuse potential study, single doses of brivaracetam tablets at therapeutic and supratherapeutic doses were compared to alprazolam (C-IV) (1.5 mg and 3 mg). Brivaracetam at the recommended single dose (50 mg) caused fewer sedative and euphoric effects than alprazolam; however, brivaracetam at supratherapeutic single doses (200 mg and 1000 mg) was similar to alprazolam on other measures of abuse.
