DailyMed bf372f42-cfc9-4fcf-8226-3936ba8833d7 | BRIVARACETAM | 6.2 Postmarketing Experience

The following adverse reactions have been identified during post approval use of brivaracetam tablets. Because these reactions are reported voluntarily from a population of uncertain size, it is not always possible to reliably estimate their frequency or establish a causal relationship to drug exposure.

                           Skin and Subcutaneous Tissue Disorders: Serious dermatologic reactions (e.g., Stevens-Johnson syndrome and toxic epidermal necrolysis) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
