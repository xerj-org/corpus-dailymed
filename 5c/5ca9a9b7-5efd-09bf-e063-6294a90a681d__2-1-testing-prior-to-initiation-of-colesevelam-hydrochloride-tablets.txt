DailyMed 5ca9a9b7-5efd-09bf-e063-6294a90a681d | COLESEVELAM HYDROCHLORIDE | 2.1 Testing Prior to Initiation of Colesevelam Hydrochloride Tablets

Obtain lipid parameters, including triglyceride (TG) levels, before starting colesevelam hydrochloride tablets. Colesevelam hydrochloride is contraindicated in patients with TG levels > 500 mg/dL
