DailyMed 5c530b12-5507-adfd-e063-6394a90afb0f | Plantar Patch | Directions

adults and children over 12 years:

                     apply patch for up to 12 hours of wear time

                     apply 1 patch at a time to the affected area, not more than 2 to 3 times daily

                     store in a cool dry place, in a resealable bag

                  children 12 years or younger: ask a doctor
