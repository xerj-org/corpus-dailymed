DailyMed 5c19b687-fd29-b22e-e063-6294a90ad67b | Imiquimod | 8.5 Geriatric Use

Of the 320 subjects treated with imiquimod cream in the AK clinical trials, 150 subjects (47%) were 65 years of age or older. No overall differences in safety or effectiveness of imiquimod cream have been observed between subjects 65 years of age and older and younger adult subjects.

  Of the 400 subjects treated with imiquimod cream in the EGW clinical studies, 5 subjects (1%) were 65 years or older. Clinical studies of imiquimod cream for EGW did not include sufficient numbers of subjects aged 65 years of age and older to determine whether they respond differently from younger adult subjects.
