DailyMed 5c52ea35-c2be-b462-e063-6294a90a19c1 | Trazodone Hydrochloride | 8.5 Geriatric Use

Reported clinical literature and experience with trazodone has not identified differences in responses between elderly and younger patients. However, as experience in the elderly withtrazodone hydrochloride is limited, it should be used with caution in geriatric patients.

                        Serotonergic antidepressants have been associated with cases of clinically significant hyponatremia in elderly patients, who may be at greater risk for this adverse reaction
