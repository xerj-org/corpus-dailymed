DailyMed 5c19b687-fd29-b22e-e063-6294a90ad67b | Imiquimod | 5.2 Systemic Reactions

Flu-like signs and symptoms have been reported with use of imiquimod cream andmay accompany, or even precede, local skin reactions

  [see Adverse Reactions (6)].

  Signs and symptoms may include fatigue, nausea, fever, myalgias, arthralgias, malaise and chills. Concomitant use of imiquimod cream and any other imiquimod products may increase the risk for and severity of systemic reactions.

  Lymphadenopathy occurred in 2% of subjects with AK treated with imiquimod cream, 3.75% and in no patients in the vehicle arm

  [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)]. This reaction resolved in all subjects by 4 weeks after completion of treatment.

  Consider an interruption of dosing if systemic reactions occur.
