DailyMed 5c2ac7cb-3a38-2174-e063-6394a90a95cc | Tramadol Hydrochloride | 5.19 Risks of Driving and Operating Machinery

Tramadol hydrochloride tablets may impair the mental or physical abilities needed to perform potentially hazardous activities such as driving a car or operating machinery. Warn patients not to drive or operate dangerous machinery unless they are tolerant to the effects of tramadol hydrochloride tablets and know how they will react to the medication
