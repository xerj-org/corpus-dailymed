DailyMed 5ca218af-b3f4-512a-e063-6394a90a3ec6 | HYDROXYCHLOROQUINE SULFATE | 5.4 Worsening of Psoriasis

Administration of hydroxychloroquine sulfate tablets to patients with psoriasis may precipitate a severe flare-up of psoriasis. Avoid hydroxychloroquine sulfate tablets in patients with psoriasis, unless the benefit to the patient outweighs the possible risk.
