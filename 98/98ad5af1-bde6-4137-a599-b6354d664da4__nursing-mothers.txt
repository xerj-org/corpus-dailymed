DailyMed 98ad5af1-bde6-4137-a599-b6354d664da4 | Methylergonovine Maleate | Nursing Mothers

Mothers should not breast-feed during treatment with methylergonovine maleate and at least 12 hours after administration of the last dose. Milk secreted during this period should be discarded. Methylergonovine Maleate may produce adverse effects in the breast-feeding infant. Methylergonovine Maleate may also reduce the yield of breast milk
