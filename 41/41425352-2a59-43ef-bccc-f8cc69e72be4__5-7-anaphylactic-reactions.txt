DailyMed 41425352-2a59-43ef-bccc-f8cc69e72be4 | Piroxicam | 5.7 Anaphylactic Reactions

Piroxicam has been associated with anaphylactic reactions in patients with and without known hypersensitivity to piroxicam and in patients with aspirin-sensitive asthma

  [see Contraindications (4) and Warnings and Precautions (5.8)].

                        Seek emergency help if an anaphylactic reaction occurs.
