DailyMed 2354c3e2-7b5f-4fdd-bbf2-7fa3c57686f8 | Tadalafil | 5.15 Risk of Allergic Reactions due to Tartrazine

Tadalafil Tablets contains FD&C Yellow No. 5 (tartrazine) which may cause allergic-type reactions (including bronchial asthma) in certain susceptible persons. Although the overall incidence of FD&C Yellow No. 5 (tartrazine) sensitivity in the general population is low, it is frequently seen in patients who also have aspirin hypersensitivity.
