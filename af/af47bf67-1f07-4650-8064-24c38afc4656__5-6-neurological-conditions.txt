DailyMed af47bf67-1f07-4650-8064-24c38afc4656 | ZUNVEYL | 5.6 Neurological Conditions

Cholinesterase inhibitors are believed to have some potential to cause generalized convulsions[seeAdverse Reactions (6.2)].Seizure activity may also be a manifestation of Alzheimer's disease. Patients with Alzheimer's disease should be monitored closely for seizures while taking ZUNVEYL.
