ZUNVEYL is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to benzgalantamine, galantamine, or to any inactive ingredient in ZUNVEYL. Serious skin reactions have occurred[seeWarnings and Precautions (5.1)andAdverse Reactions (6.2)].
