DailyMed af47bf67-1f07-4650-8064-24c38afc4656 | ZUNVEYL | 8.7 Renal Impairment

In patients with a creatinine clearance of 9 to 59 mL/min, an increase in exposure of galantamine was observed; therefore, a dosage adjustment is recommended[see Dosage and Administration (232) andClinical Pharmacology (12.3)]. The use of ZUNVEYL in patients with creatinine clearance less than 9 mL/min is not recommended.
