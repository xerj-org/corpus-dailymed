DailyMed f0511453-5f6f-4e3a-b7d0-a5cfde985656 | Utebzi | 10 OVERDOSAGE

In the event of an overdose, discontinue UTEBZI, treat symptomatically, and institute general supportive treatment. Removal of tebipenem from the systemic circulation is enhanced by hemodialysis [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]. Consider contacting the Poison Help line (1-800-222-1222) or a medical toxicologist for additional overdose management recommendations.
