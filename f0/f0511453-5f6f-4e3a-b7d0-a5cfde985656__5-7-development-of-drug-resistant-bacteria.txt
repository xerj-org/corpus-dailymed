DailyMed f0511453-5f6f-4e3a-b7d0-a5cfde985656 | Utebzi | 5.7 Development of Drug-Resistant Bacteria

Prescribing UTEBZI in the absence of a proven or strongly suspected bacterial infection or a prophylactic indication is unlikely to provide benefit to the patient and increases the risk of the development of drug‑resistant bacteria [see Indications and Usage (1.2)].
