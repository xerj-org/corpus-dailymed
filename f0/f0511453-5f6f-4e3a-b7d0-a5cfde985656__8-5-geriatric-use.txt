DailyMed f0511453-5f6f-4e3a-b7d0-a5cfde985656 | Utebzi | 8.5 Geriatric Use

Of the 843 patients treated with UTEBZI in Trial 1, 293 (35%) were 65 to less than 75 years of age and 192 (23%) patients were 75 years of age and older. No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between elderly (65 years of age and older) adults and younger adult patients, but greater sensitivity of some older individuals cannot be ruled out. No clinically significant changes in the pharmacokinetics of UTEBZI were observed in patients 65 years of age and older compared to younger adult patients [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]. Dosage adjustment for elderly patients should be based on renal function [see Dosage and Administration (2.2), Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
