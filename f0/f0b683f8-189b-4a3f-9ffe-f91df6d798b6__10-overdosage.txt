DailyMed f0b683f8-189b-4a3f-9ffe-f91df6d798b6 | Ascorbic acid | 10 OVERDOSAGE

Overdose with ascorbic acid may cause nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, facial flushing, rash, headache, fatigue or disturbed sleep. If overdose of ascorbic acid injection occurs, immediately discontinue administration and treat symptoms and signs of overdose, avoiding additional intake of ascorbic acid.
