DailyMed f0511453-5f6f-4e3a-b7d0-a5cfde985656 | Utebzi | 6.2 Postmarketing Experience

The following adverse reactions have been identified during post approval use of tebipenem pivoxil outside of the U.S. Because these reactions are reported voluntarily from a population of uncertain size, it is not always possible to reliably estimate their frequency or establish a causal relationship to drug exposure.

                        Adverse reactions not observed in clinical studies of UTEBZI that have been observed in Japan with a tebipenem pivoxil fine granules formulation in pediatric patients include:

                              •

                              Metabolism and Nutrition Disorders: Hypoglycemia with hypocarnitinemia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].

                              •

                              Neurologic: Seizures
