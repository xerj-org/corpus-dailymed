UTEBZI (tebipenem pivoxil) tablets are green, round, biconvex, film‑coated, debossed with “TBP” on one side and “300” on the other side and contain 300 mg of tebipenem pivoxil.

                  Blister pack of 80 tablets: 8 tablets per blister strip, 10 strips per pack (NDC 0173-0956-76).

                  Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°). [See USP controlled room temperature].
