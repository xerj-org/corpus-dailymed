DailyMed f0511453-5f6f-4e3a-b7d0-a5cfde985656 | Utebzi | 7.3 Drug Interference with Newborn Screening Test

Treatment of a pregnant individual with UTEBZI prior to delivery may cause a false positive test for isovaleric acidemia in the newborn as part of newborn screening. Prompt follow-up of a positive newborn screening result for isovaleric acidemia is recommended.
