Ascorbic acid injection, USP for intravenous use is a colorless to pale yellow, preservative-free, hypertonic, sterile, non-pyrogenic solution of ascorbic acid. Ascorbic acid injection must be diluted with an appropriate infusion solution (e.g., 5% Dextrose Injection, Sterile Water for Injection) [see Dosage and Administration (2.1)].
