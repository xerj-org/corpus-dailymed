DailyMed b3f69c67-db30-4266-8075-9b18c71c9fc1 | Temozolomide | 13.2 Animal Toxicology and/or Pharmacology

Toxicology studies in rats and dogs identified a low incidence of hemorrhage, degeneration, and necrosis of the retina at temozolomide doses equal to or greater than 125 mg/m

  2(0.63 times the human dose of 200 mg/m

  2). These changes were most commonly seen at doses where mortality was observed.
