DailyMed b4c4c0c7-5372-488c-82ad-4a0f8bb0e427 | Doxepin | 2.3 Administration

Doxepin should be taken within 30 minutes of bedtime.

                        To minimize the potential for next day effects, doxepin should not be taken within 3 hours of a meal [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

                        The total doxepin dose should not exceed 6 mg per day.
