DailyMed b4c4c0c7-5372-488c-82ad-4a0f8bb0e427 | Doxepin | 4.2 Co-administration with Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs)

Serious side effects and even death have been reported following the concomitant use of certain drugs with MAO inhibitors. Do not administer doxepin if patient is currently on MAOIs or has used MAOIs within the past two weeks. The exact length of time may vary depending on the particular MAOI dosage and duration of treatment.
