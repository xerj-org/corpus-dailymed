DailyMed e082a024-7850-400b-a5c2-2a140612562a | Zortress | 5.14 Interaction With Strong Inhibitors and Inducers of CYP3A

Concomitant use of Zortress with strong CYP3A inhibitors or strong CYP3A inducers is not recommended. If concomitant use is not avoidable, closely monitor everolimus whole blood trough concentrations [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
