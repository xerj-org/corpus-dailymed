DailyMed e082a024-7850-400b-a5c2-2a140612562a | Zortress | 2.3 Recommended Dosage in Adult Patients with a Liver Transplant

Start Zortress at least 30 days posttransplant. The initial recommended dosage of Zortress, in combination with reduced-dose tacrolimus, in adult patients with a liver transplant is 1 mg orally twice daily (2 mg per day) [see Dosage and Administration (2.4, 2.6)].

                        Steroid doses may be further tapered on an individualized basis depending on the clinical status of patient and function of graft.
