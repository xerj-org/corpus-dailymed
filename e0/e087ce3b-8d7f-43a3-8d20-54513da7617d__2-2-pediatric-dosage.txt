DailyMed e087ce3b-8d7f-43a3-8d20-54513da7617d | Metformin Hydrochloride | 2.2 Pediatric Dosage

Measure the metformin hydrochloride oral solution dose in the metformin hydrochloride oral solution specific dosing cup.

                           The recommended starting dose of metformin hydrochloride oral solution for pediatric patients 10 years of age and older is 500 mg (5 mL) orally twice a day, given with meals.

                           Increase dosage in increments of 500 mg (5 mL) weekly on the basis of glycemic control and tolerability, up to a maximum of 2,000 mg (20 mL) per day, given in divided doses twice daily.
