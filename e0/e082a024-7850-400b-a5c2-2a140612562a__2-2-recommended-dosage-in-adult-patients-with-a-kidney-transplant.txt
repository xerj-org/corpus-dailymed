DailyMed e082a024-7850-400b-a5c2-2a140612562a | Zortress | 2.2 Recommended Dosage in Adult Patients with a Kidney Transplant

The initial recommended dosage of Zortress, in combination with reduced-dose cyclosporine, in adult patients with a kidney transplant  is 0.75 mg orally twice daily (1.5 mg per day), administered as soon as possible after transplantation [see Dosage and Administration (2.4, 2.5)].

                        Oral prednisone should be initiated once oral medication is tolerated. Steroid doses may be further tapered on an individualized basis depending on the clinical status of patient and function of graft.
