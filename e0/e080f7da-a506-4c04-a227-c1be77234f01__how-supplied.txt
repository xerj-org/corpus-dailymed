Ibuprofen Tablets, USP are available in the following strengths, colors and sizes:

                  Ibuprofen Tablets USP,
  400 mg are white, round, biconvex, aqueous film-coated tablets, debossed "IP 464" on obverse and plain on reverse. They are available as follows:

Unit dose packages of 100 (10 x 10) NDC 60687-446-01

                  Ibuprofen Tablets USP,
  600 mg are white, oval-shaped, biconvex, aqueous film-coated tablets, debossed "IP 465" on obverse and plain on reverse. They are available as follows:

Unit dose packages of 100 (10 x 10) NDC 60687-457-01

                  Ibuprofen Tablets USP,
  800 mg are white, capsule-shaped, biconvex, aqueous film-coated tablets, debossed "IP 466" on obverse and plain on reverse. They are available as follows:

Unit dose packages of 100 (10 x 10) NDC 60687-468-01

                  Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature]. Avoid excessive heat 40°C (104°F).
