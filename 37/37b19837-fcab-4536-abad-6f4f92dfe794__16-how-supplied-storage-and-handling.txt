Brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension USP, 1% is supplied in a round LDPE bottle with an extended LLDPE controlled dropper tip and orange cap as follows:

                     Storage and Handling

                  Store brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension USP, 1% at 4°C to 30°C (39°F to 86°F). Shake well before use. After opening, brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension USP, 1% can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
