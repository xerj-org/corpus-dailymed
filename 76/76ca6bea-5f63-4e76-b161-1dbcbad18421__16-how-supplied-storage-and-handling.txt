How Supplied

                  Allopurinol is available in multiple tablet strengths with functional scoring and package sizes as listed below.

                  Allopurinol Tablets, USP 100 mg are white to off-white, uncoated, round shaped, beveled edged tablets debossed with ‘AL’ and ‘100’ separated with breakline on one side and plain surface on the other side.

                  Bottles of 10 NDC 85766-287-10 (repackaged from NDC 59651-285-XX)

                     Storage and Handling

  Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F). [See USP for Controlled Room Temperature.] Protect from moisture and light.

                  Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.
