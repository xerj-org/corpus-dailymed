DailyMed 76ca6bea-5f63-4e76-b161-1dbcbad18421 | Allopurinol | 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Allopurinol tablets, USP have functional scoring and are available in the following strengths:

                     100 mg: White to off-white, uncoated, round shaped, beveled edged tablets debossed with ‘AL’ and ‘100’ separated with breakline on one side and plain surface on the other side.

                     300 mg: White to off-white, uncoated, round shaped, beveled edged tablets debossed with ‘AL’ and ‘300’ separated with breakline on one side and plain surface on the other side.
