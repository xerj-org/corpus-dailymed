DailyMed 76b15f6a-51d6-45ec-b6cb-8b10a08ed32a | Physicians EZ Use M-pred | 7.3 Ergot-Type Oxytocic Drugs

Concurrent administration of Bupivacaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine Injection and ergot-type oxytocic drugs may cause severe, persistent hypertension or cerebrovascular accidents. Avoid use of Bupivacaine Hydrochloride and Epinephrine concomitantly with ergot-type oxytocic drugs

   Warnings and Precautions (5.6)].
