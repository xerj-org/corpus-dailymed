DailyMed 01bdc479-1f1f-af94-837c-12e7e861321a | Prednisone delayed release | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Prednisone delayed-release tablets are contraindicated in patients who have known hypersensitivity to prednisone or to any of the excipients. Rare instances of anaphylaxis have occurred in patients receiving corticosteroid therapy [see Adverse Reactions (6)
                     ].
