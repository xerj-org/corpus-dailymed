DailyMed 01bdc479-1f1f-af94-837c-12e7e861321a | Prednisone delayed release | 2.3 Method of Administration

Prednisone delayed-release tablets are for oral administration.

                        Prednisone delayed-release tablets should be taken daily with food. [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]

                        Prednisone delayed-release tablets should not be broken, divided, or chewed because the delayed release of prednisone is dependent on an intact coating [see Description (11)].
