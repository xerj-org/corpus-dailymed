DailyMed dd62a2b8-300b-d92b-7b27-a30eaa68fdd3 | Tofacitinib | 5.8 Hypoglycemia in Patients with Diabetes

Hypoglycemia, including severe hypoglycemia, has been reported following initiation of tofacitinib extended-release tablets and other JAK inhibitors in patients with diabetes. During treatment with tofacitinib extended-release tablets, consider increased monitoring of blood glucose as clinically indicated in patients with diabetes. Advise patients with diabetes to notify their healthcare provider if they develop signs or symptoms of hypoglycemia.
