DailyMed ed789dc1-0b39-4efc-990d-7b6b43a8b3ba | Irbesartan | 6.2 Postmarketing Experience

The following adverse reactions have been identified during postapproval use of irbesartan tablets. Because these reactions are reported voluntarily from a population of uncertain size, it is not always possible to estimate reliably their frequency or to establish a causal relationship to drug exposure.

Urticaria; angioedema (involving swelling of the face, lips, pharynx, and/or tongue); anaphylactic reaction including anaphylactic shock; increased liver function tests; jaundice; hepatitis; hyperkalemia; anemia; thrombocytopenia; increased cpk; tinnitus; and hypoglycemia in diabetic patients.
