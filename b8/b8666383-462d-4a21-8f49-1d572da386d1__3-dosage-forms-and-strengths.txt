DailyMed b8666383-462d-4a21-8f49-1d572da386d1 | Fluoxetine | 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Fluoxetine Capsules USP, 20 mg* are opaque green cap/opaque off white body, size ‘3’ hard gelatin capsule filled with white to off-white granular powder and imprinted with ‘E’ on opaque green cap and ‘91’ on opaque off white body with black ink.

                  *Fluoxetine base equivalent.
