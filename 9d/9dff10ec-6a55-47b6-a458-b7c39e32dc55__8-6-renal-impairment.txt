DailyMed 9dff10ec-6a55-47b6-a458-b7c39e32dc55 | Temozolomide | 8.6 Renal Impairment

No dosage adjustment is recommended for patients with creatinine clearance (CLcr) of 36 to 130 mL/min/m2

  [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].The recommended dose of Temozolomide has not been established for patients with severe renal impairment (CLcr < 36 mL/min/m2) or for patients with end-stage renal disease on dialysis.
