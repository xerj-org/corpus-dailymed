DailyMed 9d8fd052-b478-447e-a884-74fcd21275bb | Terazosin hydrochloride | Priapism

Rarely, (probably less than once in every several thousand patients), terazosin and other α1-antagonists have been associated with priapism (painful penile erection, sustained for hours and unrelieved by sexual intercourse or masturbation). Two or three dozen cases have been reported. Because this condition can lead to permanent impotence if not promptly treated, patients must be advised about the seriousness of the condition (see
                              PRECAUTIONS: Information for Patients
                           ).
