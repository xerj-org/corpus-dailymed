DailyMed 508c5de8-9c70-57f5-e063-6394a90af295 | PREXXARTAN | 2.4 Heart Failure

The recommended starting dose of PREXXARTAN
  TM is 40 mg twice daily orally. Titrate to 80 mg and 160 mg twice daily orally, as tolerated by the patient. Consider reducing the dose of concomitant diuretics. The maximum daily dose administered in clinical trials is 320 mg in divided doses.
