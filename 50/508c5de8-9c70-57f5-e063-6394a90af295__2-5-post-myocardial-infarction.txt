PREXXARTAN
  TM may be initiated as early as 12 hours after a myocardial infarction. The recommended starting dose of PREXXARTAN
  TM is 20 mg twice daily orally. Patients may be up titrated within 7 days to 40 mg twice daily orally, with subsequent titrations to a target maintenance dose of 160 mg twice daily orally, as tolerated by the patient. If symptomatic hypotension or renal dysfunction occurs, consider dosage reduction. PREXXARTAN
  TM may be given with other standard post-myocardial infarction treatment, including thrombolytics, aspirin, beta-blockers, and statins.
