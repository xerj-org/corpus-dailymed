DailyMed 508c5de8-9c70-57f5-e063-6394a90af295 | PREXXARTAN | 8.6 Renal Impairment

Safety and effectiveness of valsartan in patients with severe renal impairment (glomerular filtration rate less than 30 mL/min/1.73m
  2) have not been established. No dose adjustment is required in patients with mild (glomerular filtration rate 60 to 90 mL/min/1.73m
  2) or moderate (glomerular filtration rate 30 to 60 mL/min/1.73m
  2) renal impairment.
