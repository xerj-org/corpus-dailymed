DailyMed 508c5de8-9c70-57f5-e063-6394a90af295 | PREXXARTAN | 2.3 Pediatric Hypertension 6 to 16 Years of Age

The recommended starting dose is 0.65 mg/kg twice daily orally (up to 40 mg total daily dose). The dosage should be adjusted according to blood pressure response and tolerability, up to a maximum dose of 1.35 mg/kg twice daily orally (maximum daily dose 160 mg). Doses higher than 1.35 mg/kg twice daily orally (or >160 mg total daily dose) have not been studied in pediatric patients 6 to 16 years old.
