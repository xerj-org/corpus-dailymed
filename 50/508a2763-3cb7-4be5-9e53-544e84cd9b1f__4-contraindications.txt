DailyMed 508a2763-3cb7-4be5-9e53-544e84cd9b1f | Novolin70/30 | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

NOVOLIN 70/30 is contraindicated:

                        •

During episodes of hypoglycemia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].

                        •

In patients who have had hypersensitivity reactions to NOVOLIN 70/30 or any of its excipients [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
