DailyMed c8d012c4-f411-4484-a618-7597599ea621 | Azithromycin monohydrate | 5.8 Infusion Site Reactions

Azithromycin for injection should be reconstituted and diluted as directed and administered as an intravenous infusion over not less than 60 minutes. 

  [see 

                              Dosage and Administration (2)]

                        Local IV site reactions have been reported with the intravenous administration of azithromycin. The incidence and severity of these reactions were the same when 500 mg azithromycin was given over 1 hour (2 mg/mL as 250 mL infusion) or over 3 hr (1 mg/mL as 500 mL infusion) 

  [see 

                              Adverse Reactions (6)].

  All volunteers who received infusate concentrations above 2 mg/mL experienced local IV site reactions and, therefore, higher concentrations should be avoided.
