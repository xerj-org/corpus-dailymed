Azithromycin for Injection, USP is supplied as white to off-white lyophilized powder under a vacuum in a single-dose vial equivalent to 500 mg of azithromycin for intravenous administration. 

  These are packaged as follows:

                     NDC                           Azithromycin for Injection, USP, 500 mg/vial         Package Factor

                  70095-025-02            500 mg Single-Dose Vial                                             10 vials per carton

                     Storage Conditions

                  Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F). [See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

                     Sterile, Nonpyrogenic, Preservative-free.

                     The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
