DailyMed c8d012c4-f411-4484-a618-7597599ea621 | Azithromycin monohydrate | 1.2 Pelvic Inflammatory Disease

Azithromycin for injection is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with pelvic inflammatory disease due to the following susceptible microorganisms:
  Chlamydia trachomatis,
  Neisseria gonorrhoeae, or
  Mycoplasma hominis in patients who require initial intravenous therapy. If anaerobic microorganisms are suspected of contributing to the infection, an antimicrobial agent with anaerobic activity should be administered in combination with azithromycin for injection.
