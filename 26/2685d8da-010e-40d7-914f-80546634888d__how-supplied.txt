DailyMed 2685d8da-010e-40d7-914f-80546634888d | levocarnitine | HOW SUPPLIED

Levocarnitine Injection, USP is available in 1 g per 5 mL single dose vials packaged 5 vials per carton (NDC 68083-681-05).

                  Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature]. Avoid excessive heat. Protect from freezing. Discard unused portion of an opened vial, as the formulation does not contain a preservative.
