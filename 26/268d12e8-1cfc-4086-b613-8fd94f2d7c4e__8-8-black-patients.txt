DailyMed 268d12e8-1cfc-4086-b613-8fd94f2d7c4e | olmesartan medoxomil / amlodipine besylate / hydrochlorothiazide | 8.8 Black Patients

Of the total number of patients who received olmesartan medoxomil, amlodipine and hydrochlorothiazide tablets in a randomized trial, 29% (184/627) were black. Olmesartan medoxomil, amlodipine and hydrochlorothiazide tablets was effective in lowering both systolic and diastolic blood pressure in black patients (usually a low-renin population) to the same extent as in non-black patients.
