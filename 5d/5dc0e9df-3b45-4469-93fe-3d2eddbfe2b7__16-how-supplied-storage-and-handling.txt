How Supplied

                  Dapagliflozin Tablets 5 mg are yellow, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘D1’ on one face and plain on the other face with an approximate dimension of 7.40 mm diameter and 3.40 mm thickness.

                  Bottles of 30        NDC 42571-317-30

                  Bottles of 90        NDC 42571-317-90

                  Bottles of 1000    NDC 42571-317-10

                  Dapagliflozin Tablets 10 mg are yellow, diamond shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘D2’ on one face and plain on the other face with an approximate dimension of 11.20 mm length, 8.20 mm width and 4.20 mm thickness.

                  Bottles of 30        NDC 42571-318-30

                  Bottles of 90        NDC 42571-318-90

                  Bottles of 1000    NDC 42571-318-10

                     Storage and Handling

                  Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
