DailyMed a9cfc301-3c7f-4910-8813-41dcb0b4ca69 | Imatinib Mesylate | 2.5 Pediatric Patients with Ph+ ALL

The recommended dose of imatinib mesylate tablets to be given in combination with chemotherapy to children with newly diagnosed Ph+ ALL is 340 mg/m2/day (not to exceed 600 mg). Imatinib mesylate tablet treatment can be given as a once daily dose.
