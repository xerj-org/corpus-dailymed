DailyMed 0d2710f2-ee47-4114-ab5c-8dca74cdcb8d | Dexmedetomidine; Dexmedetomidine in Dextrose | 1.2 Procedural Sedation

Dexmedetomidine Injection and Dexmedetomidine in 5% Dextrose Injection is indicated for sedation of non-intubated adult patients prior to and/or during surgical and other procedures.
