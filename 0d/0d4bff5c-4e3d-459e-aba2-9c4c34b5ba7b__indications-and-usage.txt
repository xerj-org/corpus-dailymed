DailyMed 0d4bff5c-4e3d-459e-aba2-9c4c34b5ba7b | Doxepin Hydrochloride | INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Doxepin hydrochloride cream, 5% is indicated for the short-term (up to 8 days) management of moderate pruritus in adult patients with atopic dermatitis or lichen simplex chronicus. (See
  DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION.)
