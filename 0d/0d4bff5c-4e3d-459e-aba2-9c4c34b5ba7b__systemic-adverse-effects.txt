DailyMed 0d4bff5c-4e3d-459e-aba2-9c4c34b5ba7b | Doxepin Hydrochloride | Systemic Adverse Effects:

In controlled clinical trials of patients treated with doxepin hydrochloride cream, the most common systemic adverse event reported was drowsiness. Drowsiness occurred in 71 of 330 (22%) of patients treated with doxepin hydrochloride cream compared to 7 of 334 (2%) of patients treated with vehicle cream. Drowsiness resulted in the premature discontinuation of the drug in approximately 5% of patients treated with doxepin hydrochloride cream in controlled clinical trials.
