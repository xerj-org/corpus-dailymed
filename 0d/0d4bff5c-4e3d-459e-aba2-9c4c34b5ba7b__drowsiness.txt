DailyMed 0d4bff5c-4e3d-459e-aba2-9c4c34b5ba7b | Doxepin Hydrochloride | Drowsiness:

Since drowsiness may occur with the use of doxepin hydrochloride cream, patients should be warned of the possibility and cautioned against driving a car or operating dangerous machinery while using this drug. Patients should also be cautioned that their response to alcohol may be potentiated.

                              Sedating drugs may cause confusion and oversedation in the elderly; elderly patients generally should be observed closely for confusion and oversedation when started on doxepin hydrochloride cream. (See
  PRECAUTIONS - Geriatric Use.)
