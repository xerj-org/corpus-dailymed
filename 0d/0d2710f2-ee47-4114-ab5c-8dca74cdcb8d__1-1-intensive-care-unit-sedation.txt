DailyMed 0d2710f2-ee47-4114-ab5c-8dca74cdcb8d | Dexmedetomidine; Dexmedetomidine in Dextrose | 1.1 Intensive Care Unit Sedation

Dexmedetomidine Injection and Dexmedetomidine in 5% Dextrose Injection is indicated for sedation of initially intubated and mechanically ventilated adult patients during treatment in an intensive care setting. Dexmedetomidine Injection and Dexmedetomidine in 5% Dextrose Injection should be administered by continuous infusion not to exceed 24 hours.
