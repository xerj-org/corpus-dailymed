DailyMed 0d4bff5c-4e3d-459e-aba2-9c4c34b5ba7b | Doxepin Hydrochloride | CONTRAINDICATIONS

Because doxepin hydrochloride has an anticholinergic effect and because significant plasma levels of doxepin are detectable after topical doxepin hydrochloride cream application, the use of doxepin hydrochloride cream is contraindicated in patients with untreated narrow angle glaucoma or a tendency to urinary retention. Doxepin hydrochloride cream is contraindicated in individuals who have shown previous sensitivity to any of its components.
