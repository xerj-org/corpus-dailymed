DailyMed 5b050245-aa03-4e28-e063-6394a90aeabc | Ertapenem | 1.1 Complicated Intra-Abdominal Infections

Ertapenem for injection is indicated for the treatment of adult patients and pediatric patients (3 months of age and older) with complicated intra-abdominal infections due to

  Escherichia coli,

  Clostridium clostridioforme,

  Eubacterium lentum,

  Peptostreptococcusspecies,

  Bacteroides fragilis,

  Bacteroides distasonis,

  Bacteroides ovatus,

  Bacteroides thetaiotaomicron, or

  Bacteroides uniformis.
