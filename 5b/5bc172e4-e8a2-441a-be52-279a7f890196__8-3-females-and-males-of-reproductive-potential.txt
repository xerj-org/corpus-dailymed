DailyMed 5bc172e4-e8a2-441a-be52-279a7f890196 | Inluriyo | 8.3 Females and Males of Reproductive Potential

Refer to the Prescribing Information of abemaciclib when administered in combination with INLURIYO for contraception and infertility information. When used in combination, advise patients to use effective contraception during treatment and for the longest post-treatment duration recommended in the Prescribing Information of the individual products.

                        INLURIYO can cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman [see Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
