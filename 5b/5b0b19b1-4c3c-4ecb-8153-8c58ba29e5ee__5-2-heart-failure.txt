DailyMed 5b0b19b1-4c3c-4ecb-8153-8c58ba29e5ee | METOPROLOL TARTRATE | 5.2 Heart Failure

Worsening cardiac failure may occur during up-titration of metoprolol tartrate. If such symptoms occur, increase diuretics and restore clinical stability before advancing the dose of metoprolol tartrate[see Dosage and Administration (2)]. It may be necessary to lower the dose of metoprolol tartrate or temporarily discontinue it. Such episodes do not preclude subsequent successful titration of metoprolol tartrate.
