DailyMed 5bc172e4-e8a2-441a-be52-279a7f890196 | Inluriyo | 8.2 Lactation

Refer to the Prescribing Information of abemaciclib when administered in combination with INLURIYO for lactation information. When used in combination, advise patients to not breastfeed during treatment and for the longest post-treatment duration recommended in the Prescribing Information of the individual products.
