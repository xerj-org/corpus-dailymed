DailyMed 5b050245-aa03-4e28-e063-6394a90aeabc | Ertapenem | 7.1 Probenecid

Probenecid interferes with the active tubular secretion of ertapenem, resulting in increased plasma concentrations of ertapenem

  [see

   Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]

  . Co-administration of probenecid with ertapenem is not recommended.
