DailyMed 5b050245-aa03-4e28-e063-6394a90aeabc | Ertapenem | 1.3 Community Acquired Pneumonia

Ertapenem for injection is indicated for the treatment of adult patients and pediatric patients (3 months of age and older) with community acquired pneumonia due to

  Streptococcus pneumoniae(penicillin susceptible isolates only) including cases with concurrent bacteremia,

  Haemophilus influenzae(beta-lactamase negative isolates only), or

  Moraxella catarrhalis.
