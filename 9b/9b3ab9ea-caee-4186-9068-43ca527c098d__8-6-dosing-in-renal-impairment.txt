DailyMed 9b3ab9ea-caee-4186-9068-43ca527c098d | Amoxicillin | 8.6 Dosing in Renal Impairment

Amoxicillin is primarily eliminated by the kidney and dosage adjustment is usually required in patients with severe renal impairment (GFR less than 30 mL/min). See

  Dosing in Renal Impairment (

   2.5)

  for specific recommendations in patients with renal impairment.
