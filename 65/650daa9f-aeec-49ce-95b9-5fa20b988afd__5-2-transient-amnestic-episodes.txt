DailyMed 650daa9f-aeec-49ce-95b9-5fa20b988afd | AMELUZ | 5.2 Transient Amnestic Episodes

Transient amnestic episodes have been reported during postmarketing use of AMELUZ in combination with PDT. Inform patients and their caregivers that AMELUZ in combination with PDT may cause transient amnestic episodes. Advise them to contact the healthcare provider if the patient develops amnesia after treatment.
