DailyMed 650daa9f-aeec-49ce-95b9-5fa20b988afd | AMELUZ | Transient Amnestic Episodes

Inform patients that transient amnestic episodes have been reported with use of AMELUZ in combination with photodynamic therapy. Advise patients and their families or caregivers to contact their healthcare provider if memory impairment, confusion, or disorientation is observed [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
