DailyMed 650daa9f-aeec-49ce-95b9-5fa20b988afd | AMELUZ | 5.6 Risk of Bleeding in Patients with Coagulation Disorders

AMELUZ has not been tested on patients with inherited or acquired coagulation disorders. Take special care to avoid bleeding during lesion preparation in such patients [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)
                           ]. Any bleeding must be stopped before application of AMELUZ.
