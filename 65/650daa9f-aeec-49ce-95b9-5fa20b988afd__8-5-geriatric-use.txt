DailyMed 650daa9f-aeec-49ce-95b9-5fa20b988afd | AMELUZ | 8.5 Geriatric Use

Of the 529 subjects exposed to AMELUZ in randomized, multicenter clinical trials, 72% (382/529) of the subjects were 65 years old and over. No overall differences in safety or effectiveness were observed between these subjects and younger adult subjects, but greater sensitivity of some older individuals cannot be ruled out.
