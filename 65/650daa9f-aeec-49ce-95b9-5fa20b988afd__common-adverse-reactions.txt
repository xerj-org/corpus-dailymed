DailyMed 650daa9f-aeec-49ce-95b9-5fa20b988afd | AMELUZ | Common Adverse Reactions

Inform patients that treatment with AMELUZ in combination with PDT may result in adverse reactions which include local skin reactions at the application site such as erythema, pain/burning, irritation, edema, pruritus, exfoliation, induration, scab, and vesicles [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
