DailyMed 650daa9f-aeec-49ce-95b9-5fa20b988afd | AMELUZ | 5.1 Hypersensitivity

Several cases of hypersensitivity were reported during postmarketing use of AMELUZ prior to PDT illumination [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)
                           ]. If hypersensitivity reactions occur, clean the area of skin where AMELUZ was applied and institute appropriate therapy. Inform patients and their caregivers that AMELUZ may cause hypersensitivity, potentially including severe cases (anaphylaxis).
