DailyMed 650daa9f-aeec-49ce-95b9-5fa20b988afd | AMELUZ | 2.2 Recommended Dosage

Apply an approximately 1 mm thick layer of AMELUZ to skin lesion(s). Cover individual lesions (sBCC or AK) or the entire AK field with AMELUZ. Include approximately 5 mm to 10 mm of the surrounding skin. Do not exceed an application area of 60 cm2. Do not use more than 6 grams of AMELUZ (3 tubes) at one time.
