DailyMed 650daa9f-aeec-49ce-95b9-5fa20b988afd | AMELUZ | 1. INDICATIONS AND USAGE

AMELUZ, in combination with photodynamic therapy (PDT) using BF-RhodoLED® or RhodoLED® XL lamp, a narrowband, red light illumination source, is indicated for:

                     lesion-directed and field-directed treatment of actinic keratoses (AKs) of mild-to-moderate severity on the face and scalp in adults.

                     the treatment of superficial basal cell carcinoma (sBCC) in adults.
