DailyMed 0fc94bc2-fa38-439a-a609-1b4130cdacb1 | Hydrochlorothiazide | HOW SUPPLIED

Hydrochlorothiazide Capsules, USP contain a white to off white powder filled in size "4" hard gelatin capsules with opaque light blue colored cap and opaque light blue colored body imprinted "SG" on cap and "146" on body with black ink. They are supplied in bottles of 100 (NDC 69367-219-01), bottles of 500 (NDC 69367-219-05) and the bottles of 1,000 (NDC 69367-219-10).

                  Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.
