DailyMed 4b8d8e84-b5d2-4e56-97ec-4f72971465c5 | QNASL | 8.3 Females and Males of Reproductive Potential

Impairment of fertility was observed in rats and dogs at oral doses of beclomethasone dipropionate corresponding to 500 and 50 times the MRHD for adults on a mg/m2 basis, respectively. [see Nonclinical Toxicology
                           (13.1)].
