                        The primary outcome measure for this study was the serum level of free (unbound) digoxin. The results demonstrated that both products reduced the level of free digoxin in the serum to below the limit of assay quantitation for several hours after Fab administration. Cumulative urinary excretion of digoxin was comparable for both products and exceeded 40% of the administered dose by 24 hours. These results demonstrate that DIGIFAB and Digibind

  ®have equivalent pharmacodynamic effects on the digoxin parameters that are relevant to the treatment of digoxin toxicity.
