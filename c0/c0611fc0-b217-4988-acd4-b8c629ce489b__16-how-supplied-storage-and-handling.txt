Imatinib film-coated tablets are supplied as 100 mg and 400 mg tablets for oral administration. Each 100 mg tablet contains 119.5 mg of imatinib mesylate equivalent to 100 mg of imatinib free base. Each 400 mg tablet contains 478 mg of imatinib mesylate equivalent to 400 mg of imatinib free base.

                     Imatinib Mesylate Tablets, 100 mgare very dark yellow to brownish orange film-coated tablets, round, biconvex with bevelled edges, debossed with '100' on one side, and score on the other side.
