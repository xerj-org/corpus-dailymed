DailyMed c0611fc0-b217-4988-acd4-b8c629ce489b | Imatinib Mesylate | 2.5 Pediatric Patients With Ph+ ALL

The recommended dose of imatinib mesylate tablets to be given in combination with chemotherapy to children with newly diagnosed Ph+ ALL is 340 mg/m

  2/day (not to exceed 600 mg). Imatinib mesylate tablets treatment can be given as a once daily dose.
