DailyMed 798fee33-51ad-45d0-82f3-09289304139f | OLMESARTAN MEDOXOMIL AND HYDROCHLOROTHIAZIDE | 8.6 Renal Impairment

Safety and effectiveness of olmesartan medoxomil and hydrochlorothiazide tablets in patients with severe renal impairment (Cr Cl ≤30 m L/min) have not been established. No dose adjustment is required in patients with mild (Cr Cl 60-90 m L/min) or moderate (Cr Cl 30-60) renal impairment.
