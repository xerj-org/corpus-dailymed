Olmesartan medoxomil and hydrochlorothiazide tablets are supplied as film-coated non-scored tablets: 

                     20 mg/12.5 mg reddish-yellow coloured, round shape film coated tablets, debossed with “A01” on one side and plain on other side.

                      40 mg/12.5 mg reddish-yellow coloured, oval shape film coated tablets, debossed with “A002” on one side and plain on other side.

                     40 mg/25 mg pink coloured, oval shape film coated tablets, debossed with “A003” on one side and plain on other side.
