DailyMed e27c3e09-bc4f-4f01-be05-1c43ac72285d | Nilotinib | 5.6 Hepatotoxicity

Nilotinib may result in hepatotoxicity as measured by elevations in bilirubin, aspartate aminotransferase (AST), alanine aminotransferase (ALT), and alkaline phosphatase. Grade 3-4 elevations of bilirubin, AST, and ALT were reported at a higher frequency in pediatric than in adult patients.
