DailyMed e27c3e09-bc4f-4f01-be05-1c43ac72285d | Nilotinib | 5.7 Electrolyte Abnormalities

The use of nilotinib can cause hypophosphatemia, hypokalemia, hyperkalemia, hypocalcemia, and hyponatremia. Correct electrolyte abnormalities prior to initiating Nilotinib Capsules and during therapy. Monitor these electrolytes periodically during therapy [see Warnings and Precautions (

                              5.12

                           )].
