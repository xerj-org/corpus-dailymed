DailyMed e27c3e09-bc4f-4f01-be05-1c43ac72285d | Nilotinib | 1.2 Adult Patients With Resistant or Intolerant Ph+ CML-CP and CML-AP

Nilotinib Capsules is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with chronic phase and accelerated phase Philadelphia chromosome positive chronic myelogenous leukemia (Ph+ CML) resistant or intolerant to prior therapy that included imatinib.
