DailyMed e27c3e09-bc4f-4f01-be05-1c43ac72285d | Nilotinib | 5.8 Tumor Lysis Syndrome

Tumor lysis syndrome (TLS) cases have been reported in nilotinib treated patients with resistant or intolerant CML. Malignant disease progression, high white blood cell (WBC) counts and/or dehydration were present in the majority of these cases. Due to potential for TLS, maintain adequate hydration and correct uric acid levels prior to initiating therapy with Nilotinib Capsules.
