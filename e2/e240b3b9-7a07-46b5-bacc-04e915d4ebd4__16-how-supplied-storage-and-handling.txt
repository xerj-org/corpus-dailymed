Lanreotide Injection, 120 mg/0.5 mL is a white to pale yellow, semi-solid formulation in a single, sterile, pre-filled, ready-to-use, polypropylene syringe fitted with an automatic safety system, a bromobutyl rubber plunger stopper and a 20 mm attached needle covered by a plastic cap.

                  The pre-filled syringe is placed in a plastic tray, sealed in a laminated pouch and packed in a carton.

                  120 mg/0.5 mL, Sterile, Pre-filled Syringe:                 NDC 70121-2517-5

                     Storage and Handling

                  Store lanreotide injection in the refrigerator at 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).

                  Protect from light.

                  Store in the original package.
