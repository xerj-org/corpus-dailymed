DailyMed e27c3e09-bc4f-4f01-be05-1c43ac72285d | Nilotinib | 2.5 Dosage Modifications for Myelosuppression

Withhold or reduce Nilotinib Capsules dosage for hematological toxicities (neutropenia, thrombocytopenia) that are not related to underlying leukemia (Table 3) [see Warnings and Precautions (

                              5.1

                           )].
