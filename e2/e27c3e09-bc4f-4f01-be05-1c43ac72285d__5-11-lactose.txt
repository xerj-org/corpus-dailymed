DailyMed e27c3e09-bc4f-4f01-be05-1c43ac72285d | Nilotinib | 5.11 Lactose

Since the capsules contain lactose, Nilotinib Capsules is not recommended for patients with rare hereditary problems of galactose intolerance, severe lactase deficiency with a severe degree of intolerance to lactose-containing products, or of glucose-galactose malabsorption.
