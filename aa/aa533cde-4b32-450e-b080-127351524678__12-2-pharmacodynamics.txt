DailyMed aa533cde-4b32-450e-b080-127351524678 | Glimepiride | 12.2 Pharmacodynamics

In healthy subjects, the time to reach maximal effect (minimum blood glucose concentrations) was approximately 2-3 hours after single oral doses of glimepiride. The effects of glimepiride on HbA1c, fasting plasma glucose, and postprandial glucose have been assessed in clinical trials [see Clinical Studies (14)].
