DailyMed aa8fc2ea-3942-453b-bc19-1e4b14ba6926 | Omeprazole | 5.10 Interaction with St. John's Wort or Rifampin

Drugs which induce CYP2C19 or CYP3A4 (such as St. John’s Wort or rifampin) can substantially decrease omeprazole concentrations [see Drug Interactions ()]. Avoid concomitant use of omeprazole with St. John’s Wort or rifampin.
