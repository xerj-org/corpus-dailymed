                     Storage

                  Store Omeprazole Delayed-Release Capsules in a tight container protected from light and moisture. Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
