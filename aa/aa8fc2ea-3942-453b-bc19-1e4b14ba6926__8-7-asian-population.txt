DailyMed aa8fc2ea-3942-453b-bc19-1e4b14ba6926 | Omeprazole | 8.7 Asian Population

In studies of healthy subjects, Asians had approximately a four-fold higher exposure than Caucasians. Dosage reduction of omeprazole to 10 mg once daily is recommended for Asian patients for maintenance of healing of EE [see Dosage and Administration (), Clinical Pharmacology ()].
