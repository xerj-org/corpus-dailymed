DailyMed aad6e18f-04f8-4799-b366-873ec5a50317 | Lactated Ringers | 5.9 Interference of Lactated Ringer’s Injection with Interpretation of Serum Lactate Levels in Patients with Severe Metabolic Acidosis

Administration of Lactated Ringer’s Injection may result in interference with the interpretation of serum lactate levels in patients with severe metabolic acidosis. [see Drug Interactions (7.5)].
