DailyMed aa8fc2ea-3942-453b-bc19-1e4b14ba6926 | Omeprazole | 8.6 Hepatic Impairment

In patients with hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Class A, B, or C) exposure to omeprazole substantially increased compared to healthy subjects. Dosage reduction of omeprazole to 10 mg once daily is recommended for patients with hepatic impairment for maintenance of healing of EE [see Dosage and Administration (), Clinical Pharmacology ()].
