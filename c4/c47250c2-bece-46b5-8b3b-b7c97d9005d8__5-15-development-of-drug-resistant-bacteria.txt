DailyMed c47250c2-bece-46b5-8b3b-b7c97d9005d8 | Ciprofloxacin Hydrochloride | 5.15 Development of Drug Resistant Bacteria

Prescribing ciprofloxacin tablets in the absense of a proven or strongly suspected bacterial infection or a prophylactic indication is unlikely to provide benefit to the patient and increases the risk of the development of drug-resistant bacteria.
