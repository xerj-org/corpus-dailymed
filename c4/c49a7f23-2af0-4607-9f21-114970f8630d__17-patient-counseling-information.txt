Pregnancy: Advise female patients of childbearing age about the consequences of exposure to olmesartan medoxomil during pregnancy. Discuss treatment options with women planning to become pregnant. Tell patients to report pregnancies to their physicians as soon as possible 

  [see

   Warnings and Precautions (5.1)and

   Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].

                     Lactation: Advice nursing women not to breastfeed during treatment with Olmesartan Medoxomil tablets

  [see

   Use in Specific Populations (8.2)].

                     Hyperkalemia: Advice patients not to use potassium supplements or salt substitutes that contain potassium without consulting their healthcare provider

  [see

   Drug Interactions (7.1)].
