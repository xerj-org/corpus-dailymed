DailyMed c4d65f28-983f-42b4-bb23-023ae0fe81b2 | Xadago | 5.4 Dyskinesia

XADAGO may cause dyskinesia or exacerbate pre-existing dyskinesia.

                        In clinical trials, the incidence of dyskinesia was 21% for XADAGO 50 mg, 18% for XADAGO 100 mg, and 9% for placebo.  There was a greater incidence of dyskinesia causing study discontinuation in patients treated with XADAGO 50 mg or 100 mg (1%), compared to placebo (0%)

  [see

   Adverse Reactions (6.1)]

  .

                        Reducing the patient's daily levodopa dosage or the dosage of another dopaminergic drug may mitigate dyskinesia.
