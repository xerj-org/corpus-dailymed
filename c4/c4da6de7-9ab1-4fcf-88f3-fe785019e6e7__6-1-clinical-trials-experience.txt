DailyMed c4da6de7-9ab1-4fcf-88f3-fe785019e6e7 | Estradiol | 6.1 Clinical Trials Experience

Because clinical trials are conducted under widely varying conditions, adverse reaction rates observed in the clinical trials of a drug cannot be directly compared to rates in the clinical trials of another drug and may not reflect the rates observed in practice.

                        There were no clinical trials conducted with estradiol transdermal system. Estradiol transdermal system is bioequivalent to the original formulation of estradiol transdermal system.
