DailyMed c4da6de7-9ab1-4fcf-88f3-fe785019e6e7 | Estradiol | 2.4 Prevention of Postmenopausal Osteoporosis

Start therapy with estradiol transdermal system 0.025 mg per day applied to the skin twice weekly.

                        In women not currently taking oral estrogens or in women switching from another estradiol transdermal therapy, treatment with estradiol transdermal system may be initiated at once. In women who are currently taking oral estrogens, initiate treatment with estradiol transdermal system 1 week after withdrawal of oral hormone therapy, or sooner if menopausal symptoms reappear in less than 1 week.

                        Estradiol transdermal system may be given continuously in a woman who does not have an intact uterus. In a woman with an intact uterus, estradiol transdermal system may be given on a cyclic schedule (for example, 3 weeks on estradiol transdermal system followed by 1 week off estradiol transdermal system).
