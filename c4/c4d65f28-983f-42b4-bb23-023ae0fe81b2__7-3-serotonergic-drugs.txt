DailyMed c4d65f28-983f-42b4-bb23-023ae0fe81b2 | Xadago | 7.3 Serotonergic Drugs

Concomitant use of XADAGO with SNRIs; triazolopyridine, tricyclic or tetracyclic antidepressants; cyclobenzaprine (a skeletal muscle relaxant that is a tricyclic antidepressant derivative); or St. John's wort is contraindicated

  [see

   Warnings and Precautions (5.2)]

  . At least 14 days should elapse between discontinuation of XADAGO and initiation of treatment with these drugs.

                        Monitor patients for symptoms of serotonin syndrome if SSRIs are used by patients treated with XADAGO

  [see

   Warnings and Precautions (5.2)]
