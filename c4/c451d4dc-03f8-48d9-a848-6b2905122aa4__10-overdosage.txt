DailyMed c451d4dc-03f8-48d9-a848-6b2905122aa4 | sugammadex | 10 OVERDOSAGE

In premarketing clinical trials, one case of accidental overdose with 40 mg/kg Sugammadex Injection was reported without significant effects.

                  Sugammadex Injection can be removed using hemodialysis with a high-flux filter, but not with a low-flux filter. Based upon clinical studies, Sugammadex Injection concentrations in plasma are reduced with a high-flux filter by about 70% after a 3- to 6-hour dialysis session.
