Olmesartan medoxomil tablets, USP are supplied as;

                  5 mg - white to off white, round, film-coated tablets debossed with 'O' on one side and plain on other side containing 5 mg of olmesartan medoxomil, USP.

                  20 mg - white to off white, round, film-coated tablets debossed with 'OLM' on one side and '20' on other side containing 20 mg of olmesartan medoxomil, USP.

                  40 mg - white to off white, oval shaped, film-coated tablets debossed with 'OLM' on one side and '40' on other side containing 40 mg of olmesartan medoxomil, USP.

                     Storage

  Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

  Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
