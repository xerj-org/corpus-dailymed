Sugammadex Injection is a sterile, clear, colorless to slightly yellow-brown, non-pyrogenic aqueous solution intended for intravenous infusion. Sugammadex Injection is available as follows:

                     200 mg/2 mL (100 mg/mL) in a single-dose vial for bolus injection

                     500 mg/5 mL (100 mg/mL), in a single-dose vial for bolus injection
