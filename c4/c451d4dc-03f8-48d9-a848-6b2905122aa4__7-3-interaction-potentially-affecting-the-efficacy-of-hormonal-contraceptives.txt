DailyMed c451d4dc-03f8-48d9-a848-6b2905122aa4 | sugammadex | 7.3 Interaction Potentially Affecting the Efficacy of Hormonal Contraceptives

In vitrobinding studies indicate that Sugammadex Injection may bind to progestogen, thereby decreasing progestogen exposure. Therefore, the administration of a bolus dose of Sugammadex Injection is considered to be equivalent to missing dose(s) of

  oralcontraceptives containing an estrogen or progestogen. If an oral contraceptive is taken on the same day that Sugammadex Injection is administered, the patient must use an additional, non-hormonal contraceptive method or back-up method of contraception (such as condoms and spermicides) for the next 7 days.

                        In the case of

  non-oralhormonal contraceptives, the patient must use an additional, non-hormonal contraceptive method or back-up method of contraception (such as condoms and spermicides) for the next 7 days.
