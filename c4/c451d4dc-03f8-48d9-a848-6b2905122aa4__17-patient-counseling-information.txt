DailyMed c451d4dc-03f8-48d9-a848-6b2905122aa4 | sugammadex | 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Advise females of reproductive potential using hormonal contraceptives that Sugammadex Injection may reduce the contraceptive effect

   .Instruct females to use an additional, non-hormonal method of contraception for the next 7 days following Sugammadex Injection administration

   [see Drug Interactions (7.3)].
