                  Ciprofloxacin Tablets, USP are film coated and available in 250 mg, 500 mg and 750 mg (ciprofloxacin equivalent) strengths. Ciprofloxacin tablets are white to slightly yellowish. The inactive ingredients are colloidal silicon dioxide, pregelatinized starch (corn), magnesium stearate, sodium starch glycolate (potato).

                  The 500 mg and 750 mg contains polyethylene glycol, polyvinyl alcohol (partially hydrolyzed), titanium dioxide and talc.
