DailyMed c49a7f23-2af0-4607-9f21-114970f8630d | OLMESARTAN MEDOXOMIL | 8.8 Black Patients

The antihypertensive effect of olmesartan medoxomil tablets was smaller in black patients (usually a low-renin population), as has been seen with ACE inhibitors, beta-blockers and other angiotensin receptor blockers.
