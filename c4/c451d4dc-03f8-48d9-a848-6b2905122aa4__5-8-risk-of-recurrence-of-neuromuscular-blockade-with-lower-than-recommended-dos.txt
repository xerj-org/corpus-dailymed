DailyMed c451d4dc-03f8-48d9-a848-6b2905122aa4 | sugammadex | 5.8 Risk of Recurrence of Neuromuscular Blockade with Lower Than Recommended Dosing

The use of lower than recommended doses of Sugammadex Injection may lead to an increased risk of recurrence of neuromuscular blockade after initial reversal and is not recommended

  [see Dosage and Administration (2.2), Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
