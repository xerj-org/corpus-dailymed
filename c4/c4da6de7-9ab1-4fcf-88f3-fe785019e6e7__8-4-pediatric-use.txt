DailyMed c4da6de7-9ab1-4fcf-88f3-fe785019e6e7 | Estradiol | 8.4 Pediatric Use

Estradiol transdermal system is not indicated for use in pediatric patients. Clinical studies have not been conducted in the pediatric population.

                        If estrogen is administered to patients whose bone growth is not complete, periodic monitoring of bone maturation and effects on epiphyseal centers is recommended during estrogen administration.
