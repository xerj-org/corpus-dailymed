DailyMed c451d4dc-03f8-48d9-a848-6b2905122aa4 | sugammadex | 7.4 Interference with Laboratory Tests

Sugammadex Injection may interfere with the serum progesterone assay. Interference with this test was observed at sugammadex plasma concentrations of 100 mcg/mL, which may be observed for up to 30 minutes after a 16 mg/kg dose.
