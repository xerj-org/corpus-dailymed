DailyMed b7491a1a-570e-40ea-b65b-f956c9e284a6 | Rosuvastatin Calcium | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Rosuvastatin tablets are contraindicated in the following conditions:

                     Acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis

   [see

    Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].

                     Hypersensitivity to rosuvastatin or any excipients in rosuvastatin tablets. Hypersensitivity reactions including rash, pruritus, urticaria, and angioedema have been reported with rosuvastatin

   [see

    Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
