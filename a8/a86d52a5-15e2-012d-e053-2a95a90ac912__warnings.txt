Allergy alert:

                     Do not use if you are allergic to omeprazole

                     omeprazole may cause severe skin reactions.

                  Symptoms may include:

                     skin reddening

                     blisters

                     rash

                  If an allergic reaction occurs, stop use and seek medical help right away.
