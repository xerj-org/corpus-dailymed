DailyMed eb02c52d-907c-43fb-aa8f-2f378a9087e7 | Chloroquine Phosphate | HOW SUPPLIED

Chloroquine Phosphate Tablets USP, 250 mg, equivalent to 150 mg of chloroquine base are supplied as white to off-white, round tablets, single scored on one side and debossed with “0115” over “2790” on the other side.

                  They are available as follows:

                  Bottles of 50:              NDC 0115-2790-06

                  Bottles of 500:            NDC 0115-2790-02

                  Chloroquine Phosphate Tablets USP, 500 mg, equivalent to 300 mg of chloroquine base are supplied as white to off-white, round tablets, debossed with "7010" on one side and plain on the other side.

                  They are available as follows:

                  Bottles of 25:              NDC 0115-7010-09

                  Bottles of 100:            NDC 0115-7010-01

                  Bottles of 500:            NDC 0115-7010-02

                  Dispense in tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.

                  Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature]
