DailyMed ebf30894-41cf-480c-8bc3-56f592a13813 | Herceptin Hylecta | 1.1 Adjuvant Breast Cancer

HERCEPTIN HYLECTA is indicated for adjuvant treatment of adults with HER2 overexpressing node positive or node negative (ER/PR negative or with one high risk feature [see Clinical Studies (14.1)]) breast cancer:

                           as part of a treatment regimen consisting of doxorubicin, cyclophosphamide, and either paclitaxel or docetaxel

                           as part of a treatment regimen with docetaxel and carboplatin

                           as a single agent following multi-modality anthracycline based therapy.

                        Select patients for therapy based on an FDA-authorized companion diagnostic for trastuzumab [see Dosage and Administration (2.2)].
