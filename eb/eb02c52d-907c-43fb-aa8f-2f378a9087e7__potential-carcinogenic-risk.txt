DailyMed eb02c52d-907c-43fb-aa8f-2f378a9087e7 | Chloroquine Phosphate | Potential Carcinogenic Risk

Experimental data showed a potential risk of inducing gene mutations. There is insufficient evidence regarding the carcinogenicity of chloroquine in experimental animals (see
                              PRECAUTIONS, Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility
                           ). In humans, there are insufficient data to rule out an increased risk of cancer in patients receiving long-term treatment.
