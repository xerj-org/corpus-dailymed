DailyMed ebf30894-41cf-480c-8bc3-56f592a13813 | Herceptin Hylecta | 1.2 Metastatic Breast Cancer

HERCEPTIN HYLECTA is indicated in adults:

                           In combination with paclitaxel for first-line treatment of HER2-overexpressing metastatic breast cancer

                           As a single agent for treatment of HER2-overexpressing breast cancer in patients who have received one or more chemotherapy regimens for metastatic disease.

                        Select patients for therapy based on an FDA-authorized companion diagnostic for trastuzumab [see Dosage and Administration (2.2)].
