Complete blood cell counts should be checked periodically if patients are given prolonged therapy (see
                              ADVERSE REACTIONS
                           ).

                        Chloroquine may cause hemolysis in glucose-6 phosphate dehydrogenase (G-6-PD) deficiency. Blood monitoring may be needed as hemolytic anemia may occur, in particular in association with other drugs that cause hemolysis (see
                              ADVERSE REACTIONS
                           ).
