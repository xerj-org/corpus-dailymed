DailyMed 1bda03a2-6c7d-4c9e-b98a-e5e01bebb3b1 | Amoxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium | 2.4 Patients with Renal Impairment

Patients with impaired renal function do not generally require a reduction in dose unless the impairment is severe. Renal impairment patients with a glomerular filtration rate (GFR) of less than 30 mL/min should

  NOTreceive the 875 mg dose (based on the amoxicillin component) of  amoxicillin and clavulanate potassium tablet. See dosing regimens in patients with severe renal impairment provided in Table 4.
