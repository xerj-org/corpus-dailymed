DailyMed 1bda03a2-6c7d-4c9e-b98a-e5e01bebb3b1 | Amoxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium | 4.2 Cholestatic Jaundice/Hepatic Dysfunction

Amoxicillin and clavulanate potassium tablets are contraindicated in patients with a previous history of cholestatic jaundice/hepatic dysfunction associated with amoxicillin and clavulanate potassium tablets.
