DailyMed dc24aa15-330c-4698-872f-159d7e582b4b | Mycophenolic Acid | 2.3 Administration

Mycophenolic acid delayed release tablets should be taken on an empty stomach, 1 hour before or 2 hours after food intake [see

                              Clinical Pharmacology (

    12.3)

                           ].

                        Mycophenolic acid delayed release tablets should not be crushed, chewed, or cut prior to ingesting. The tablets should be swallowed whole in order to maintain the integrity of the enteric coating.

                        Pediatric patients with a BSA of 1.19 to 1.58 m

  2may be dosed either with three mycophenolic acid delayed release 180 mg tablets, or one 180 mg tablet plus one 360 mg tablet twice daily (1,080 mg daily dose). Patients with a BSA of >1.58 m

  2may be dosed either with four mycophenolic acid delayed release 180 mg tablets, or two mycophenolic acid delayed release 360 mg tablets twice daily (1,440 mg daily dose). Pediatric doses for patients with BSA <1.19 m

  2cannot be accurately administered using currently available formulations of mycophenolic acid delayed release tablets.
