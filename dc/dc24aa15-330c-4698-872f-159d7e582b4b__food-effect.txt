DailyMed dc24aa15-330c-4698-872f-159d7e582b4b | Mycophenolic Acid | Food Effect

Compared to the fasting state, administration of mycophenolic acid delayed release 720 mg with a high-fat meal (55 g fat, 1000 calories) had no effect on the systemic exposure (AUC) of MPA. However, there was a 33% decrease in the maximal concentration (C

  max), a 3.5-hour delay in the T

  lag(range, -6 to 18 hours), and 5.0-hour delay in the T

  max(range, -9 to 20 hours) of MPA. To avoid the variability in MPA absorption between doses, mycophenolic acid delayed release should be taken on an empty stomach [see
