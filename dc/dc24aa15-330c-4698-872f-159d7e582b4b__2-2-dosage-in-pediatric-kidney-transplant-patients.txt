DailyMed dc24aa15-330c-4698-872f-159d7e582b4b | Mycophenolic Acid | 2.2 Dosage in Pediatric Kidney Transplant Patients

The recommended dose of mycophenolic acid delayed release tablets in conversion (at least 6 months post-transplant) pediatric patients age 5 years and older is 400 mg/m

  2body surface area (BSA) administered twice daily (up to a maximum dose of 720 mg administered twice daily).
