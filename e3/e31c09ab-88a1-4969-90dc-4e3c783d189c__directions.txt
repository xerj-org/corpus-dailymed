DailyMed e31c09ab-88a1-4969-90dc-4e3c783d189c | OZIPCO IVERMECTIN | Directions

Important: Read warnings before use.

                  Children 6 months of age to under 12 years of age: an adult should supervise use.

                  Adults and children 6 months of age and over: inspect all household members for lice and/or nits (eggs).

                  Hair and scalp must be dry before applying product. Apply product directly to dry hair and scalp. Completely cover scalp and hair closest to the scalp first, then apply outward toward the ends. Use only the amount needed to completely cover hair and scalp, up to 1 entire tube. Rub product throughout hair.

                  Allow product to stay on hair and scalp for 10 minutes after it has been applied. After 10 minutes, rinse completely using only water.

                  After rinsing, dry and style as usual. Wait 24 hours before applying shampoo.

                  Wash hands after applying product. Nit combing is not necessary. This is a single-use product. Discard tube after use. If infestation continues, see a doctor for other treatments.
