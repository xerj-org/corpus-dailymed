DailyMed 4fe97dac-d952-40f5-9c6a-67cda048ad8f | Gamunex-C | 1.2 Idiopathic Thrombocytopenic Purpura (ITP)

GAMUNEX-C FlexBag is indicated for the treatment of adults and children with Idiopathic Thrombocytopenic Purpura to raise platelet counts to prevent bleeding or to allow a patient with ITP to undergo surgery.
