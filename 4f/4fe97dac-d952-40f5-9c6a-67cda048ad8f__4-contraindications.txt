DailyMed 4fe97dac-d952-40f5-9c6a-67cda048ad8f | Gamunex-C | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

GAMUNEX-C FlexBag is contraindicated in:

                     Patients with known hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylactic or severe systemic reaction, to the administration of human immune globulin. 

                     IgA deficient patients with antibodies to IgA and a history of hypersensitivity.
