DailyMed 4fe97dac-d952-40f5-9c6a-67cda048ad8f | Gamunex-C | 10 OVERDOSAGE

With intravenous administration, overdose of GAMUNEX-C FlexBag may lead to fluid overload and hyperviscosity. Patients at risk of complications of fluid overload and hyperviscosity include elderly patients and those with cardiac renal impairment.
