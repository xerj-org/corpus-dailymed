DailyMed a798faf5-cd97-4e35-a700-94c372978c46 | Piperacillin and Tazobactam | 7.3 Vancomycin

Studies have detected an increased incidence of acute kidney injury in patients concomitantly administered piperacillin and tazobactam and vancomycin as compared to vancomycin alone [see Warnings and Precautions (

                              5.7

                           )].

                        Monitor kidney function in patients concomitantly administered with piperacillin and tazobactam and vancomycin.

                        No pharmacokinetic interactions have been noted between piperacillin and tazobactam and vancomycin.
