DailyMed a7b8ea07-e926-f0a5-c20d-92f23ed350d0 | Hydrocodone Bitartrate | 5.13 Increased Risk of Seizures in Patients with Seizure Disorders

The hydrocodone in hydrocodone bitartrate extended-release capsules may increase the frequency of seizures in patients with seizure disorders, and may increase the risk occurring in other clinical settings associated with seizures. Regularly evaluate patients with a history of seizure disorders for worsened seizure control during hydrocodone bitartrate extended-release capsules therapy.
