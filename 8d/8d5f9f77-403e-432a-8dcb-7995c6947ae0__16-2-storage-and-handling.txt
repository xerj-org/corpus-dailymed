DailyMed 8d5f9f77-403e-432a-8dcb-7995c6947ae0 | Topiramate | 16.2 Storage and Handling

Topiramate Extended-Release Capsules, USP should be stored at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature]. Protect from moisture.

                        Keep out of reach of children.

                        Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP with a child-resistant closure.
