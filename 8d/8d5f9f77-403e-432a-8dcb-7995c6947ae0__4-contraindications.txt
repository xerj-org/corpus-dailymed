DailyMed 8d5f9f77-403e-432a-8dcb-7995c6947ae0 | Topiramate | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Topiramate Extended-Release Capsules are contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity reaction to topiramate, Topiramate Extended-Release Capsules, or any of the inactive ingredients of Topiramate Extended-Release Capsules. Anaphylaxis and angioedema have occurred with topiramate [see Warnings and Precautions (5.13)].
