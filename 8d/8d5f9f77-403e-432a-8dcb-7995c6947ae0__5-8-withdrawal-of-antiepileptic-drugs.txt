DailyMed 8d5f9f77-403e-432a-8dcb-7995c6947ae0 | Topiramate | 5.8 Withdrawal of Antiepileptic Drugs

In patients with or without a history of seizures or epilepsy, antiepileptic drugs, including topiramate extended-release capsules, should be gradually withdrawn to minimize the potential for seizures or increased seizure frequency [see Clinical Studies (14)]. In situations where rapid withdrawal of topiramate extended-release capsules is medically required, appropriate monitoring is recommended.
