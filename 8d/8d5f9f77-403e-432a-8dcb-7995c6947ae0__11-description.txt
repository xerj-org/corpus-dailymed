Topiramate, USP, is a sulfamate-substituted monosaccharide. Topiramate Extended-Release Capsules, USP are available as 25 mg, 50 mg, 100 mg, 150 mg, and 200 mg capsules for oral administration as whole capsules or opened and sprinkled onto a spoonful of soft food.

                  Topiramate, USP is a white to off-white powder. Topiramate, USP is freely soluble in polar organic solvents such as acetonitrile and acetone; and very slightly soluble to practically insoluble in non-polar organic solvents such as hexanes. Topiramate, USP has the molecular formula C12H21NO8S and a molecular weight of 339.4.                   Topiramate Extended-Release Capsules, USP contain beads of topiramate, USP in a capsule. The inactive ingredients are ethylcellulose, hypromellose, microcrystalline cellulose, povidone and talc.

                  In addition, the capsule shell contains ferric oxide red (25 mg and 200 mg), ferric oxide yellow (50 mg and 150 mg), ferrosoferric oxide (100 mg only), hypromellose and titanium dioxide.

                  The capsule is imprinted with black ink containing ferrosoferric oxide, potassium hydroxide and shellac.

                  FDA approved dissolution test specifications differ from the USP.
