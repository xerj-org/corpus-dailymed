DailyMed 4dd36cf5-8f73-404a-8b1d-3bd53bd90c25 | PERCOCET | Risks of Driving and Operating Machinery

PERCOCET may impair the mental or physical abilities needed to perform potentially hazardous activities such as driving a car or operating machinery.  Warn patients not to drive or operate dangerous machinery unless they are tolerant to the effects of PERCOCET and know how they will react to the medication [see PRECAUTIONS; Information for Patients/Caregivers].
