DailyMed 4ddf5a4d-8e84-419d-8e3a-c91b85501885 | Meloxicam | 2.3 Rheumatoid Arthritis

For the relief of the signs and symptoms of rheumatoid arthritis, the recommended starting and maintenance oral dose of meloxicam oral suspension is 7.5 mg once daily. Some patients may receive additional benefit by increasing the dose to 15 mg once daily.
