DailyMed 4ddf5a4d-8e84-419d-8e3a-c91b85501885 | Meloxicam | 5.9 Serious Skin Reactions

NSAIDs, including meloxicam, can cause serious skin adverse reactions such as exfoliative dermatitis, Stevens-Johnson Syndrome (SJS), and toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), which can be fatal. NSAIDs can also cause fixed drug eruption (FDE). FDE may present as a more severe variant known as generalized bullous fixed drug eruption (GBFDE), which can be life-threatening. These serious events may occur without warning. Inform patients about the signs and symptoms of serious skin reactions, and to discontinue the use of meloxicam oral suspension at the first appearance of skin rash or any other sign of hypersensitivity. meloxicam oral suspension is contraindicated in patients with previous serious skin reactions to NSAIDs [see Contraindications (4)].
