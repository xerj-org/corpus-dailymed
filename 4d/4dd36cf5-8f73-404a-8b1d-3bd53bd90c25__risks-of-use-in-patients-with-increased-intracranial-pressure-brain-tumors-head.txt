DailyMed 4dd36cf5-8f73-404a-8b1d-3bd53bd90c25 | PERCOCET | Risks of Use in Patients with Increased Intracranial Pressure, Brain Tumors, Head Injury, or Impaired Consciousness

In patients who may be susceptible to the intracranial effects of CO2 retention (e.g., those with evidence of increased intracranial pressure or brain tumors), PERCOCET may reduce respiratory drive, and the resultant CO2 retention can further increase intracranial pressure. Monitor such patients for signs of sedation and respiratory depression, particularly when initiating therapy with PERCOCET. 

                        Opioids may also obscure the clinical course in a patient with a head injury.  Avoid the use of PERCOCET in patients with impaired consciousness or coma.
