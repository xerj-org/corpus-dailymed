                  Store tablets between 15° and 30°C (59° and 86°F).

                     PAXIL (paroxetine) oral suspension

                  10 mg/5 mL in bottles containing 250 mL of paroxetine (orange color and flavor) (NDC 60505-0402-5). The oral suspension is not currently marketed.

                  Store oral suspension at or below 25°C (77°F).
