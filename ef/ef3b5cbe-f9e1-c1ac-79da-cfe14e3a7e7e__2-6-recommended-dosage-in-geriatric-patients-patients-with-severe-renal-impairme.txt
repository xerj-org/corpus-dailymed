DailyMed ef3b5cbe-f9e1-c1ac-79da-cfe14e3a7e7e | PAXIL | 2.6 Recommended Dosage in Geriatric Patients, Patients with Severe Renal Impairment, and Patients with Severe Hepatic Impairment

The recommended starting PAXIL dosage is 10 mg/day in geriatric patients, patients with severe renal impairment, and patients with severe hepatic impairment. The maximum recommended dosage in these patients is 40 mg/day.
