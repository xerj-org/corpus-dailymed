NOT FOR USE IN KITTENS LESS THAN 6 WEEKS OF AGE OR WEIGHING LESS THAN 2 LBS.

                  Consult a veterinarian before using in severely debilitated, pregnant, or nursing cats.

                  For oral use in cats and kittens only. Not for human use. Keep out of reach of children. Wash hands thoroughly after handling tablets.
