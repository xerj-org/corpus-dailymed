BROAD SPECTRUM CAT DEWORMER

                  (Praziquantel / Pyrantel Pamoate Tablets)

                  For Cats & Kittens Only

                  CHICKEN FLAVOR

                  Praziquantel: 18.2 mg. Pyrantel Pamoate: 72.6 mg.

                  For the removal of common gastrointestinal parasites in cats and kittens, including tapeworms, roundworms, and hookworms.

                  NOT FOR USE IN KITTENS LESS THAN 6 WEEKS OF AGE OR WEIGHING LESS THAN 2 LBS.

                  For oral use in cats and kittens only. Not for human use. Keep out of reach of children.

                  70 SCORED TABLETS PER BOTTLE. NDC 86274-002-01.
