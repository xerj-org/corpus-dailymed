DailyMed ef3b5cbe-f9e1-c1ac-79da-cfe14e3a7e7e | PAXIL | 8.7 Hepatic Impairment

Compared to those without hepatic impairment (HI), paroxetine plasma concentrations in patients with severe hepatic impairment (HI) were increased, which may increase the risk of paroxetine-associated adverse reactions.

                        The recommended starting and maximum dosage of PAXIL is lower in patients with severe HI compared to those without HI [see Dosage and Administration (2.5) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].  The recommended dosage in patients with mild or moderate HI is the same as in those without HI.
