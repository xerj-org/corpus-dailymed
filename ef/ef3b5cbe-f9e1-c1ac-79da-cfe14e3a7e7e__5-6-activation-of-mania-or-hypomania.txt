DailyMed ef3b5cbe-f9e1-c1ac-79da-cfe14e3a7e7e | PAXIL | 5.6 Activation of Mania or Hypomania

In patients with bipolar disorder, treating a depressive episode with PAXIL or another antidepressant may precipitate a mixed/manic episode. During controlled clinical trials of PAXIL for unipolar depression, hypomania or mania occurred in approximately 1% of PAXIL-treated patients compared to 1.1% of active-control and 0.3% of placebo-treated patients.

                        Prior to initiating treatment with PAXIL, screen patients for any personal or family history of bipolar disorder, mania, or hypomania.
