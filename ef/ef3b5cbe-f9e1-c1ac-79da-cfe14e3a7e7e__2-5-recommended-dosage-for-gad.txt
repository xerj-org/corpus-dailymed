DailyMed ef3b5cbe-f9e1-c1ac-79da-cfe14e3a7e7e | PAXIL | 2.5 Recommended Dosage for GAD

The recommended starting PAXIL dosage in adult patients with generalized anxiety disorder (GAD) is 10 mg once daily. After one week, increase the PAXIL dosage to 20 mg once daily. In patients with an inadequate response, may increase the PAXIL dosage by 10 mg/day at weekly intervals (e.g., after 1 week may increase the PAXIL dosage from 20 mg/day to 30 mg/day), depending on tolerability, up to a maximum of 50 mg/day.  

                        In clinical trials the effectiveness of PAXIL in the treatment GAD was demonstrated in patients dosed in a range of 20 mg to 50 mg once daily (after titration). There is not sufficient evidence to suggest a greater benefit with a PAXIL dosage  higher than 20 mg once daily [see Clinical Studies (14.5)].
