DailyMed ef4f424f-bf80-47b1-9ab7-75c34b4838bb | Broad Spectrum Cat Dewormer | Indications and Usage

For the removal of common gastrointestinal parasites in cats and kittens:

                  Tapeworms: Dipylidium caninum, Taenia taeniaeformis, Echinococcus multilocularis

                  Roundworms: Toxocara cati, Toxascaris leonina

                  Hookworms: Ancylostoma tubaeforme, Ancylostoma braziliense
