DailyMed ef3b5cbe-f9e1-c1ac-79da-cfe14e3a7e7e | PAXIL | 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

PAXIL is indicated in adults for the treatment of:

                     Major depressive disorder (MDD) in adults

                     Obsessive compulsive disorder (OCD) in adults

                     Panic disorder (PD) in adults

                     Social anxiety disorder (SAD) in adults

                     Generalized anxiety disorder (GAD) in adults

                     Posttraumatic stress disorder (PTSD) in adults
