DailyMed 0ef9de1a-b786-4b6c-a250-3f5ac532b16a | METFORMIN HYDROCHLORIDE | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Metformin hydrochloride extended-release tablets are contraindicated in patients with:

                        •

Severe renal impairment (eGFR below 30 mL/minute/1.73 m2) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

                        •

Known hypersensitivity to metformin.

                        •

Acute or chronic metabolic acidosis, including diabetic ketoacidosis, with or without coma.
