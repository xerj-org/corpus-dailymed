Cyclophosphamide Capsules, USP

                     25 mg, supplied as a blue/blue hard gelatin capsule with “NSP 25” printed in black ink
                     on the cap and body containing a white to off-white powder.

NDC 70301-2013-1: Bottle of 100 Capsules

                     50 mg, supplied as a blue/blue hard gelatin capsule with “NSP 50” printed in black ink
                     on the cap and body containing a white to off-white powder.

NDC 70301-2015-1: Bottle of 100 Capsules

                     Storage

                  Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); with excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

                   Cyclophosphamide is an antineoplastic product. Follow special handling and disposal procedures.
