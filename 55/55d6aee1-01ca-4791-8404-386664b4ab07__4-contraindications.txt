DailyMed 55d6aee1-01ca-4791-8404-386664b4ab07 | Olmesartan Medoxomil and Hydrochlorothiazide | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Olmesartan medoxomil and hydrochlorothiazide tablets are contraindicated:

                     In patients with hypersensitivity to any component of olmesartan medoxomil and hydrochlorothiazide tablets

   [see Adverse Reactions (

    6.1,

    6.2)]

                     In patients with anuria

   [see Warnings and Precautions (

    5.3) and Adverse Reactions (

    6.1)]

                     For co-administration with aliskiren in patients with diabetes

   [see Drug Interactions (

    7.4)].
