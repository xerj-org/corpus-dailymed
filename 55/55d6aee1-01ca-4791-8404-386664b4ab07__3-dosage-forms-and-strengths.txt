Olmesartan medoxomil and hydrochlorothiazide tablets are supplied as film-coated, non-scored tablets:

                     20 mg/12.5 mg beige, beveled edge round, film-coated tablets, debossed with "OLH" on one side and plain on other side

                     40 mg/12.5 mg beige, oval shaped, film-coated tablets debossed with "OLH" on one side and "12.5" on other side

                     40 mg/25 mg pink, oval shaped, film-coated tablets, debossed with "OLH" on one side and "25" on other side
