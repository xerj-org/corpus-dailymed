Diclofenac sodium topical solution 1.5% is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug, available as a clear, colorless to faintly pink-orange solution for topical application.

                  Diclofenac sodium topical solution contains 1.5% w/w diclofenac sodium, USP, a benzeneacetic acid derivative that is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID), designated chemically as 2-[(2,6-dichlorophenyl)amino]-benzeneacetic acid, monosodium salt. It is a white to off-white crystalline powder, hygroscopic, and odorless that is freely soluble in methanol, soluble in alcohol, and sparingly soluble in water. The molecular weight is 318.13.                   Each 1 mL of solution contains 16.05 mg of diclofenac sodium, USP. The inactive ingredients in diclofenac sodium topical solution include: alcohol, dimethyl sulfoxide USP (DMSO, 45.5% w/w), glycerin, propylene glycol, and purified water.
