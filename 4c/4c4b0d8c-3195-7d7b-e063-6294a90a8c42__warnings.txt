Liver warning: This product contains acetaminophen. Severe liver damage may occur if you take:

                     more than 6 tablets in 24 hours, which is the maximum daily amount

                     with other drugs containing acetaminophen

                     3 or more alcoholic drinks every day while using this product

                     Caffeine warning: The recommended dose of this product contains about as much caffeine as a cup of coffee. Limit the use of caffeine-containing metications, foods, or beverages while taking this product because too much cafeine may cause nervousness, irritability, sleeplessness, and, occasionally, rapid heartbeat.
