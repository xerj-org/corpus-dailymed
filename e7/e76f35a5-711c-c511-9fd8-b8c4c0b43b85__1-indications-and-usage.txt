DailyMed e76f35a5-711c-c511-9fd8-b8c4c0b43b85 | Deflazacort Oral Suspension | 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Deflazacort oral suspension is indicated for the treatment of Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD) in patients 5 years of age and older.

                     Additional pediatric use information is approved for PTC Therapeutics, Inc.'s Emflaza
                              ®

                         (deflazacort) oral suspension. However, due to PTC Therapeutics, Inc.'s marketing exclusivity rights, this drug product is not labeled with that information.
