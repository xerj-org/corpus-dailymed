DailyMed e76f35a5-711c-c511-9fd8-b8c4c0b43b85 | Deflazacort Oral Suspension | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Deflazacort oral suspension is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to deflazacort or to any of the inactive ingredients. Instances of hypersensitivity, including anaphylaxis, have occurred in patients receiving corticosteroid therapy [see Warnings and Precautions (

                        5.15

                     ) and Adverse Reactions (

                        6.2

                     )].
