                           Switching from Tofacitinib Tablets to Tofacitinib Extended-Release Tablets

  Patients treated with tofacitinib tablets:

                           5 mg twice daily may be switched to tofacitinib extended-release tablets 11 mg once daily the day following the last dose of tofacitinib tablets 5 mg.

                           10 mg twice daily may be switched to tofacitinib extended-release tablets 22 mg once daily the day following the last dose of tofacitinib tablets 10 mg.
