DailyMed ffa6819b-23a4-478b-8fb7-cc90e8142fc5 | TOFACITINIB | 5.7 Hypersensitivity Reactions

Hypersensitivity reactions may occur with tofacitinib tablets. Reactions such as angioedema and urticaria that may reflect drug hypersensitivity have been observed in patients receiving tofacitinib tablets. Some events were serious. If a serious hypersensitivity reaction occurs, promptly discontinue tofacitinib tablets while evaluating the potential cause or causes of the reaction
