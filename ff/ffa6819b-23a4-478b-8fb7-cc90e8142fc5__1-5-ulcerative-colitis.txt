DailyMed ffa6819b-23a4-478b-8fb7-cc90e8142fc5 | TOFACITINIB | 1.5 Ulcerative Colitis

Tofacitinib tablets are indicated for the treatment of adult patients with moderately to severely active ulcerative colitis (UC), who have an inadequate response or intolerance to one or more TNF blockers.

                           Limitations of Use

                        Use of tofacitinib tablets in combination with biological therapies for UC or with potent immunosuppressants such as azathioprine and cyclosporine is not recommended.
