DailyMed ff660787-880f-4f2b-baac-fd2dbfcd79c2 | Selexipag; Selexipag TITRATION PACK | 10 OVERDOSAGE

Isolated cases of overdose in adults with selexipag tablets up to 3,200 mcg were reported. Mild, transient nausea was the only reported consequence. In the event of overdose, supportive measures must be taken as required. Dialysis is unlikely to be effective because selexipag and its active metabolite are highly protein-bound.
