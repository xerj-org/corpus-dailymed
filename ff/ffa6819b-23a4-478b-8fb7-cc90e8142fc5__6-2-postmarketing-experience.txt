DailyMed ffa6819b-23a4-478b-8fb7-cc90e8142fc5 | TOFACITINIB | 6.2 Postmarketing Experience

The following adverse reactions have been identified during post-approval use of tofacitinib. Because these reactions are reported voluntarily from a population of uncertain size, it is not always possible to reliably estimate their frequency or establish a causal relationship to drug exposure.

                           Immune system disorders:Drug hypersensitivity (events such as angioedema and urticaria have been observed)

                           Infections and infestations:Progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy

                           Metabolism and nutrition disorders:Hypoglycemia

                           Skin and subcutaneous tissue disorders:Acne
