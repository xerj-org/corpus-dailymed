DailyMed ffa6819b-23a4-478b-8fb7-cc90e8142fc5 | TOFACITINIB | 2.2 Important Administration Instructions

Tofacitinib extended-release tablets is not substitutable with tofacitinib tablets. Switching between tofacitinib tablets and tofacitinib extended-release tablets should be made by the healthcare provider.

                           Dose interruption is recommended for management of lymphopenia, neutropenia, and anemia

   [see

    Warnings and Precautions (5.9)
                              and

                                 Adverse Reactions (6.1)]

   .

                           Interrupt use of tofacitinib tablets if a patient develops a serious infection until the infection is controlled

   [see

    Warnings and Precautions (5.1)]

   .

                           Take tofacitinib tablets with or without food

   [see

    Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]

   .
