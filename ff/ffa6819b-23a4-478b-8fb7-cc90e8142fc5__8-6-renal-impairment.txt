Moderate and Severe Renal Impairment

                        Tofacitinib tablet-treated patients with moderate renal impairment (RI) (CLcr ≥30 mL/minuteand ≤50 mL/minute) or severe RI (<30 mL/minute) had greater tofacitinib blood concentrations than tofacitinib tablet-treated patients with normal renal function (CLcr >80 mL/minute). The recommended dosage of tofacitinib tablets in patients with moderate or severe RI (including those with severe RI who are undergoing hemodialysis) is lower than the recommended dosage in patients with normal renal function

  .

                           Mild Renal Impairment

                        The recommended dosage in patients with mild RI (CLcr >50 and ≤80 mL/minute) is the same as patients with normal renal function.
