DailyMed 3d011250-a517-4c27-8ce3-d59d441ea61c | Haloperidol decanoate | 5.15 Risk of Severe Neurotoxicity in Patients with Thyrotoxicosis

Severe neurotoxicity (rigidity, inability to walk or talk) may occur in patients with thyrotoxicosis who are also receiving antipsychotic drugs, including haloperidol decanoate.
