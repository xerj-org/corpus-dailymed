DailyMed 3d011250-a517-4c27-8ce3-d59d441ea61c | Haloperidol decanoate | 7.1 Drugs that Prolong the QTc Interval

Avoid concomitant use of haloperidol decanoate with other drugs with a known potential to prolong the QTc interval. If concomitant use cannot be avoided [

  see Warnings and Precautions(

  5.2)]:

                        • Obtain ECGs when initiating and during concomitant use as clinically indicated.

                        • Obtain serum electrolytes (including potassium, calcium, phosphorus and magnesium) when initiating and during concomitant use as clinically indicated.

                        QTc interval prolongation has been observed with haloperidol decanoate treatment. Concomitant use of haloperidol decanoate with other products that prolong the QTc interval may result in a greater increase in the QTc interval and adverse reactions associated with QTc interval prolongation, including torsade de pointes, other serious arrythmias and sudden death [

  see Warnings and Precautions(

  5.2)].
