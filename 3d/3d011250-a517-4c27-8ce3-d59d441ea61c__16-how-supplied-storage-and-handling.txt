                  Storage and Handling

                  Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature]. Do not refrigerate or freeze. Protect from light. Retain in carton until contents are used. Keep out of reach of children. Discard unused portion.
