DailyMed 180985f0-3fbd-4868-a826-b84101618a03 | Tizanidine | 5.5 Hypersensitivity Reactions

Tizanidine hydrochloride can cause anaphylaxis. Signs and symptoms of hypersensitivity, including respiratory compromise, urticaria, and angioedema of the throat and tongue, have been reported. Tizanidine hydrochloride is contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity reactions to tizanidine [see Contraindications (4)].
