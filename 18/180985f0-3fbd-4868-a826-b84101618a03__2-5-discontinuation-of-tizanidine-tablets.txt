DailyMed 180985f0-3fbd-4868-a826-b84101618a03 | Tizanidine | 2.5 Discontinuation of Tizanidine Tablets

When discontinuing tizanidine tablets, particularly in patients who have been receiving high doses for long periods or who may be on concomitant treatment with narcotics, decreasethe dosage by 2 mg to 4 mg per day to minimize the risk of withdrawal adverse reactions [see Drug Abuse and Dependence (9.3)].
