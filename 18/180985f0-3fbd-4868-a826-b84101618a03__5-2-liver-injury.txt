DailyMed 180985f0-3fbd-4868-a826-b84101618a03 | Tizanidine | 5.2 Liver Injury

Tizanidine hydrochloride may cause hepatocellular liver injury. Liver function test abnormality and hepatotoxicity have been observed with tizanidine hydrochloride [see Adverse Reactions (6.1, 6.2)]. Monitoring of aminotransferase levels is recommended at baseline and 1 month after maximum dose is achieved, or if hepatic injury is suspected[see Dosage and Administration (2.1) and Use in Specific Populations (8.7)].
