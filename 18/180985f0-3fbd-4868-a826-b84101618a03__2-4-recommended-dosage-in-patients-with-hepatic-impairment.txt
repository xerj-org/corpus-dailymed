DailyMed 180985f0-3fbd-4868-a826-b84101618a03 | Tizanidine | 2.4 Recommended Dosage in Patients with Hepatic Impairment

In patients with hepatic impairment, use lowerindividual doses during titration. If higher doses are required, individual doses rather than dosing frequency should be increased [see Use in Specific Populations (8.7) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
