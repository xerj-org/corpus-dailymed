DailyMed 180985f0-3fbd-4868-a826-b84101618a03 | Tizanidine | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Tizanidine hydrochloride is contraindicated in patients:

                     taking strong CYP1A2 inhibitors [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].

                     with a history of hypersensitivity to tizanidine or the ingredients in tizanidine tablets. Symptoms have included anaphylaxis and angioedema [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
