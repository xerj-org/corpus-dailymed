DailyMed 44ec2918-4e0c-4e2d-9157-81295a15df57 | Pregabalin | 9.3 Dependence

In clinical studies, following abrupt or rapid discontinuation of pregabalin, some patients reported symptoms including insomnia, nausea, headache or diarrhea

  [see

   Warnings and Precautions (5.4)],
                           consistent with physical dependence. In the postmarketing setting, in addition to these reported symptoms, other reported adverse reactions include, but are not limited to, seizures, depression, suicidal ideation and behavior, agitation, confusion, disorientation, psychotic symptoms, pain, sweating, tremor, dizziness, and malaise.
