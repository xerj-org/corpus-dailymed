DailyMed 44ec2918-4e0c-4e2d-9157-81295a15df57 | Pregabalin | 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Pregabalin capsule is indicated for:

                     Management of neuropathic pain associated with diabetic peripheral neuropathy

                     Management of postherpetic neuralgia

                     Adjunctive therapy for the treatment of partial-onset seizures in patients 1month of age and older

                     Management of fibromyalgia

                     Management of neuropathic pain associated with spinal cord injury
