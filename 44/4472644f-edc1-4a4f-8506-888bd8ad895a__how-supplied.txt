DailyMed 4472644f-edc1-4a4f-8506-888bd8ad895a | Vetsulin | HOW SUPPLIED

vetsulin® is supplied as a sterile injectable suspension in multidose vials containing 10 mL of 40 International Units/mL porcine insulin zinc suspension or in multidose cartridges containing 2.7 mL of 40 International Units/mL porcine insulin zinc suspension. Vials are supplied in cartons of one, 10 mL vial. Cartridges are supplied in cartons of 10, 2.7 mL cartridges.
