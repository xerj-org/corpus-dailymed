DailyMed 09736975-2493-4dbc-bbe0-cc56a1fec53f | Gabapentin | 10 OVERDOSAGE

Signs of acute toxicity in animals included ataxia, labored breathing, ptosis, sedation, hypoactivity, or excitation.

                  Acute oral overdoses of gabapentin capsules havebeen reported. Symptoms have included double vision, tremor, slurred speech, drowsiness, altered mental status, dizziness, lethargy, and diarrhea. Fatal respiratory depression has been reported with gabapentin capsules overdose, alone and in combination with other CNS depressants.

                  Gabapentin can be removed by hemodialysis.

                  If overexposure occurs, call your poison control center at 1-800-222-1222.
