DailyMed 0961f83e-25e9-4ffc-8669-44e4e9169978 | Bupropion Hydrochloride | 8.7 Hepatic Impairment

In patients with moderate to severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh score: 7 to 15), the maximum dose of bupropion hydrochloride tablets is 75 mg daily. In patients with mild hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh score: 5 to 6), consider reducing the dose and/or frequency of dosing [
                           see Dosage and Administration (2.2),

                              Clinical Pharmacology (12.3) ].
