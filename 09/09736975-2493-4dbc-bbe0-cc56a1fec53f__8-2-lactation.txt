DailyMed 09736975-2493-4dbc-bbe0-cc56a1fec53f | Gabapentin | 8.2 Lactation

Risk Summary

                        Gabapentin is secreted in human milk following oral administration. The effects on the breastfed infant and on milk production are unknown. The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be considered along with the mother's clinical need for gabapentin capsules and any potential adverse effects on the breastfed infant from gabapentin capsules or from the underlying maternal condition.
