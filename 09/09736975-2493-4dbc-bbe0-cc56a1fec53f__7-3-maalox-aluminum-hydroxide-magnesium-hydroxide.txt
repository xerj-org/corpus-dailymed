DailyMed 09736975-2493-4dbc-bbe0-cc56a1fec53f | Gabapentin | 7.3 Maalox® (aluminum hydroxide, magnesium hydroxide)

The mean bioavailability of gabapentin was reduced by about 20% with concomitant use of an antacid (Maalox

  ®) containing magnesium and aluminum hydroxides. It is recommended that gabapentin be taken at least 2 hours following Maalox administration

  [see

   Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]

  .
