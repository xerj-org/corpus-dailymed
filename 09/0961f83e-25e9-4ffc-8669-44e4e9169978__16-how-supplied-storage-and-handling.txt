DailyMed 0961f83e-25e9-4ffc-8669-44e4e9169978 | Bupropion Hydrochloride | 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bupropion hydrochloride tablets, USP 75 mg are yellow, round, film coated, biconvex tablets debossed with "C112" on one side and plain on other side. Tablets are provided as follows:

                  Bottle of 100 Tablets (NDC 72241-020-05)

Bottle of 1000 Tablets (NDC 72241-020-11)

                  A carton containing a total of 100 tablets (10 blister packs of 10 tablets each) (NDC 72241-020-17)

                  Bupropion hydrochloride tablets, USP 100 mg are maroon, round, film coated, biconvex tablets debossed with "C111" on one side and plain on other side. Tablets are provided as follows:

                  Bottle of 100 Tablets (NDC 72241-021-05)

Bottle of 1000 Tablets (NDC 72241-021-11)

A carton containing a total of 100 tablets (10 blister packs of 10 tablets each) (NDC 72241-021-17)

Store at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF); excursions permitted between 15º to 30ºC (59º to 86ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature]. Protect from light and moisture.
