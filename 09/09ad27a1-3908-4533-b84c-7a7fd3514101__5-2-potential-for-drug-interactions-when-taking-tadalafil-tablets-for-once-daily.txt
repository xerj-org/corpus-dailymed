DailyMed 09ad27a1-3908-4533-b84c-7a7fd3514101 | TADALAFIL | 5.2 Potential for Drug Interactions When Taking Tadalafil Tablets for Once Daily Use

Physicians should be aware that tadalafil tablets for once daily use provide continuous plasma tadalafil levels and should consider this when evaluating the potential for interactions with medications (e.g., nitrates, alpha-blockers, anti-hypertensives and potent inhibitors of CYP3A4) and with substantial consumption of alcohol [see Drug Interactions (7.1, 7.2, 7.3)].
