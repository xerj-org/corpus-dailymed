DailyMed 8faf357b-99a1-440b-8eac-8545cb19a69c | Mycophenolate Mofetil | 17.6 Acute Inflammatory Syndrome

Inform patients that acute inflammatory reactions have been reported in some patients who received mycophenolate mofetil tablets. Some reactions were severe, requiring hospitalization. Advise patients to contact their physician if they develop fever, joint stiffness, joint pain or muscle pains [see Warnings and Precautions (5.7)].
