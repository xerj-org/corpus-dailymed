DailyMed 8faf357b-99a1-440b-8eac-8545cb19a69c | Mycophenolate Mofetil | 16.1 Handling and Disposal

Mycophenolate mofetil (MMF) has demonstrated teratogenic effects in humans [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1) and Use in Specific Populations (8.1)]. Mycophenolate mofetil tablets USP should not be crushed. Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures
