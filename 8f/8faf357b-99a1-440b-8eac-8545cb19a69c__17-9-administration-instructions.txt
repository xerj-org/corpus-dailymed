DailyMed 8faf357b-99a1-440b-8eac-8545cb19a69c | Mycophenolate Mofetil | 17.9 Administration Instructions

Advise patients not to crush mycophenolate mofetil tablets.

                           Advise patients to take a missed dose as soon as they remember, except if it is closer than 2 hours to the next scheduled dose; in this case they should continue to take mycophenolate mofetil tablets at the usual times.
