Mycophenolate Mofetil Tablets USP, 500 mg are purple colored, modified capsule shaped, film-coated tablets debossed with "M1" on one side and plain on the other side. They are supplied as follows:

 Bottles of 100   NDC 59651-638-01

                        Bottles of 500   NDC 59651-638-05

 Storage and Dispensing Information:

                           Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

                           Dispense in light-resistant containers, such as the manufacturer’s original containers.
