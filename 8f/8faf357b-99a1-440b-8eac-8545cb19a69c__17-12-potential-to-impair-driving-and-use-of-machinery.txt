DailyMed 8faf357b-99a1-440b-8eac-8545cb19a69c | Mycophenolate Mofetil | 17.12 Potential to Impair Driving and Use of Machinery

Advise patients that mycophenolate mofetil tablets can affect the ability to drive or operate machines. Patients should avoid driving or operating machines if they experience somnolence, confusion, dizziness, tremor or hypotension during treatment with mycophenolate mofetil tablets [see Warnings and Precautions (5.15)].
