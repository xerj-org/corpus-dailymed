DailyMed 8faf357b-99a1-440b-8eac-8545cb19a69c | Mycophenolate Mofetil | 17.5 Gastrointestinal Tract Complications

Inform patients that mycophenolate mofetil tablets can cause gastrointestinal tract complications including bleeding, intestinal perforations, and gastric or duodenal ulcers. Advise the patient to contact their healthcare provider if they have symptoms of gastrointestinal bleeding, or sudden onset or persistent abdominal pain [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
