DailyMed 4e8516dc-a3c6-4d8f-aa14-2c37c6c8f6e6 | ISOTRETINOIN | 5.7 Pancreatitis

Acute pancreatitis has been reported with isotretinoin use in patients with either elevated or normal serum triglyceride levels. In rare instances, fatal hemorrhagic pancreatitis has been reported. If symptoms of pancreatitis occur, discontinue isotretinoin and instruct patients to seek medical attention.
