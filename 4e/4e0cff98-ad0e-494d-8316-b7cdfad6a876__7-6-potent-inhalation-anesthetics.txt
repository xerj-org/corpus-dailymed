DailyMed 4e0cff98-ad0e-494d-8316-b7cdfad6a876 | Bupivacaine Hydrochloride | 7.6 Potent Inhalation Anesthetics

Serious dose-related cardiac arrhythmias may occur if preparations containing a vasoconstrictor such as epinephrine are used in patients during or following the administration of potent inhalation anesthetics.
