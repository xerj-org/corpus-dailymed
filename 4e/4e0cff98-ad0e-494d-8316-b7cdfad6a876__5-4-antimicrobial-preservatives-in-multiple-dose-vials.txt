DailyMed 4e0cff98-ad0e-494d-8316-b7cdfad6a876 | Bupivacaine Hydrochloride | 5.4 Antimicrobial Preservatives in Multiple-Dose Vials

Avoid use of bupivacaine hydrochloride solutions containing antimicrobial preservatives, i.e., those supplied in multiple-dose vials, for epidural or caudal anesthesia because safety has not been established with such use.
