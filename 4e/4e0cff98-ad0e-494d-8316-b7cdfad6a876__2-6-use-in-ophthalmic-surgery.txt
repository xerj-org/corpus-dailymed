DailyMed 4e0cff98-ad0e-494d-8316-b7cdfad6a876 | Bupivacaine Hydrochloride | 2.6 Use in Ophthalmic Surgery

When bupivacaine hydrochloride injection 0.75% (7.5 mg/mL) is used for retrobulbar block, complete corneal anesthesia usually precedes onset of clinically acceptable external ocularmuscle akinesia. Therefore, presence of akinesia rather than anesthesia alone should determine readiness of the patient for surgery [
