DailyMed 4e8516dc-a3c6-4d8f-aa14-2c37c6c8f6e6 | ISOTRETINOIN | 7.3 Oral Contraceptives

Isotretinoin Capsules did not result in clinically significant changes in the pharmacokinetics of norethindrone and ethinyl estradiol when used concomitantly with a norethindrone and ethinyl estradiol oral contraceptive [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]. It is not known if there is an interaction with the concomitant use of isotretinoin with other oral contraceptives.
