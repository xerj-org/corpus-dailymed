DailyMed 4e0cff98-ad0e-494d-8316-b7cdfad6a876 | Bupivacaine Hydrochloride | 8.7 Renal Impairment

Bupivacaine is known to be substantially excreted by the kidney, and the risk of adverse reactions to this drug may be greater in patients with renal impairment. This should be considered when selecting the bupivacaine hydrochloride dosage [

  see Use in Specific Populations (

   8.5)

  ].
