DailyMed 4e8516dc-a3c6-4d8f-aa14-2c37c6c8f6e6 | ISOTRETINOIN | 5.9 Hearing Impairment

Impaired hearing has been reported in patients taking isotretinoin; in some cases, the hearing impairment has been reported to persist after treatment has been discontinued. Mechanism(s) and causality for this reaction have not been established. Discontinue isotretinoin treatment in patients who experience tinnitus or hearing impairment and refer them for specialized care for further evaluation.
