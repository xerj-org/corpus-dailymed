DailyMed 4e8516dc-a3c6-4d8f-aa14-2c37c6c8f6e6 | ISOTRETINOIN | 5.3 Isotretinoin Capsules and ABSORICA LD are Not Substitutable

Given that the bioavailability and the recommended dosage of isotretinoin capsules and ABSORICA LD are different, isotretinoin capsules and ABSORICA LD are not substitutable on a mg per mg basis. For example, isotretinoin capsules and ABSORICA LD have a 20 mg strength; however, these strengths have different bioavailability and are not substitutable.
