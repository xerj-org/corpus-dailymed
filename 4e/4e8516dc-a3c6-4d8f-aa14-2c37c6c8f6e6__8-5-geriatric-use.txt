DailyMed 4e8516dc-a3c6-4d8f-aa14-2c37c6c8f6e6 | ISOTRETINOIN | 8.5 Geriatric Use

Clinical studies of isotretinoin did not include sufficient numbers of geriatric subjects (subjects aged 65 years of age and older) to determine whether they respond differently from younger adult subjects. The effects of aging may increase some risks associated with isotretinoin treatment.
