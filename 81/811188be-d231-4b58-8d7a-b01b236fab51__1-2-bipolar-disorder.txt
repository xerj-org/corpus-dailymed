DailyMed 811188be-d231-4b58-8d7a-b01b236fab51 | Lamotrigine | 1.2 Bipolar Disorder

Lamotrigine is indicated for the maintenance treatment of bipolar I disorder to delay the time to occurrence of mood episodes (depression, mania, hypomania, mixed episodes) in patients treated for acute mood episodes with standard therapy [see Clinical Studies (

  14.2)].

                           Limitations of Use

                        Treatment of acute manic or mixed episodes is not recommended. Effectiveness of lamotrigine in the acute treatment of mood episodes has not been established.
