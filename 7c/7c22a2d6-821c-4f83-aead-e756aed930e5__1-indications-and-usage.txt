DailyMed 7c22a2d6-821c-4f83-aead-e756aed930e5 | Naproxen Sodium | 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release Tablets are indicated for the treatment of:

                     rheumatoid arthritis (RA)

                     osteoarthritis (OA)

                     ankylosing spondylitis (AS)

                     tendinitis, bursitis

                     acute gout

                     primary dysmenorrhea (PD)

                     the relief of mild to moderate pain 

                  [see Warnings and Precautions (5)].
