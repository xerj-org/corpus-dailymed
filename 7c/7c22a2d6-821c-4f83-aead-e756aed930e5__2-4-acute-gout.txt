DailyMed 7c22a2d6-821c-4f83-aead-e756aed930e5 | Naproxen Sodium | 2.4 Acute Gout

The recommended dose on the first day is two to three Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release 500 mg tablets (1,000 to 1,500 mg) once daily, followed by two Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release 500 mg tablets (1,000 mg) once daily, until the attack has subsided.
