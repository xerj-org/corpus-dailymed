DailyMed 7c22a2d6-821c-4f83-aead-e756aed930e5 | Naproxen Sodium | 5.9 Serious Skin Reactions

NSAIDs, including naproxen can cause serious skin adverse reactions such as exfoliative dermatitis, Stevens-Johnson Syndrome (SJS), and toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), which can be fatal. NSAIDs can also cause fixed drug eruption (FDE). FDE may present as a more severe variant known as generalized bullous fixed drug eruption (GBFDE), which can be life-threatening. These serious events may occur without warning. Inform patients about the signs and symptoms of serious skin reactions, and to discontinue the use of Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release Tablets at the first appearance of skin rash or any other sign of hypersensitivity.

                        Naproxen sodium is contraindicated in patients with previous serious skin reactions to NSAIDs [see Contraindications (4)].
