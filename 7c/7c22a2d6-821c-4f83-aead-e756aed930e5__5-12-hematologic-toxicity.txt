DailyMed 7c22a2d6-821c-4f83-aead-e756aed930e5 | Naproxen Sodium | 5.12 Hematologic Toxicity

Anemia has occurred in NSAID-treated patients. This may be due to occult or gross blood loss, fluid retention, or an incompletely described effect on erythropoiesis. If a patient treated with Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release Tablets has any signs or symptoms of anemia, monitor hemoglobin or hematocrit.

                        NSAIDs, including Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release Tablets, may increase the risk of bleeding events. Co-morbid conditions such as coagulation disorders, concomitant use of warfarin, other anticoagulants, antiplatelet agents (e.g., aspirin), serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs) and serotonin norepinephrine reuptake inhibitors (SNRIs) may increase this risk. Monitor these patients for signs of bleeding [see Drug Interactions (7)].
