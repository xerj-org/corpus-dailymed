Infertility

                           FemalesBased on the mechanism of action, the use of prostaglandin-mediated NSAIDs, including naproxen sodium, may delay or prevent rupture of ovarian follicles, which has been associated with reversible infertility in some women. Published animal studies have shown that administration of prostaglandin synthesis inhibitors has the potential to disrupt prostaglandin-mediated follicular rupture required for ovulation. Small studies in women treated with NSAIDs have also shown a reversible delay in ovulation. Consider withdrawal of NSAIDs, including naproxen sodium, in women who have difficulties conceiving or who are undergoing investigation of infertility.
