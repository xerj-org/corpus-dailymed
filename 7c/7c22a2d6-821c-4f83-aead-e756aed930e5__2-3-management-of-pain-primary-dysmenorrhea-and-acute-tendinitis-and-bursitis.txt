DailyMed 7c22a2d6-821c-4f83-aead-e756aed930e5 | Naproxen Sodium | 2.3 Management of Pain, Primary Dysmenorrhea, and Acute Tendinitis and Bursitis

The recommended starting dose is two Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release 500 mg tablets (1,000 mg) once daily. For patients requiring greater analgesic benefit, two Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release 750 mg tablets (1,500 mg) or three Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release 500 mg tablets (1,500 mg) may be used for a limited period. Thereafter, the total daily dose should not exceed two Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release 500 mg tablets (1,000 mg).
