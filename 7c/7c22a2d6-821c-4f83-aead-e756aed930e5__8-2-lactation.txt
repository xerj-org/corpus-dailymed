Risk Summary

The naproxen anion has been found in the milk of lactating women at a concentration of approximately 1% of that found in the plasma.  The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be considered along with the mother’s clinical need for naproxen sodium and any potential adverse effects on the breastfed infant from the naproxen sodium or from the underlying maternal condition.
