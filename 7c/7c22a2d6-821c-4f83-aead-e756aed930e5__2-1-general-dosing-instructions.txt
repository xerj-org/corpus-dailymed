DailyMed 7c22a2d6-821c-4f83-aead-e756aed930e5 | Naproxen Sodium | 2.1 General Dosing Instructions

Carefully consider the potential benefits and risks of Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release Tablets and other treatment options before deciding to use Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release Tablets. Use the lowest effective dosage for the shortest duration consistent with individual patient treatment goals [see Warnings and Precautions (5)]. 

                        After observing the response to initial therapy with Naproxen Sodium Controlled-Release Tablets, the dose and frequency should be adjusted to suit an individual patient’s needs.
