DailyMed 89eb2ffc-02d9-4b46-b480-e9b10af33027 | TOFACITINIB CITRATE | 1.3 Ankylosing Spondylitis

Tofacitinib tablets are indicated for the treatment of adult patients with active ankylosing spondylitis (AS), who have had an inadequate response or intolerance to one or more TNF blockers.

                           Limitations of Use

                        Use of tofacitinib in combination with biologic DMARDs or potent immunosuppressants such as azathioprine and cyclosporine is not recommended.
