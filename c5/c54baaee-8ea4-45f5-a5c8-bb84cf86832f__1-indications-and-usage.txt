DailyMed c54baaee-8ea4-45f5-a5c8-bb84cf86832f | Methadone Hydrochloride | 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Methadone hydrochloride tablets for oral suspension contain methadone, an opioid agonist indicated for the:

                     Detoxification treatment of opioid addiction (heroin or other morphine-like drugs).

                     Maintenance treatment of opioid addiction (heroin or other morphine-like drugs), in conjunction with appropriate social and medical services.

                     Limitations of Use

                  Methadone products used for the treatment of opioid addiction in detoxification or maintenance programs are subject to the conditions for distribution and use required under 42 CFR 8.12 [see Dosage and Administration (

                        2.1)].
