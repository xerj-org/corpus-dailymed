DailyMed c54baaee-8ea4-45f5-a5c8-bb84cf86832f | Methadone Hydrochloride | 5.16 Risks of Driving and Operating Machinery

Methadone hydrochloride tablets for oral suspension may impair the mental or physical abilities needed to perform potentially hazardous activities such as driving a car or operating machinery. Warn patients not to drive or operate dangerous machinery unless they are tolerant to the effects of methadone hydrochloride tablets for oral suspension and know how they will react to the medication [see Patient Counseling Information (17)].
