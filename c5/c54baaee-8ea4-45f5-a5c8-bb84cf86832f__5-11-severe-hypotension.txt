DailyMed c54baaee-8ea4-45f5-a5c8-bb84cf86832f | Methadone Hydrochloride | 5.11 Severe Hypotension

Methadone may cause severe hypotension including orthostatic hypotension and syncope in ambulatory patients. There is an increased risk in patients whose ability to maintain normal blood pressure is compromised by a reduced blood volume or concurrent administration of certain CNS depressant drugs (e.g. phenothiazines or general anesthetics) [see Drug Interactions (

                                 7

                           )
                           ]. Monitor these patients for signs of hypotension after initiating or titrating the dosage of methadone hydrochloride tablets for oral suspension. In patients with circulatory shock, methadone hydrochloride tablets for oral suspension may cause vasodilation that can further reduce cardiac output and blood pressure. Avoid the use of methadone hydrochloride tablets for oral suspension in patients with circulatory shock.
