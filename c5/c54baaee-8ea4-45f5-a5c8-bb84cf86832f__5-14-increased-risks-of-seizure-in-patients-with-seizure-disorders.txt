DailyMed c54baaee-8ea4-45f5-a5c8-bb84cf86832f | Methadone Hydrochloride | 5.14 Increased Risks of Seizure in Patients with Seizure Disorders

Methadone may increase frequency of seizures in patients with seizure disorders, and increase the risks of seizures occurring in other clinical settings associated with seizures. Monitor patients with a history of seizure disorders for worsened seizure control during methadone hydrochloride tablets for oral suspension therapy.
