Telmisartan tablets USP, are available as white to off-white, uncoated tablets containing telmisartan 20 mg, 40 mg, or 80 mg. Tablets are marked with “S” on one side, and on the other side, with either “511”, “512”, or “513” for the 20 mg, 40 mg, and 80 mg strengths, respectively. Tablets are provided as follows:

                  Telmisartan tablets USP, 40 mg are oval and packaged in bottles of:

                  bottles of 30 (NDC 68788-8665-3)

                  bottles of 60 (NDC 68788-8665-6)

                  bottles of 90 (NDC 68788-8665-9)

                     Storage

                  Store at 20°Cto 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°Cto 30°C (59°Fto 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature]. Keep bottle tightly closed. Tablets should not be removed from bottle until immediately before administration.
