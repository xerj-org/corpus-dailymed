DailyMed aea9a3ad-c244-42be-9cd3-5df83fe2b522 | DEXMEDETOMIDINE HYDROCHLORIDE IN 0.9% SODIUM CHLORIDE | 1.2 Procedural Sedation

Dexmedetomidine hydrochloride in 0.9% sodium chloride injection is indicated for sedation of non-intubated adult patients prior to and/or during surgical and other procedures.

                        Dexmedetomidine hydrochloride in 0.9% sodium chloride injection is indicated for sedation of non-intubated pediatric patients aged 1 month to less than 18 years prior to and during non-invasive procedures.
