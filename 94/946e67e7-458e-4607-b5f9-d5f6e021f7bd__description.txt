Tretinoin cream USP, 0.025% is used for the topical treatment of acne vulgaris. Tretinoin cream, USP contains tretinoin, 0.025% by weight, in a hydrophilic cream vehicle of butylated hydroxytoluene, isopropyl myristate, polyoxyl 40 stearate, purified water, sorbic acid, stearic acid, stearyl alcohol and xanthan gum.
