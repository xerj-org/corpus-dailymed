DailyMed 946e67e7-458e-4607-b5f9-d5f6e021f7bd | Tretinoin | ADVERSE REACTIONS

The skin of certain sensitive individuals may become excessively red, edematous, blistered or crusted. If these effects occur, the medication should either be discontinued until the integrity of the skin is restored or the medication should be adjusted to a level the patient can tolerate. True contact allergy to topical tretinoin is rarely encountered. Temporary hyper- or hypopigmentation has been reported with repeated application of tretinoin. Some individuals have been reported to have heightened susceptibility to sunlight while under treatment with tretinoin. To date, all adverse effects of tretinoin have been reversible upon discontinuance of therapy (see DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION).
