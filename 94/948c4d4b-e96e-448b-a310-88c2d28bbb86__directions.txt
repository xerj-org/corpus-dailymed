DailyMed 948c4d4b-e96e-448b-a310-88c2d28bbb86 | Lidocaine 4% Patch | Directions

Adult and children 12 years of age and over:

                     Clean and dry affected area

                     Peel patch off plastic liner and place patch on affected area for no more than 12 hours

                     Wash hands after applying patch

                     Under age 12, consult a physician before use

                     For easier removal of the patch, apply rubbing alcohol or baby oil to adhesive edge while gently peeling

                     Children under 12 years of age: Consult a doctor
