DailyMed 8351aa37-552d-471d-b293-c564dcb6ec29 | Ceftriaxone | Interaction with Calcium

Two in vitro studies, one using adult plasma and the other neonatal plasma from umbilical cord blood have been carried out to assess interaction of ceftriaxone and calcium. Ceftriaxone concentrations up to 1 mM (in excess of concentrations achieved in vivo following administration of 2 grams ceftriaxone infused over 30 minutes) were used in combination with calcium concentrations up to 12 mM (48 mg/‌dL). Recovery of ceftriaxone from plasma was reduced with calcium concentrations of 6 mM (24 mg/‌dL) or higher in adult plasma or 4 mM (16 mg/‌dL) or higher in neonatal plasma. This may be reflective of ceftriaxone-calcium precipitation.
