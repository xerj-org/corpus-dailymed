DailyMed 8351aa37-552d-471d-b293-c564dcb6ec29 | Ceftriaxone | Pediatric Use

Safety and effectiveness of ceftriaxone in neonates, infants and pediatric patients have been established for the dosages described in the 
                              DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION
                            section. In vitro studies have shown that ceftriaxone, like some other cephalosporins, can displace bilirubin from serum albumin. Ceftriaxone should not be administered to hyperbilirubinemic neonates, especially prematures (see
                              CONTRAINDICATIONS
                           ).
