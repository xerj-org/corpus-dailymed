DailyMed 834db232-afb0-4dcf-93e0-009ce8245c20 | UPLIZNA | 8.5 Geriatric Use

NMOSD

                        Clinical studies of UPLIZNA did not include sufficient numbers of patients aged 65 and over to determine whether they respond differently from younger patients (aged 18 to 64 years of age).

                           IgG4-RD

                        Twenty-nine percent (32 out of 112) of patients 65 years of age or older, were treated with UPLIZNA. There were no overall age-related differences in safety or efficacy observed compared to younger patients (aged 18 to 64 years of age).
