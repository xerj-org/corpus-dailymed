DailyMed 834db232-afb0-4dcf-93e0-009ce8245c20 | UPLIZNA | 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in original carton to protect from light.

                           Do not freeze.

                           Do not shake.

                           Store vials upright.
