DailyMed 83297e2e-9199-4b3d-9f44-a921787758fe | LAMOTRIGINE | 5.12 Addition of Lamotrigine to a Multidrug Regimen that Includes Valproate

Because valproate reduces the clearance of lamotrigine, the dosage of lamotrigine in the presence of valproate is less than half of that required in its absence

  [see Dosage and Administration (2.2, 2.3, 2.4), Drug Interactions (7)].
