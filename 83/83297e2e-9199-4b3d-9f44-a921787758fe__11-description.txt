Lamotrigine, USP, an AED of the phenyltriazine class, is chemically unrelated to existing AEDs. Lamotrigine, USP chemical name is 3,5-diamino-6-(2,3-dichlorophenyl)-1,2,4-triazine, its molecular formula is C

  9H

  7Cl

  2N

  5, and its molecular weight is 256.1 g/mol. Lamotrigine, USP is a white to pale cream-colored powder. Lamotrigine is very slightly soluble in water, slightly soluble in 0.1 M HCl, sparingly soluble in methanol and in ethanol 96%.

                  Lamotrigine orally disintegrating tablets, USP are supplied for oral administration. The tablets contain 25 mg (white to off-white), 50 mg (white to off-white), 100 mg (white to off-white), or 200 mg (white to off-white) of lamotrigine, USP and the following inactive ingredients: colloidal silicon dioxide, croscarmellose sodium, magnesium stearate, mannitol, microcrystalline cellulose, peppermint flavor, sodium stearyl fumarate and sucralose.
