DailyMed 40bf2923-24b1-4af1-8d20-03cf06b455a5 | Carbamazepine | Combination Therapy:

Carbamazepine may be used alone or with other anticonvulsants. When added to existing anticonvulsant therapy, the drug should be added gradually while the other anticonvulsants are maintained or gradually decreased, except phenytoin, which may have to be increased (see

  PRECAUTIONS, Drug Interactions, and

  Pregnancy).
