DailyMed 60381a7e-d7ae-4384-925d-c19c418d1663 | Tocilizumab-anoh | 2.5 Recommended Dosage for Systemic Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis

Tocilizumab-anoh may be used as an intravenous infusion or as a subcutaneous injection alone or in combination with methotrexate. Do not change a dose based solely on a single visit body weight measurement, as weight may fluctuate.
