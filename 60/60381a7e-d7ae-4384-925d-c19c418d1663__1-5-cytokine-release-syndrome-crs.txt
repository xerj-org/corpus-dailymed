DailyMed 60381a7e-d7ae-4384-925d-c19c418d1663 | Tocilizumab-anoh | 1.5 Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS)

Tocilizumab-anoh is indicated for the treatment of chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T cell-induced severe or life-threatening cytokine release syndrome in adults and pediatric patients 2 years of age and older.
