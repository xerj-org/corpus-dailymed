DailyMed 60381a7e-d7ae-4384-925d-c19c418d1663 | Tocilizumab-anoh | 5.5 Immunosuppression

The impact of treatment with tocilizumab products on the development of malignancies is not known but malignancies were observed in clinical studies [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)]. Tocilizumab-anoh is an immunosuppressant, and treatment with immunosuppressants may result in an increased risk of malignancies.
