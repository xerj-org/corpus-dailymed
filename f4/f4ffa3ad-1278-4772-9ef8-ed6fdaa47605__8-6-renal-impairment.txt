DailyMed f4ffa3ad-1278-4772-9ef8-ed6fdaa47605 | LACOSAMIDE | 8.6 Renal Impairment

No dose adjustment is necessary in patients with mild to moderate renal impairment (CL

  CR≥30 mL/min). In patients with severe renal impairment (CL

  CR<30 mL/min as estimated by the Cockcroft-Gault equation for adults; CL

  CRless than 30 mL/min/1.73m

  2as estimated by the Schwartz equation for pediatric patients) and in those with end-stage renal disease, a reduction of 25% of the maximum dosage is recommended

  [see

   Dosage and Administration (2.4)and

   Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]

  .

                        In all patients with renal impairment, dose initiation and titration should be based on clinical response and tolerability.

                        Lacosamide is effectively removed from plasma by hemodialysis. Dosage supplementation of up to 50% following hemodialysis should be considered.
