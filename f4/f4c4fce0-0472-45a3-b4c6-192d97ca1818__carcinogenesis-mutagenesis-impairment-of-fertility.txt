DailyMed f4c4fce0-0472-45a3-b4c6-192d97ca1818 | CLINDAMYCIN HYDROCHLORIDE | Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Long-term studies in animals have not been performed with clindamycin to evaluate carcinogenic potential. Genotoxicity tests performed included a rat micronucleus test and an Ames Salmonella reversion test. Both tests were negative.

                        Fertility studies in rats treated orally with up to 300 mg/kg/day (approximately 1.6 times the highest recommended adult human dose based on mg/m

  2) revealed no effects on fertility or mating ability.
