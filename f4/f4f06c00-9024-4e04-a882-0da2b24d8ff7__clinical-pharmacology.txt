Atenolol is a beta

  1-selective (cardioselective) beta-adrenergic receptor blocking agent without membrane stabilizing or intrinsic sympathomimetic (partial agonist) activities. This preferential effect is not absolute, however, and at higher doses, atenolol tablets inhibit beta

  2-adrenoreceptors, chiefly located in the bronchial and vascular musculature.
