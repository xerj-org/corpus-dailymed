DailyMed f4ffa3ad-1278-4772-9ef8-ed6fdaa47605 | LACOSAMIDE | 2.5 Dosage Information for Patients with Hepatic Impairment

For patients with mild or moderate hepatic impairment, a reduction of 25% of the maximum dosage is recommended. The dose initiation and titration should be based on clinical response and tolerability in patients with hepatic impairment. Lacosamide use is not recommended in patients with severe hepatic impairment.

                           Concomitant Strong CYP3A4 and CYP2C9 Inhibitors
