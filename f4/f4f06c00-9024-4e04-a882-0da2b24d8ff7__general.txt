Patients already on a beta-blocker must be evaluated carefully before atenolol tablets are administered. Initial and subsequent atenolol tablets dosages can be adjusted downward depending on clinical observations including pulse and blood pressure. Atenolol tablets may aggravate peripheral arterial circulatory disorders.
