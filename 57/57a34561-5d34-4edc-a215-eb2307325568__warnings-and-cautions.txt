DailyMed 57a34561-5d34-4edc-a215-eb2307325568 | OZIPCO CHICKEN DEWORMER | Warnings and cautions

KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN.

                  For animal use only. Not for human use.

                  Consult a veterinarian before use in sick, debilitated, or stressed birds.

                  Do not use in chicks under 2 weeks of age unless directed by a veterinarian.

                  Use only as directed. Overdosing may increase the risk of adverse effects. If unexpected reactions occur, discontinue use and consult a veterinarian immediately.
