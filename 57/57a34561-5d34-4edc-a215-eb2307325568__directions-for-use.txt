DailyMed 57a34561-5d34-4edc-a215-eb2307325568 | OZIPCO CHICKEN DEWORMER | Directions for use

Shake well before each use.

                  Administer orally in drinking water.

                  Use 1 mg fenbendazole per kg of total flock body weight once daily for 5 consecutive days.

                  This is equivalent to 0.005 mL of product per kg of total flock body weight per day.

                  Prepare fresh medicated drinking water daily and ensure it is the only source of drinking water during treatment.
