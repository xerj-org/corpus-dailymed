DailyMed 57c515c8-f4af-b876-e063-6394a90af0cc | IMPAVIDO | 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Each IMPAVIDO capsule contains 50 mg miltefosine in an opaque, red, hard gelatin capsule. IMPAVIDO capsules are supplied in a folded peel/push-through child-resistant blister card. Each blister card contains 14 capsules. Each carton contains two blister cards (NDC 71863-135-28).

Store at 20-25 °C (68-77 °F); excursions permitted to 15-30 °C (59-86 °F). [See USP Controlled Room Temperature]. Protect from moisture.

Dispense only in the original carton.
