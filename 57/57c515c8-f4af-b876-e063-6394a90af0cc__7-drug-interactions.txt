DailyMed 57c515c8-f4af-b876-e063-6394a90af0cc | IMPAVIDO | 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

In vitro and animal metabolism studies showed that miltefosine did not markedly induce or inhibit the activity of the major human cytochrome P450 enzymes
  [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]. The potential of miltefosine to interact with drug transporters has not been evaluated.
