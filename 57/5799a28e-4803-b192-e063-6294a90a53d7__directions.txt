DailyMed 5799a28e-4803-b192-e063-6294a90a53d7 | pinworm medicine | Directions

Adults, children 12 years of age and over, and children 2 years to under 12 years of age: Oral dosage is a single dose of 5 milligrams of pyrantel base per pound, or 11 milligrams per kilogram, of body weight not to exceed 1 gram (4 tablets).

■
   Carefully read additional product information on the side panel before using this medication

■ treat the entire household unless otherwise advised

■ take only according to directions and do not exceed the recommended dosage unless directed by a doctor.

■ take one time as a single dose; do not repeat treatment unless directed by a doctor

■ this product can be taken any time of day, with or without meals. It may be taken alone or with milk or fruit juice. Use of a laxative is not necessary prior to, during, or after medication.
