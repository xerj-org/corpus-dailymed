DailyMed bb6dba23-e7a6-4765-a1e6-b9e277ce0381 | NIKTIMVO | 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

NIKTIMVO (axatilimab-csfr) injection is a slightly opalescent, pale brownish yellow solution. It is supplied in a carton containing one single-dose vial as:

                     9 mg/0.18 mL (NDC 50881-034-12)

                     9 mg/0.9 mL (NDC 50881-036-17)

                     22 mg/0.44 mL (NDC 50881-023-11)

                     22 mg/2.2 mL (10 mg/mL) (NDC 50881-047-18)

                  Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze or shake.
