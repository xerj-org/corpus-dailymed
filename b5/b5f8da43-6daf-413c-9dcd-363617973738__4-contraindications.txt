DailyMed b5f8da43-6daf-413c-9dcd-363617973738 | TOPIRAMATE | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Topiramate extended-release capsules is contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity reaction to topiramate, topiramate extended-release capsules, or any of the inactive ingredients of topiramate extended-release capsules. Anaphylaxis and angioedema have occurred with topiramate [see Warnings and Precautions (5.13)].
