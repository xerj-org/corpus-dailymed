DailyMed b5c12c9a-62eb-481a-8099-7ad35242f7bf | Bupropion Hydrochloride | 8.6 Renal Impairment

Consider a reduced dose and/or dosing frequency of bupropion hydrochloride tablets in patients with renal impairment (GFR: less than 90 mL/min). Bupropion and its metabolites are cleared renally and may accumulate in such patients to a greater extent than usual. Monitor closely for adverse reactions that could indicate high bupropion or metabolite exposures
