                     Storage

 Store famotidine for oral suspension, USP dry powder at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

                  Dispense in a USP tight, light-resistant container.
