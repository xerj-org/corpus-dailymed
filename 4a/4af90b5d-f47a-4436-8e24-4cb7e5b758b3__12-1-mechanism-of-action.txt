DailyMed 4af90b5d-f47a-4436-8e24-4cb7e5b758b3 | FAMOTIDINE | 12.1 Mechanism of Action

Famotidine is a competitive inhibitor of H2-receptors. The primary clinically important pharmacologic activity of famotidine is inhibition of gastric secretion. Both the acid concentration and volume of gastric secretion are suppressed by famotidine, while changes in pepsin secretion are proportional to volume output.
