The recommended dosage and duration of famotidine for oral suspension in pediatric patients with normal renal function is shown in Table 2.

                           After preparation, the concentration of famotidine for oral suspension is 8 mg/mL [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)].

                           Table 2: Recommended Dosage and Duration of Famotidine for Oral Suspension in Pediatric Patients with Normal Renal Function

                                    b Use conservative measures (e.g., thickened feedings) concurrently [see Use in Specific Populations (8.4)].

                                    c After 4 weeks of treatment re-evaluate the patient. Consider an additional 4 weeks of treatment if treatment benefit outweighs potential risks.
