Vitamin Supplementation

                           Initiate folic acid 400 mcg to 1,000 mcg orally once daily, beginning 7 days before the first dose of pemetrexed for injection and continuing until 21 days after the last dose of pemetrexed for injection [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

                           Administer vitamin B12, 1 mg intramuscularly, 1 week prior to the first dose of pemetrexed for injection and every 3 cycles thereafter. Subsequent vitamin B12 injections may be given the same day as treatment with pemetrexed for injection [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)]. Do not substitute oral vitamin B

                           Corticosteroids
