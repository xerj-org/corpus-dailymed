The effectiveness of ZEGERID has been established, in part, based on studies of an oral delayed-release omeprazole product for the treatment of active duodenal ulcer, active benign gastric ulcer, symptomatic GERD, EE due to acid-mediated GERD, and maintenance of healing of EE due to acid-mediated GERD [see Clinical Studies (14.1, 14.2,
                     14.3
                     , 14.4, 14.5)].

                  ZEGERID for oral suspension was studied for the reduction of risk of upper GI bleeding in critically ill adult patients [see Clinical Studies (14.6)].
