DailyMed cd6868b9-5824-442b-8d65-4db29ecb70a4 | ZEGERID | 8.7 Asian Population

In studies of healthy subjects, Asians had approximately a four-fold higher exposure than Caucasians. Avoid use of ZEGERID in Asian patients for maintenance of healing of erosive esophagitis [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.5)].
