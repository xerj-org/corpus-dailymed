DailyMed 77b82fac-3f6e-44c9-8dcc-362f01ad7a4b | Amoxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium | 5.7 Potential for Microbial Overgrowth

The possibility of superinfections with mycotic or bacterial pathogens should be considered during therapy. If superinfections occur (commonly involving

  Pseudomonasspp. or

  Candidaspp.), discontinue amoxicillin and clavulanate potassium and institute appropriate therapy.
