Amoxicillin and clavulanate potassium tablets are indicated for the treatment of urinary tract infections due to confirmed or suspected beta-lactamase-producing

  E. coli,

  Klebsiellaspecies, and

  Enterobacterspecies as follows

  [see

   Use in Specific Populations (8.4),

   Clinical Studies (14.2)]:

                              Amoxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium Tablets:adults and pediatric patients weighing greater than or equal to 40 kg
