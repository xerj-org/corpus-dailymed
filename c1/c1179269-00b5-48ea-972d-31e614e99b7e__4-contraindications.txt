DailyMed c1179269-00b5-48ea-972d-31e614e99b7e | Adzenys XR-ODT | 4. CONTRAINDICATIONS

ADZENYS XR-ODT is contraindicated:

                     In patients known to be hypersensitive to amphetamine, or other components of ADZENYS XR-ODT. Hypersensitivity reactions such as angioedema and anaphylactic reactions have been reported in patients treated with other amphetamine products [see

                           Adverse Reactions (6.2)
                        ].

                     Patients taking monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs), or within 14 days of stopping MAOIs (including MAOIs such a linezolid or intravenous methylene blue), because of an increased risk of hypertensive crisis

   [see

    Warnings and Precautions (5.7)
                        ,

                           Drug Interactions 7.1].
