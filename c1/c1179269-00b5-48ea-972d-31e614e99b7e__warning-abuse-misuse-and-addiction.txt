DailyMed c1179269-00b5-48ea-972d-31e614e99b7e | Adzenys XR-ODT | WARNING: ABUSE, MISUSE AND ADDICTION

ADZENYS XR-ODT has a high potential for abuse and misuse, which can lead to the development of a substance use disorder, including addiction. Misuse and abuse of CNS stimulants, including ADZENYS XR-ODT, can result in overdose and death
   [see Overdosage (10)], and this risk is increased with higher doses or unapproved methods of administration, such as snorting or injection.

Before prescribing ADZENYS XR-ODT, assess each patient’s risk for abuse, misuse, and addiction. Educate patients and their families about these risks, proper storage of the drug, and proper disposal of any unused drug. Throughout ADZENYS XR-ODT treatment, reassess each patient’s risk of abuse, misuse, and addiction and frequently monitor for signs and symptoms of abuse, misuse, and addiction
   [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1), Drug Abuse and Dependence (9.2)].
