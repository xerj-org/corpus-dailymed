DailyMed c1179269-00b5-48ea-972d-31e614e99b7e | Adzenys XR-ODT | 2.3 Dosage Recommendations in Pediatric Patients

The recommended starting dosage is 6.3 mg once daily in the morning. Increase in increments of 3.1 mg or 6.3 mg at weekly intervals. The maximum recommended dose is 18.8 mg daily for patients 6 to 12 years, and 12.5 mg daily for patients 13 to 17 years [see

                              Use in Specific Populations (8.3),
   Clinical Studies (14)
                           ].
