DailyMed c19664aa-a797-4192-b5f3-e906a7cac048 | Benztropine Mesylate | INDICATIONS AND USAGE

For use as an adjunct in the therapy of all forms of parkinsonism.

                  Useful also in the control of extrapyramidal disorders (except tardive dyskinesia – see
                        PRECAUTIONS
                     ) due to neuroleptic drugs (e.g., phenothiazines).
