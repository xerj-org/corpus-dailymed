DailyMed 29743168-6dd7-4ee0-bc02-10dbcf2b7896 | Foscarnet Sodium | Geriatric Use

No studies of the efficacy or safety of foscarnet sodium injection in persons 65 years of age or older have been conducted. However, foscarnet sodium injection has been used in patients age 65 years of age and older. The pattern of adverse events seen in these patients is consistent across all age groups. This drug is known to be substantially excreted by the kidney, and the risk of toxic reactions to this drug may be greater in patients with impaired renal function. Because elderly patients are more likely to have decreased renal function, care should be taken in dose selection, and renal function should be monitored. (See DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION).
