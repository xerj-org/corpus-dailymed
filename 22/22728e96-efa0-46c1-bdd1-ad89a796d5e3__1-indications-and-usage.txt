DailyMed 22728e96-efa0-46c1-bdd1-ad89a796d5e3 | ADZYNMA | 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

ADZYNMA is indicated for prophylactic or on demand enzyme replacement therapy (ERT) in adult and pediatric patients with congenital thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (cTTP) [see Use in Specific Populations (8.4), Clinical Studies (14)].
