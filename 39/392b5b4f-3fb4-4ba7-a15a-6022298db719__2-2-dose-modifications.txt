DailyMed 392b5b4f-3fb4-4ba7-a15a-6022298db719 | EXEMESTANE | 2.2 Dose Modifications

Concomitant use of strong CYP 3A4 inducers decreases exemestane exposure. For patients receiving exemestane with a strong CYP 3A4 inducer such as rifampicin or phenytoin, the recommended dose of exemestane is 50 mg once daily after a meal
  [see Drug Interactions (
   7) and Clinical Pharmacology (
   12.3)].
