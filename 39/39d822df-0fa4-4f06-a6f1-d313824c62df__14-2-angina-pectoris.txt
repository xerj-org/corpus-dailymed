DailyMed 39d822df-0fa4-4f06-a6f1-d313824c62df | Metoprolol Tartrate | 14.2 Angina Pectoris

In controlled clinical trials, metoprolol tartrate tablets, administered two or four times daily, have been shown to be an effective antianginal agent, reducing the number of angina attacks and increasing exercise tolerance. The dosage used in these studies ranged from 100 mg to 400 mg daily. A controlled, comparative, clinical trial showed that metoprolol tartrate tablets were indistinguishable from propranolol in the treatment of angina pectoris.
