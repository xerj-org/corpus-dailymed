DailyMed 394a49a8-81bb-43ba-8d6a-fa6fa5030587 | Ramipril | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Ramipril capsules are contraindicated in patients who are hypersensitive to this product or any other ACE inhibitor (e.g., a patient who has experienced angioedema during therapy with any other ACE inhibitor).

  Ramipril capsules are contraindicated in combination with a neprilysin inhibitor (e.g., sacubitril). Do not administer ramipril capsules within 36 hours of switching to or from sacubitril/valsartan, a neprilysin inhibitor

  [see

   Warnings and Precautions (5.1)]

  .

  Do not co-administer ramipril capsules with aliskiren:

  • in patients with diabetes
