DailyMed 392b5b4f-3fb4-4ba7-a15a-6022298db719 | EXEMESTANE | 8.6 Hepatic Impairment

The AUC of exemestane was increased in subjects with moderate or severe hepatic impairment (Childs-Pugh B or C)

  [see Clinical Pharmacology (

   12.3)]

  . However, based on experience with exemestane at repeated doses up to 200 mg daily that demonstrated a moderate increase in non life-threatening adverse reactions, dosage adjustment does not appear to be necessary.
