Exemestane tablets are white to off-white, round compound cup tablets with “25” on one side and plain on the reverse.

Exemestane tablets are packaged in HDPE bottles with a child-resistant screw cap, supplied in packs of 30 tablets.

NDC 70301-2017-1         HDPE bottle of 30 tablets

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
