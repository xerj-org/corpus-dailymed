DailyMed 39d822df-0fa4-4f06-a6f1-d313824c62df | Metoprolol Tartrate | 14.1 Hypertension

In controlled clinical studies, metoprolol tartrate tablets have been shown to be an effective antihypertensive agent when used alone or as concomitant therapy with thiazide-type diuretics, at dosages of 100 mg to 450 mg daily. In controlled, comparative, clinical studies, metoprolol tartrate tablets have been shown to be as effective an antihypertensive agent as propranolol, methyldopa, and thiazide-type diuretics, to be equally effective in supine and standing positions.
