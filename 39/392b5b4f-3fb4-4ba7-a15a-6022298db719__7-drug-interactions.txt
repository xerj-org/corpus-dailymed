Drugs That Induce CYP 3A4

  Co-medications that induce CYP 3A4 (e.g., rifampicin, phenytoin, carbamazepine, phenobarbital, or St. John’s wort) may

  significantly decrease exposure to exemestane. Dose modification is recommended for patients who are also receiving a

  strong CYP 3A4 inducer 

  [see Dosage and Administration (

   2.2) and Clinical Pharmacology (

   12.3)]
