DailyMed 39d822df-0fa4-4f06-a6f1-d313824c62df | Metoprolol Tartrate | 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Advise patients to take metoprolol tartrate tablets regularly and continuously, as directed, preferably with or immediately following meals. If a dose is missed, the patient should take only the next scheduled dose (without doubling it). Patients should not interrupt or discontinue metoprolol tartrate tablets without consulting the physician.

                  Advise patients (1) to avoid operating automobiles and machinery or engaging in other tasks requiring alertness until the patient’s response to therapy with metoprolol tartrate tablets has been determined; (2) to contact the physician if any difficulty in breathing occurs; (3) to inform the physician or dentist before any type of surgery that he or she is taking metoprolol tartrate tablets.

                  Inform patients or caregivers that there is a risk of hypoglycemia when metoprolol tartrate tablets is given to patients who are fasting or who are vomiting. Monitor for symptoms of hypoglycemia.
