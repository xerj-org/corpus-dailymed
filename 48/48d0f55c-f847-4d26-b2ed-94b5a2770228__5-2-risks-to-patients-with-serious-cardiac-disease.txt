DailyMed 48d0f55c-f847-4d26-b2ed-94b5a2770228 | Amphetamine Extended-Release | 5.2 Risks to Patients with Serious Cardiac Disease

Sudden death has been reported in patients with structural cardiac abnormalities or other serious cardiac disease who were treated with CNS stimulants at the recommended ADHD dosage.

                           Avoid amphetamine extended-release orally disintegrating tablets use in patients with known structural cardiac abnormalities, cardiomyopathy, serious cardiac arrhythmia, coronary artery disease, or other serious cardiac disease.
