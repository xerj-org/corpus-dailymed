DailyMed 483bd97f-c4d0-4e23-aaa8-6334f4471e0c | PAXIL CR | 2.5 Recommended Dosage in Geriatric Patients, Patients with Severe Renal Impairment, and Patients with Severe Hepatic Impairment

For the treatment of MDD, PD, SAD, or PMDD in geriatric patients (those 65 years of age or older), patients with severe renal impairment, and patients with severe hepatic impairment  [see Use in Specific Populations (8.5, 8.6, 8.7)]:

                           The recommended starting dosage of PAXIL CR is 12.5 mg/day. If these patients have an adequate response, may increase the PAXIL CR dosage by 12.5 mg/day at weekly intervals (e.g., after 1 wee, may increase the dosage from 12.5 mg/day to 25 mg/day) up to the maximum recommended dosage.

                            The maximum recommended dosage is 50 mg/day for MDD or PD, 37.5 mg/day for SAD; and 25 mg/day for PMDD.  

                        In these patients with PMDD, may administer PAXIL CR intermittently  [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)].
