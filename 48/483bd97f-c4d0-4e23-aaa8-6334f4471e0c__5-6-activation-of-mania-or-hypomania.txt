DailyMed 483bd97f-c4d0-4e23-aaa8-6334f4471e0c | PAXIL CR | 5.6 Activation of Mania or Hypomania

In patients with bipolar disorder, treating a depressive episode with PAXIL CR or another antidepressant may precipitate a mixed/manic episode.  During controlled clinical trials of immediate‑release paroxetine tablets for unipolar depression, hypomania or mania occurred in approximately 1% of immediate-release paroxetine tablets‑treated patients compared to 1.1% of active‑control-treated patients and 0.3% of placebo‑treated patients.

                        Prior to initiating treatment with PAXIL CR, screen patients for any personal or family history of bipolar disorder, mania, or hypomania.
