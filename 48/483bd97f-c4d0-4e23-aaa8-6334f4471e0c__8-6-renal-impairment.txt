DailyMed 483bd97f-c4d0-4e23-aaa8-6334f4471e0c | PAXIL CR | 8.6 Renal Impairment

Compared to those with normal renal function, paroxetine plasma concentrations in patients with renal impairment (RI) were increased, which may increase the risk of paroxetine-associated adverse reactions. Paroxetine plasma concentrations were higher in patients with greater degrees of RI.  

                        The recommended starting and maximum dosage of PAXIL CR is lower in patients with severe RI compared to those with normal renal function [see Dosage and Administration (2.5) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]. The recommended PAXIL CR dosage in patients with mild RI or moderate RI is the same as in those with normal renal function.
