The following overdose complications have been reported with paroxetine overdose:

                     Seizures, which may be delayed, and altered mental status including coma.

                     Cardiovascular toxicity, which may be delayed, including QRS and QTcinterval prolongation. Hypertension most commonly seen, but rarely can seehypotension alone or with concomitant products including alcohol.

                     Serotonin syndrome (patients with a multiple drug overdosage with other proserotonergic drugs may have a higher risk).

                  If an overdose occurs, consider contacting a Poison Help Line (1-800-222-1222) or a medical toxicologist for additional overdosage management recommendations.
