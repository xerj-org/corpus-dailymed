DailyMed 48d0f55c-f847-4d26-b2ed-94b5a2770228 | Amphetamine Extended-Release | 5.3 Increased Blood Pressure and Heart Rate

CNS stimulants cause an increase in blood pressure (mean increase about 2 to 4 mm Hg) and heart rate (mean increase about 3 to 6 bpm). Some patients may have larger increases.

                           Monitor all amphetamine extended-release orally disintegrating tablets-treated patients for potential tachycardia and hypertension.
