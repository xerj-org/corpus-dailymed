DailyMed fc726765-5d5a-4d6e-b037-b847bda9fb7c | Kerendia | 8.5 Geriatric Use

Of the 6510 patients who received Kerendia in the FIDELIO-DKD and FIGARO-DKD studies, 55% were 65 years and older, and 14% were 75 years and older. Of the 2993 patients who received Kerendia in the FINEARTS-HF study, 79% were 65 years and older, and 43% were 75 years and older. Of the 119 patients who received Kerendia in the FINE-ONE study, 22% were 65 years and older, and 3% were 75 years and older. No overall differences in safety or efficacy were observed between these patients and younger patients. No dose adjustment is required.
