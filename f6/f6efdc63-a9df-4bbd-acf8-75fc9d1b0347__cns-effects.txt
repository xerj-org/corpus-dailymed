DailyMed f6efdc63-a9df-4bbd-acf8-75fc9d1b0347 | METOCLOPRAMIDE | CNS Effects

Restlessness, drowsiness, fatigue, and lassitude may occur in patients receiving the recommended prescribed dosage of metoclopramide injection. Insomnia, headache, confusion, dizziness, or mental depression with suicidal ideation also may occur (see
                              WARNINGS
                           ). In cancer chemotherapy patients being treated with 1 mg/kg to 2 mg/kg per dose, incidence of drowsiness is about 70%. There are isolated reports of convulsive seizures without clear-cut relationship to metoclopramide. Rarely, hallucinations have been reported.
