FAVLYXA injection contains fluorouracil, a nucleoside metabolic inhibitor. Fluorouracil, a fluorinated pyrimidine, has a chemical name of 5-fluoro-2,4 (1H,3H)-pyrimidinedione. Its structural formula is:

                     4

                     2

                     2

                  Molecular weight: 130.08 g/mole

                  FAVLYXA injection is a sterile, colorless to light yellow clear solution available in 250 mg/10 mL single-dose vials and 2,500 mg/100 mL multiple-dose vials for intravenous bolus or infusion administration. Each mL contains 25 mg fluorouracil in water for injection. Sodium hydroxide is added to adjust the pH to 8.6 to 9.4.
