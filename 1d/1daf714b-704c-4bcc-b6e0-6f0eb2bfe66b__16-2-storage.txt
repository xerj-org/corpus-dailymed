DailyMed 1daf714b-704c-4bcc-b6e0-6f0eb2bfe66b | FAVLYXA | 16.2 Storage

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted from 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F). [see USP Controlled Room Temperature]. Protect from light. Retain in carton until time of use. Fluorouracil is a hazardous drug. Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures [see References (15)].
