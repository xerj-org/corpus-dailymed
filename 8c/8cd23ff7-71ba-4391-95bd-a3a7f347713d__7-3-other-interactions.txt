DailyMed 8cd23ff7-71ba-4391-95bd-a3a7f347713d | Tolterodine Tartrate | 7.3 Other Interactions

No clinically relevant interactions have been observed when tolterodine was co-administered with warfarin, with a combined oral contraceptive drug containing ethinyl estradiol and levonorgestrel, or with diuretics
  [see

                                 CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY (12.3)
                              ].
