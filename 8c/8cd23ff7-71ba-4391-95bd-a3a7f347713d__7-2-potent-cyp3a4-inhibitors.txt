DailyMed 8cd23ff7-71ba-4391-95bd-a3a7f347713d | Tolterodine Tartrate | 7.2 Potent CYP3A4 Inhibitors

Ketoconazole (200 mg daily), a potent CYP3A4 inhibitor, increased the mean C
  max and AUC of tolterodine by 2- and 2.5-fold, respectively, in CYP2D6 poor metabolizers.

                        For patients receiving ketoconazole or other potent CYP3A4 inhibitors such as itraconazole, clarithromycin, or ritonavir, the recommended dose of tolterodine tartrate extended-release capsules is 2 mg once daily
  [see

                                 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION (2.2)
                               and

                                 CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY (12.3)
                              ].
