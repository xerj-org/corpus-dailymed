Divalproex sodium extended-release tablets USP, 250 mg are available as pink colored, oval shaped, biconvex film coated tablets imprinted with "U 380" on one side and plain on the other. Each film-coated extended-release tablet contains divalproex sodium, USP equivalent to 250 mg of valproic acid.

                  Divalproex sodium extended-release tablets USP, 500 mg are available as yellow colored, oval shaped, biconvex film coated tablets imprinted with "U 381" on one side and plain on the other. Each film-coated extended-release tablet contains divalproex sodium, USP equivalent to 500 mg of valproic acid.
