DailyMed 2de86096-4ec7-47df-94db-9c7f6e5df424 | divalproex sodium | 5.14 Angioedema

Angioedema has been reported in patients treated with valproate in the postmarketing setting.

                        Valproate should be discontinued immediately if symptoms of angioedema, such as facial, perioral, or upper airway swelling occur. Valproate should be discontinued permanently if a clear alternative etiology for the reaction cannot be established [see Contraindications (4)].
