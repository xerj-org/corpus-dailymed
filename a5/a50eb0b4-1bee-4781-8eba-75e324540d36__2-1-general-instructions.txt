DailyMed a50eb0b4-1bee-4781-8eba-75e324540d36 | Ticagrelor | 2.1 General Instructions

Advise patients who miss a dose of ticagrelor tablets to take their next dose at its scheduled time.

                        For patients who are unable to swallow tablets whole, ticagrelor tablets can be crushed, mixed with water, and drunk. The mixture can also be administered via a nasogastric tube (CH
  8or greater)
  [see
   Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

                        Do not administer ticagrelor tablets with another oral P2Y
  12 platelet inhibitor.

                        Avoid aspirin at doses higher than recommended
  [see
   Clinical Studies (14.1)].
