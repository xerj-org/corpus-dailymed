DailyMed a50eb0b4-1bee-4781-8eba-75e324540d36 | Ticagrelor | 1.2 Coronary Artery Disease but No Prior Stroke or Myocardial Infarction

Ticagrelor tablets are indicated to reduce the risk of a first MI or stroke in patients with coronary artery disease (CAD) at high risk for such events

  [see

   Clinical Studies (14.2)].

  While use is not limited to this setting, the efficacy of ticagrelor tablets was established in a population with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM).
