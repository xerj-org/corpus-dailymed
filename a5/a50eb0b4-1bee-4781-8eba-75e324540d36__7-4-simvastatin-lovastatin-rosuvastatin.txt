DailyMed a50eb0b4-1bee-4781-8eba-75e324540d36 | Ticagrelor | 7.4 Simvastatin, Lovastatin, Rosuvastatin

Ticagrelor increases serum concentrations of simvastatin and lovastatin because these drugs are metabolized by CYP3A4. Avoid simvastatin and lovastatin doses greater than 40 mg

  [see

   Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

                        Ticagrelor increases serum concentration of rosuvastatin because rosuvastatin is a BCRP substrate

  [see

   Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]
