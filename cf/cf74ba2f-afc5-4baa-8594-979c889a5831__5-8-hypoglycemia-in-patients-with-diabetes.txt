DailyMed cf74ba2f-afc5-4baa-8594-979c889a5831 | XELJANZ; XELJANZXR | 5.8 Hypoglycemia in Patients with Diabetes

Hypoglycemia, including severe hypoglycemia, has been reported following initiation of XELJANZ/XELJANZ XR and other JAK inhibitors in patients with diabetes. During treatment with XELJANZ/XELJANZ XR, consider increased monitoring of blood glucose as clinically indicated in patients with diabetes. Advise patients with diabetes to notify their healthcare provider if they develop signs or symptoms of hypoglycemia.
