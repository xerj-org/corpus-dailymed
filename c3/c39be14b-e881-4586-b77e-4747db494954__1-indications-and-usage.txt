DailyMed c39be14b-e881-4586-b77e-4747db494954 | Pantoprazole Sodium | 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Pantoprazole sodium for injection is indicated for treatment of:

                         gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) and a history of erosive esophagitis (EE) for up to 10 days in adults.

                         pathological hypersecretory conditions including Zollinger-Ellison (ZE) Syndrome in adults.
