DailyMed c35f72dc-d90e-43f8-9899-f7d1da89460d | Maraviroc | 6.2 Postmarketing Experience

The following adverse events have been identified during post-approval use of maraviroc tablets. Because these reactions are reported voluntarily from a population of uncertain size, it is not always possible to reliably estimate their frequency or establish a causal relationship to drug exposure.

                           Skin and Subcutaneous Tissue Disorders

                        Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS), drug rash with eosinophilia and systemic symptoms (DRESS), toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN).
