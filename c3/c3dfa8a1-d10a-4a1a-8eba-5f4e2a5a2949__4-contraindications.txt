DailyMed c3dfa8a1-d10a-4a1a-8eba-5f4e2a5a2949 | Metformin Hydrochloride; METFORMIN HYDROCHLORIDE | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Metformin hydrochloride tablets and Metformin hydrochloride extended-release tablets are contraindicated in patients with:

                     Severe renal impairment (eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73 m2) [see
                           Warnings and Precautions (5.1)
                        ].

                     Hypersensitivity to metformin.

                     Acute or chronic metabolic acidosis, including diabetic ketoacidosis, with or without coma.
