DailyMed c604e181-e336-4194-bf96-f65dd164d461 | Selexipag; Selexipag TITRATION PACK | 7.2 CYP2C8 Inducers

Concomitant administration with an inducer of CYP2C8 and UGT 1A3 and 2B7 enzymes (rifampin) halved exposure to the active metabolite. Increase selexipag up to twice the dose when co-administered with rifampin. Reduce selexipag when rifampin is stopped [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
