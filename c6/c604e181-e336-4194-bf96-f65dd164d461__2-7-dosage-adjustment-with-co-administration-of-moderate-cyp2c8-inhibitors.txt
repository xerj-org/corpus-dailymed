DailyMed c604e181-e336-4194-bf96-f65dd164d461 | Selexipag; Selexipag TITRATION PACK | 2.7 Dosage Adjustment with Co-administration of Moderate CYP2C8 Inhibitors

When co-administered with moderate CYP2C8 inhibitors (e.g., clopidogrel, deferasirox and teriflunomide) in adult patients, reduce the dosing of selexipag to once daily [see Drug Interactions (7.1) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

                           Pediatric use information is approved for Actelion Pharmaceuticals US, Inc.'s Uptravi® (Selexipag) Tablets. However, due to Actelion Pharmaceuticals US, Inc.'s marketing exclusivity rights, this drug product is not labeled with that information.
