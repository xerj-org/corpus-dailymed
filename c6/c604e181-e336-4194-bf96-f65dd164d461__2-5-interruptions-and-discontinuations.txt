DailyMed c604e181-e336-4194-bf96-f65dd164d461 | Selexipag; Selexipag TITRATION PACK | 2.5 Interruptions and Discontinuations

If a dose of selexipag tablets are missed, patients should take a missed dose as soon as possible unless the next dose is within the next 6 hours.

                        If treatment is missed for 3 days or more, restart selexipag tablets at a lower dose and then re-titrate.
