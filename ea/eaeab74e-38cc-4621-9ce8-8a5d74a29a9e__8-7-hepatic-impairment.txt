DailyMed eaeab74e-38cc-4621-9ce8-8a5d74a29a9e | Sildenafil | 8.7 Hepatic Impairment

In volunteers with hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Class A and B), sildenafil clearance was reduced, resulting in higher plasma exposure of sildenafil (47% for C

  maxand 85% for AUC). The pharmacokinetics of sildenafil in patients with severely impaired hepatic function (Child-Pugh Class C) have not been studied. A starting dose of 25 mg should be considered in patients with any degree of hepatic impairment

  [see Dosage and Administration 

   (2.5)and Clinical Pharmacology

   (12.3)].
