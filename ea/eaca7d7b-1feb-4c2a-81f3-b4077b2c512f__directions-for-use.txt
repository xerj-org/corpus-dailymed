DailyMed eaca7d7b-1feb-4c2a-81f3-b4077b2c512f | OZIPCO DEWORMER FENBENDAZOLE | Directions for use

Shake well before each use.

                  Administer orally with the enclosed dosing syringe, directly into the mouth or mixed with a small amount of food.

                  Give 50 mg fenbendazole per kg of body weight once daily for 3 consecutive days.

                  This is equivalent to 0.5 mL of product per kg of body weight per day.

                  Weigh the cat before dosing and give the full 3-day course, even if signs improve.

                  Repeat treatment as advised by your veterinarian.
