DailyMed bc99e6af-7050-4653-a681-5b252305a970 | Diclofenac SodiumDelayed Release | Fetal Toxicity

Inform pregnant women to avoid use of diclofenac sodium and other NSAIDs, starting at 30 weeks gestation because of the risk of the premature closure of the fetal ductus arteriosus. If treatment with diclofenac sodium is needed for a pregnant woman between about 20 to 30 gestation, advise her that she may need to be monitored for oligohydramnios, if treatment continues for longer than 48 hours [see     WARNINGS; Fetal Toxicity;     PRECAUTIONS; Pregnancy]
