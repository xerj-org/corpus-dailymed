BIJUVA is contraindicated in women with any of the following conditions:

                        Abnormal genital bleeding of unknown etiology [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

                        Breast cancer or a history of breast cancer [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

                        Estrogen-dependent neoplasia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

                        Active deep vein thrombosis (DVT), pulmonary embolisum (PE), or history of these conditions [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

                        Active arterial thromboembolic disease (for example, stroke, myocardial infarction (MI)), or a history of these conditions [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

                        Known anaphylactic reaction, angioedema, or hypersensitivity to BIJUVA.

                        Hepatic impairment or disease [see Warnings and Precautions (5.8)]

                        Known thrombophilic disorders, such as protein C, protein S, or antithrombin deficiency
