DailyMed be4bc0de-0fdc-4d46-8d25-be43c79e6a06 | Verzenio | 8.3 Females and Males of Reproductive Potential

Refer to the Prescribing Information of the agents administered in combination with VERZENIO for contraception and infertility information. When used in combination, advise patients to use effective contraception during treatment and for the longest post-treatment duration recommended in the prescribing information of the individual products.

                        Based on animal studies, VERZENIO can cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman [see Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
