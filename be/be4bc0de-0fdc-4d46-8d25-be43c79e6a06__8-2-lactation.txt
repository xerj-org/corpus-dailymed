DailyMed be4bc0de-0fdc-4d46-8d25-be43c79e6a06 | Verzenio | 8.2 Lactation

Refer to the Prescribing Information of the agents administered in combination with VERZENIO for lactation information. When used in combination, advise patients to not breastfeed during treatment and for the longest post-treatment duration recommended in the prescribing information of any of the individual products.
