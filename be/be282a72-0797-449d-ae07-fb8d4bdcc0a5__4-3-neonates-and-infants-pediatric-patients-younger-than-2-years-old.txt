DailyMed be282a72-0797-449d-ae07-fb8d4bdcc0a5 | Brimonidine tartrate and Timolol maleate | 4.3 Neonates and Infants (Pediatric Patients Younger Than 2 Years Old)

Brimonidine tartrate/timolol maleate ophthalmic solution is contraindicated in neonates and infants (pediatric patients younger than 2 years old) [see Use in Specific Populations (8.4)].
