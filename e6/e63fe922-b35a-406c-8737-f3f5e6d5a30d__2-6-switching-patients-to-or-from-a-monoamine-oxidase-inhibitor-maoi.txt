DailyMed e63fe922-b35a-406c-8737-f3f5e6d5a30d | PAROXETINE | 2.6 Switching Patients to or From a Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor (MAOI)

At least 14 days must elapse between discontinuation of a monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI and initiation of paroxetine tablets. In addition, at least 14 days must elapse after stopping paroxetine tablets before starting an MAOI antidepressant 

  [see Contraindications (

   4), Warnings and Precautions (

   5.2)].
