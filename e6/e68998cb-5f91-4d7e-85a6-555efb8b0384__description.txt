Carbamazepine Tablets, carbamazepine USP, is an anticonvulsant and specific analgesic for trigeminal neuralgia, available for oral administration as tablets of 100 mg and 200 mg. Its chemical name is 5H-dibenz[b,f ]azepine-5-carboxamide, and its structural formula is:

                  Carbamazepine USP is a white to off-white powder, practically insoluble in water and soluble in alcohol and in acetone. Its molecular weight is 236.27 g/mol.

                     Inactive Ingredients

                  Tablets: pregelatinized maize starch, FD&C red 40, croscarmellose sodium, colloidal silicon dioxide and magnesium stearate.
