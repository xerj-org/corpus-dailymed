How Supplied

                  ROTARIX is supplied in a prefilled ready-to-use oral dosing applicator with a plunger stopper (NDC 58160-740-02) in a carton of 10 (NDC 58160-740-21). Each dose is 1.5 mL.

                  The tip cap and plunger stopper of the oral dosing applicator are not made with natural rubber latex.

                     Storage

                  Store refrigerated at 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F). Do not freeze. Discard if the vaccine has been frozen. Keep in original package to protect from light.
