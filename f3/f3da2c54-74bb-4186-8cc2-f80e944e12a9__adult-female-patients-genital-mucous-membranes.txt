DailyMed f3da2c54-74bb-4186-8cc2-f80e944e12a9 | Lidocaine and prilocaine | Adult Female Patients-Genital Mucous Membranes

For minor procedures on the female external genitalia, such as removal of condylomata acuminata, as well as for use as pretreatment for anesthetic infiltration, apply a thick layer (5 grams to 10 grams) of lidocaine and prilocaine cream for 5 minutes to 10 minutes.

                        Occlusion is not necessary for absorption, but may be helpful to keep the cream in place. Patients should be lying down during the lidocaine and prilocaine cream application, especially if no occlusion is used. The procedure or the local anesthetic infiltration should be performed immediately after the removal of lidocaine and prilocaine cream.
