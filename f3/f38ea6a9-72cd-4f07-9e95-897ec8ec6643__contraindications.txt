DailyMed f38ea6a9-72cd-4f07-9e95-897ec8ec6643 | Florfinasone Otic | CONTRAINDICATIONS:

Do not use in dogs with known tympanic membrane perforation (see Precautions).

                  Florfinasone™ Otic is contraindicated in dogs with known or suspected hypersensitivity to florfenicol, terbinafine hydrochloride, or mometasone furoate.
