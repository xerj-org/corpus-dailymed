DailyMed f38ea6a9-72cd-4f07-9e95-897ec8ec6643 | Florfinasone Otic | DESCRIPTION:

Florfinasone™ Otic contains 16.6 mg/mL florfenicol, 14.8 mg/mL terbinafine (equivalent to 16.6 mg/mL terbinafine hydrochloride) and 2.2 mg/mL mometasone furoate. Inactive ingredients include purified water, propylene carbonate, propylene glycol, ethyl alcohol, and polyethylene glycol.
