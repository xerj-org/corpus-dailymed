DailyMed 36bca22f-49ea-42ce-926b-035c42eb6038 | Fosfomycin Tromethamine | DOSAGE & ADMINISTRATION

The recommended dosage for women 18 years of age and older for uncomplicated urinary tract infection (acute cystitis) is one sachet of Fosfomycin Tromethamine Granules for Oral Solution. Fosfomycin Tromethamine Granules for Oral Solution may be taken with or without food.

                  Fosfomycin Tromethamine Granules for Oral Solution should not be taken in its dry form. Always mix Fosfomycin Tromethamine Granules for Oral Solution with water before ingesting. (See PREPARATION section).
