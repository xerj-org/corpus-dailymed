Levofloxacin Oral Solution, USP is supplied in multi-use 100 mL (NDC 0527-1948-66), 200 mL (0527-1948-68), and 480 mL (NDC 0527-1948-70) bottles of 25 mg/mL levofloxacin oral solution.

                  Levofloxacin Oral Solution, USP should be stored at 25° C (77° F); excursions permitted to 15° - 30° C (59° to 86° F) [refer to USP controlled room temperature].

                  Levofloxacin Oral Solution, USP is distributed by Lannett Company, Inc., Philadelphia, PA 19136

                  Keep out of reach of children.
