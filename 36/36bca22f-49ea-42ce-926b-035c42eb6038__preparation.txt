DailyMed 36bca22f-49ea-42ce-926b-035c42eb6038 | Fosfomycin Tromethamine | PREPARATION

Fosfomycin Tromethamine Granules for Oral Solution should be taken orally. Pour the entire contents of a single-dose sachet of Fosfomycin Tromethamine into 3 to 4 ounces of water (1/2 cup) and stir to dissolve. Do not use hot water. Fosfomycin Tromethamine Granules for Oral Solution should be taken immediately after dissolving in water.
