DailyMed 36bca22f-49ea-42ce-926b-035c42eb6038 | Fosfomycin Tromethamine | NURSING MOTHERS

It is not known whether fosfomycin tromethanmine is excreted in human milk. Because many drugs are excreted in human milk and because of the potential for serious adverse reactions in nursing infants from fosfomycin tromethamine, a decision should be made whether to discontinue nursing or to not administer the drug, taking into account the importance of the drug to the mother.
