DailyMed 362034e9-2830-19a5-e063-6394a90a8dfb | ANASTROZOLE | 5.1 Ischemic Cardiovascular Events

In women with pre-existing ischemic heart disease, an increased incidence of ischemic cardiovascular events was observed with anastrozole tablets in the ATAC trial (17% of patients on anastrozole tablets and 10% of patients on tamoxifen). Consider risk and benefits of anastrozole tablets therapy in patients with pre-existing ischemic heart disease

  [see

   Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
