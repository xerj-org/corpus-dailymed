DailyMed 362034e9-2830-19a5-e063-6394a90a8dfb | ANASTROZOLE | 2.1 Recommended Dose

The dose of anastrozole tablets are one 1 mg tablet taken once a day. For patients with advanced breast cancer, anastrozole tablets should be continued until tumor progression. Anastrozole tablets can be taken with or without food.

                        For adjuvant treatment of early breast cancer in postmenopausal women, the optimal duration of therapy is unknown. In the ATAC trial, anastrozole tablets were administered for five years

  [see

   Clinical Studies (14.1)]

  .

                        No dosage adjustment is necessary for patients with renal impairment or for elderly patients

  [see

   Use in Specific Populations (8.6)].
