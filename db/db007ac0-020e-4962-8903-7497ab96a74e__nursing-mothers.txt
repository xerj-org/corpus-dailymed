DailyMed db007ac0-020e-4962-8903-7497ab96a74e | Imipramine Hydrochloride | Nursing Mothers

Limited data suggest that imipramine hydrochloride tablets, is likely to be excreted in human breast milk. As a general rule, a woman taking a drug should not nurse since the possibility exists that the drug may be excreted in breast milk and be harmful to the child.
