DailyMed 632bb50c-3bcb-4c85-9056-fc33410550ae | ZEPZELCA | 5.5 Embryo-Fetal Toxicity

Based on animal data and its mechanism of action [see

                              Clinical Pharmacology (12.1)
                              ]

                            ZEPZELCA can cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman. Intravenous administration of a single dose of lurbinectedin (approximately 0.2 times the 3.2 mg/m2 clinical dose) to pregnant animals during the period of organogenesis caused 100% embryo lethality in rats. Advise pregnant women of the potential risk to a fetus. Advise female patients of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during treatment with ZEPZELCA and for 7 months after the last dose. Advise male patients with female partners of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during treatment with ZEPZELCA and for 4 months after the last dose [see Use in Specific Populations (8.1, 8.3)].
