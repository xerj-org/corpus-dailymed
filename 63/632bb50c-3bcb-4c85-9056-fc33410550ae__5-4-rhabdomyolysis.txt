DailyMed 632bb50c-3bcb-4c85-9056-fc33410550ae | ZEPZELCA | 5.4 Rhabdomyolysis

Rhabdomyolysis has been reported in patients treated with ZEPZELCA.

                        Monitor creatine phosphokinase (CPK) prior to initiating ZEPZELCA and periodically during treatment as clinically indicated. Withhold or reduce the dose based on severity [see Dosage and Administration (2.2)].

                           In the IMforte study [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)], among 235 patients who had a creatine phosphokinase laboratory evaluation, increased creatine phosphokinase occurred in 9% who received ZEPZELCA in combination with atezolizumab.
