DailyMed 63e0b7ca-6b2d-42a9-b9d4-4523b8a20425 | Rabeprazole Sodium | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Rabeprazole is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to rabeprazole, substituted benzimidazoles, or to any component of the formulation. Hypersensitivity reactions may include anaphylaxis, anaphylactic shock, angioedema, bronchospasm, acute tubulointerstitial nephritis, and urticaria [ 

   see Warnings and Precautions

    (5.3), 

                        Adverse Reactions (

    6)
                        ].

                     PPIs, including rabeprazole, are contraindicated with rilpivirine-containing products [see Drug Interactions (

   7)].

                     For information about contraindications of antibacterial agents (clarithromycin and amoxicillin) indicated in combination with rabeprazole sodium delayed-release tablets, refer to the

   Contraindicationssection of their package inserts.
