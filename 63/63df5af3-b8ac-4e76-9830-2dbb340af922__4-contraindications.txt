DailyMed 63df5af3-b8ac-4e76-9830-2dbb340af922 | Tivicay PD; Tivicay | 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

TIVICAY and TIVICAY PD are contraindicated in patients:

                        •

with previous hypersensitivity reaction to dolutegravir [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

                        •

receiving dofetilide due to the potential for increased dofetilide plasma concentrations and the risk for serious and/or life-threatening events [see Drug Interactions (7)].
