DailyMed 632bb50c-3bcb-4c85-9056-fc33410550ae | ZEPZELCA | 2.5 Recommended Prophylactic Medications

Consider administering the following pre-infusion medications [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)]:

                              •

                              To reduce the risk of febrile neutropenia, administer granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (G-CSF) [Refer to Prescribing Information].

                              •

                              To reduce the risk of nausea, administer the following pre-infusion medications for antiemetic prophylaxis prior to Cycle 1 and consider administering for subsequent cycles:

                               

                                      ⸰    Corticosteroids (dexamethasone 8 mg or equivalent intravenously)

                               

                                      ⸰    Serotonin antagonists (ondansetron 8 mg or equivalent intravenously)
