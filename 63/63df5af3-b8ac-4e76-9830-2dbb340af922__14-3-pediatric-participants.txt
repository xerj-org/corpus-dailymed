IMPAACT P1093 is a Phase I/II, multicenter, open‑label trial to evaluate the pharmacokinetic parameters, safety, tolerability, and efficacy of TIVICAY or TIVICAY PD in combination treatment regimens in infants, children, and adolescents with HIV‑1 aged at least 4 weeks to 18 years. Participants were stratified by 5 age cohorts: Cohort 1, aged 12 to less than 18 years; Cohort 2A, aged 6 to less than 12 years; Cohort 3, aged 2 to less than 6 years; Cohort 4, aged 6 months to less than 2 years; and Cohort 5, aged 4 weeks to less than 6 months. Seventy-five participants received the recommended dose (determined by weight and age) of TIVICAY or TIVICAY PD [see Dosage and Administration (2.1, 2.3, 2.4)].

                        These 75 participants had a median age of 27 months (range: 1 to 214), were 59% female, and 68% were Black or African American. At baseline, mean plasma HIV-1 RNA was 4.4 log10 copies/mL, median CD4+ cell count was 1,225 cells/mm3 (range: 1 to 8,255), and median CD4+% was 23% (range: 0.3% to 49%). Overall, 33% had baseline plasma HIV-1 RNA greater than 50,000 copies/mL and 12% had a CDC HIV clinical classification of category C. The majority (80%) of participants were treatment-experienced, but all were INSTI-naïve. Most participants had previously used at least 1 NNRTI (44%) or 1 PI (76%).

                        Virologic outcomes from IMPAACT P1093 include participants who received either TIVICAY tablets or TIVICAY PD tablets for oral suspension as per the dosing recommendations for their weight band and who had reached Week 24 (n = 58) or Week 48 (n = 42). At Week 24, 62% of participants achieved HIV-1 RNA less than 50 copies/mL and 86% achieved HIV-1 RNA less than 400 copies/mL (Snapshot algorithm). The median CD4 count (percent) increase from baseline to Week 24 was 105 cells/mm3 (5%). At Week 48, 69% of participants achieved HIV-1 RNA less than 50 copies/mL and 79% achieved HIV-1 RNA less than 400 copies/mL (Snapshot algorithm). The median CD4 count (percent) increase from baseline to Week 48 was 141 cells/mm3 (7%).
