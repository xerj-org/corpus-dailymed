DailyMed 63df5af3-b8ac-4e76-9830-2dbb340af922 | Tivicay PD; Tivicay | 8.6 Hepatic Impairment

No clinically important pharmacokinetic differences between participants with moderate hepatic impairment and matching healthy participants were observed. No dosage adjustment is necessary for patients with mild to moderate hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Score A or B). The effect of severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Score C) on the pharmacokinetics of dolutegravir has not been studied. Therefore, TIVICAY and TIVICAY PD are not recommended for use in patients with severe hepatic impairment [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
