DailyMed d57720ab-9f83-4da9-a57f-0d55e00a605c | ATORVASTATIN CALCIUM | 7.3 Atorvastatin Calcium Effects on Other Drugs

Table 4 presents atorvastatin calcium effect on other drugs and instructions for preventing or managing them.

                           Table 4: Atorvastatin Calcium Effects on Other Drugs

                                    Oral Contraceptives
                                  |

                                    Clinical Impact:
                                  |
                                 Co-administration of atorvastatin calcium and an oral contraceptive increased plasma concentrations of norethindrone and ethinyl estradiol

     [see Clinical Pharmacology (

      12.3)].

                                  |

                                    Intervention:
                                  |
                                 Consider this when selecting an oral contraceptive for patients taking atorvastatin calcium. |

                                    Digoxin
                                  |

                                    Clinical Impact:
                                  |
                                 When multiple doses of atorvastatin calcium and digoxin were co-administered, steady state plasma digoxin concentrations increased

     [see Clinical Pharmacology (

      12.3)]

     .

     |

                                    Intervention:
                                  |
                                 Monitor patients taking digoxin appropriately. |
