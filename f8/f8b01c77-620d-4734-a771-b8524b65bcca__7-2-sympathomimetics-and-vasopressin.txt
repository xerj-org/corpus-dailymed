DailyMed f8b01c77-620d-4734-a771-b8524b65bcca | KETAMINE HYDROCHLORIDE | 7.2 Sympathomimetics and Vasopressin

Sympathomimetics and vasopressin may enhance the sympathomimetic effects of ketamine. Closely monitor vital signs when ketamine hydrochloride and sympathomimetics or vasopressin are co-administered and consider dose adjustment individualized to the patient’s clinical situation.
