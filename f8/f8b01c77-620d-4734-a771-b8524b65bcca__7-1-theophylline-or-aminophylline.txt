DailyMed f8b01c77-620d-4734-a771-b8524b65bcca | KETAMINE HYDROCHLORIDE | 7.1 Theophylline or Aminophylline

Concomitant administration of ketamine hydrochloride and theophylline or aminophylline may lower the seizure threshold. Consider using an alternative to ketamine hydrochloride in patients receiving theophylline or aminophylline.
