DailyMed 6faf465b-c3a3-4ae7-9e16-1ba758f6962a | Pantoprazole Sodium | 8.5 Geriatric Use

In short-term U.S. clinical trials, EE healing rates in the 107 elderly patients (≥ 65 years old) treated with pantoprazole sodium delayed-release tablets were similar to those found in patients under the age of 65. The incidence rates of adverse reactions and laboratory abnormalities in patients aged 65 years and older were similar to those associated with patients younger than 65 years of age.
