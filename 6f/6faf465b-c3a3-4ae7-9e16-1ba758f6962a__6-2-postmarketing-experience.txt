DailyMed 6faf465b-c3a3-4ae7-9e16-1ba758f6962a | Pantoprazole Sodium | 6.2 Postmarketing Experience

The following adverse reactions have been identified during postapproval use of pantoprazole sodium delayed-release tablets. Because these reactions are reported voluntarily from a population of uncertain size, it is not always possible to reliably estimate their frequency or establish a causal relationship to drug exposure.

                        These adverse reactions are listed below by body system:

                              Gastrointestinal Disorders: 
                           fundic gland polyps

                              General Disorders and Administration Conditions:
                            asthenia, fatigue, malaise

                              Hematologic:
                            pancytopenia, agranulocytosis

                              Hepatobiliary Disorders:
                            hepatocellular damage leading to jaundice and hepatic failure

                              Immune System Disorders:
                            anaphylaxis (including anaphylactic shock), systemic lupus erythematosus

                              Infections and Infestations:
                            Clostridium difficile-associated diarrhea

                              Investigations:
                            weight changes

                              Metabolism and Nutritional Disorders:
                            hypomagnesemia, hypocalcemia, hypokalemia, hyponatremia

                              Musculoskeletal Disorders:
                            rhabdomyolysis, bone fracture

                              Nervous:
                            ageusia, dysgeusia

                              Psychiatric Disorders:
                            hallucination, confusion, insomnia, somnolence

                              Renal and Genitourinary Disorders:
                            acute tubulointerstitial nephritis, erectile dysfunction

                              Skin and Subcutaneous Tissue Disorders:
                            severe dermatologic reactions (some fatal), including erythema multiforme, SJS/TEN, DRESS, AGEP, angioedema (Quincke’s edema) and cutaneous lupus erythematosus
