How Supplied: Pantoprazole Sodium Delayed-Release Tablets, USP are available containing 22.55 mg or 45.10 mg of pantoprazole sodium, USP equivalent to 20 mg or 40 mg of pantoprazole, respectively.

                  The 20 mg tablets are dark yellow, film-coated, oval, unscored tablets imprinted with M P8 in black ink on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side. 

                  The 40 mg tablets are dark yellow, film-coated, oval, unscored tablets imprinted with M P9 in black ink on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side. They are available as follows:

                  NDC 0615-7629-07

blistercards of 7 tablets

                  NDC 0615-7629-14

blistercards of 14 tablets

                  NDC 0615-7629-05

blistercards of 15 tablets

                  NDC 0615-7629-28

blistercards of 28 tablets

                  NDC 0615-7629-39

blistercards of 30 tablets

                  NDC 0615-7629-30

unit-dose boxes of 30 tablets

                     Storage: Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F). [See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

                  Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

                     PHARMACIST: Dispense a Medication Guide with each prescription.
