DailyMed 6fd1dafe-4b51-49c7-bce1-c4295b2bd292 | Nicardipine Hydrochloride | 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

                        Freezing does not adversely affect the product, but exposure to elevated temperatures should be avoided. Avoid excessive heat. Protect from light. Store in overwrap until ready to use.
