                  KYBELLA (deoxycholic acid) injection, 10 mg/mL is a clear colorless, sterile solution for subcutaneous use.  Each 2 mL vial of KYBELLA contains 20 mg of deoxycholic acid, a cytolytic agent, as the active ingredient and the following inactive ingredients: benzyl alcohol (18 mg), dibasic sodium phosphate (2.84 mg), sodium chloride (8.76 mg), sodium hydroxide (2.86 mg) in water for injection, USP.  Hydrochloric acid and additional sodium hydroxide are added as necessary to adjust the formulation to pH 8.3.  Each vial is for single patient use.
