DailyMed fe431ed4-ea6f-4e99-b4bc-ec25ae7b8553 | KYBELLA | 5.4 Risk of Injecting in Proximity to Vulnerable Anatomic Structures

To avoid potential tissue damage, do not inject KYBELLA into or in close proximity (1-1.5 cm) to salivary glands, lymph nodes and muscles.

                        Avoid inadvertent injection directly into an artery or a vein as it can result in vascular injury.
