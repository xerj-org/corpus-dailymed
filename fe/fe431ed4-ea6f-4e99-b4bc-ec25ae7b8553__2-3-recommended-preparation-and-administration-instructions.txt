                           Avoid Injections Near the Area of the Marginal Mandibular Nerve

                        To avoid injury to the marginal mandibular nerve:

                           Inject KYBELLA only within the target submental fat treatment area (see Figures 1 and 3).

                           Do not inject above the inferior border of the mandible.

                           Do not inject within a region defined by a 1-1.5 cm line below the inferior border (from the angle of the mandible to the mentum).

                           Administration Instructions

                        Consider use of ice/cold packs, topical and/or injectable local anesthesia (e.g., lidocaine) to enhance patient comfort.

                        Outline the planned KYBELLA treatment area with a surgical pen and apply a 1 cm injection grid to mark the injection sites (Figure 3).

                            and follow these subcutaneous administration instructions:

                           Using a large bore needle, draw 1 mL of KYBELLA into a sterile 1 mL syringe and expel any air bubbles in the syringe barrel.

                           Have the patient tense the platysma.

                           Pinch the submental fat and, using a 30 gauge (or smaller) 0.5 inch needle, inject 0.2 mL of KYBELLA into the pre-platysmal fat (see Figure 2) next to each of the marked injection sites by advancing the needle perpendicular to the skin. 

                           Do not withdraw the needle from the subcutaneous fat during injection; this could increase the risk of intradermal exposure and very superficial injections (into the dermis) may result in skin ulceration and necrosis [see Warnings
                               and
                              Precautions (

                                 5.3

                              )].

                           Avoid injecting into the post-platysmal fat by injecting KYBELLA into fat tissue at the depth of approximately mid-way into the subcutaneous fat layer (Figure 2).

                           If at any time resistance is met as the needle is inserted, indicating the possibility of contact with fascial or nonfat tissue, withdraw the needle to an appropriate depth before the injection is administered.

                           Avoid injecting into other tissues such as the muscle, salivary glands, lymph nodes; and artery or vein [see Warnings and Precautions (

                                 5.4

                              )]. 

                           Upon needle withdrawal, may apply pressure to each injection site as necessary to minimize bleeding; an adhesive dressing may be applied.

                        After use, discard any remaining solution in the vial. Do not dilute or mix KYBELLA with other compounds.
