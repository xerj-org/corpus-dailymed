                        Inject KYBELLA into subcutaneous fat tissue in the submental area using an area-adjusted dose of 2 mg/cm2  (a single treatment of up to 100 mg (up to a total of 10 mL)) that consists of up to a maximum of 50 subcutaneous injections, (2 mg (0.2 mL)) each, spaced 1 cm apart.

                        Up to 6 treatments may be administered at intervals no less than 1 month apart.
