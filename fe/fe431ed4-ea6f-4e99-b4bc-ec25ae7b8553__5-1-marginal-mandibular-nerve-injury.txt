DailyMed fe431ed4-ea6f-4e99-b4bc-ec25ae7b8553 | KYBELLA | 5. 1 Marginal Mandibular Nerve Injury

Cases of marginal mandibular nerve injury, manifested as an asymmetric smile or facial muscle weakness (paresis), were reported in KYBELLA-treated patients during clinical trials.  All marginal mandibular nerve injuries reported from the trials resolved without additional treatment (range 1-298 days, median 44 days).

                        To avoid the potential for marginal mandibular nerve injury, do not inject KYBELLA into or in close proximity to the marginal mandibular branch of the facial nerve [see Dosage and Administration (
