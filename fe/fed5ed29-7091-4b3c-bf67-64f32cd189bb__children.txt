DailyMed fed5ed29-7091-4b3c-bf67-64f32cd189bb | PROCHLORPERAZINE EDISYLATE | Children

DO NOT USE IN PEDIATRIC SURGERY

                        Children seem more prone to develop extrapyramidal reactions, even on moderate doses. Therefore, use lowest effective dosage. Tell parents not to exceed prescribed dosage, since the possibility of adverse reactions increases as dosage rises.

                        Occasionally the patient may react to the drug with signs of restlessness and excitement; if this occurs, do not administer additional doses. Take particular precaution in administering the drug to children with acute illnesses or dehydration (see under
