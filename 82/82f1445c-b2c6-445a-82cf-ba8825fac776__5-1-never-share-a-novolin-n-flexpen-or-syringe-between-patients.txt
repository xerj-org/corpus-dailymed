DailyMed 82f1445c-b2c6-445a-82cf-ba8825fac776 | NovolinN | 5.1 Never Share a NOVOLIN N FlexPen or Syringe between Patients

NOVOLIN N FlexPen must never be shared between patients, even if the needle is changed. Patients using NOVOLIN N vials must never share needles or syringes with another person. Sharing poses a risk for transmission of blood-borne pathogens.
