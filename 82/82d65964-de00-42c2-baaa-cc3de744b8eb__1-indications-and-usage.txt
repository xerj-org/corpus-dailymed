DailyMed 82d65964-de00-42c2-baaa-cc3de744b8eb | Tamsulosin Hydrochloride | 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Tamsulosin hydrochloride capsules are indicated for the treatment of the signs and symptoms of benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) [see ]. Tamsulosin hydrochloride capsules are not indicated for the treatment of hypertension.
