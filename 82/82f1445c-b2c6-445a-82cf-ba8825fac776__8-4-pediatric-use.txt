DailyMed 82f1445c-b2c6-445a-82cf-ba8825fac776 | NovolinN | 8.4 Pediatric Use

NOVOLIN N is indicated to improve glycemic control in pediatric patients with diabetes mellitus.

                        The dosage of NOVOLIN N must be individualized in pediatric patients based on metabolic needs and frequent monitoring of blood glucose to reduce the risk of hypoglycemia [see Dosage and Administration (2.2) and Warnings and Precautions (5.2, 5.3)].
