DailyMed 82f1445c-b2c6-445a-82cf-ba8825fac776 | NovolinN | 8.5 Geriatric Use

The effect of age on the pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of NOVOLIN N has not been studied.  Elderly patients using insulin, including NOVOLIN N, may be at increased risk of hypoglycemia due to co-morbid disease [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
