The time course of insulin action (i.e., glucose lowering) may vary considerably in different individuals or within the same individual. NOVOLIN N is an intermediate-acting insulin with a variable and dose dependent duration of action of up to 24 hours. When injected subcutaneously, the glucose-lowering effect of NOVOLIN N begins approximately 1 to 2 hours post-dose.
