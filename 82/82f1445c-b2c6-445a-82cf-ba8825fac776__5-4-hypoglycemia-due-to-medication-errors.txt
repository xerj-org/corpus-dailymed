DailyMed 82f1445c-b2c6-445a-82cf-ba8825fac776 | NovolinN | 5.4 Hypoglycemia Due to Medication Errors

Accidental mix-ups between NOVOLIN N and other insulin products have been reported. To avoid medication errors between NOVOLIN N and other insulins, instruct patients to always check the insulin label before each injection.
