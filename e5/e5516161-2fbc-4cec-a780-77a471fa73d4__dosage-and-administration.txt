DailyMed e5516161-2fbc-4cec-a780-77a471fa73d4 | OstiCoxib EQ | Dosage and Administration:

Always provide the Client Information Sheet with the prescription. The recommended dosage of OstiCoxib EQ is one 57 mg tablet administered orally to horses weighing 800 – 1300 lbs, once daily for up to 14 days. For ease of administration, OstiCoxib EQ may be given with food.

 The overall duration of treatment with any firocoxib formulation in horses, including OstiCoxib EQ, firocoxib injection or firocoxib oral paste, should not exceed 14 days. Please see the package insert for firocoxib injection or firocoxib oral paste for appropriate prescribing information for those formulations.
